# SEO Edits — "OpenClaw for X" Targeting

## Strategy
Target "OpenClaw for [industry]" long-tail keywords on every vertical page. Limited competition, high-intent buyers who already know OpenClaw.

## Edits Per Page (ALL 11 verticals)

### 1. Title Tag
- **Before:** `AI Assistant for Real Estate Agents — NYC Claw`
- **After:** `OpenClaw for Real Estate Agents — AI Setup & Configuration | NYC Claw`
- Pattern: `OpenClaw for [Industry] — AI Setup & Configuration | NYC Claw`

### 2. Meta Description
- Must include "OpenClaw" naturally
- Pattern: `We set up OpenClaw for [industry professionals]. [Pain point solved]. Configured for your workflow. Setup starts at $1,200.`

### 3. H2 Section — "What is OpenClaw?"
- Add a NEW section between the capabilities section and the "Do the math" section
- Short 2-3 sentence explainer of what OpenClaw is
- H2: `OpenClaw for [Industry]: Your AI, Configured`
- Body: What OpenClaw is (open-source AI assistant, runs on your devices, privacy-first) + why it matters for this industry
- Include internal link back to homepage

### 4. OG Title
- Match the new title tag pattern

### 5. Schema Markup
- Update Service schema `name` field to include "OpenClaw"
- e.g., `"name": "OpenClaw Setup for Real Estate Agents"`

### DO NOT:
- Change the H1 (keep the emotional hook)
- Change the URL structure
- Add "OpenClaw" to every heading (looks spammy)
- Remove any existing content
- Change Stripe links
- Change pricing
- Mention $50 anywhere
- Change the nav or footer
