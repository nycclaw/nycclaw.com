# Accounting Firms — Research for Landing Page

## Staffing Crisis
- 340,000+ accountants/auditors left the profession since 2020 — a 17% workforce decline (BLS)
- 83% of senior leaders report an accounting talent shortage (2024 Finance & Accounting Talent Market Outlook)
- 36% of CFOs cite talent shortages as a significant business risk (SquareWorks)
- 97% of accounting firms admit they're not using technology efficiently (Verizon study)

## Tax Season Burnout
- 48% of accountants work 51-60 hours/week during busy season; 19% work 61-70 hrs; 12% work 71+ hrs (Distinct Recruitment 2025 survey)
- 82% of tax professionals identify as feeling burned out (Thomson Reuters)
- 99% of accountants experience burnout at some point (AICPA survey)
- CPAs routinely work 60-80 hour weeks during tax season (CPA Practice Advisor / MACPA)

## Time Drains
- CPAs lose 15+ hours weekly to non-billable admin during tax season (Bizora)
- Chasing clients for missing documents is one of the top time drains
- Status tracking across hundreds of returns creates bottleneck
- 30% of management time can be saved with workflow automation (AmelioSuite)
- Automation reduces expense report costs from ~$26.63 to $6.85 (PairSoft/Levvel Research)

## Industry Complexity
- 42% of jurisdictions expect growing tax/accounting complexity (TMF Group 2024)
- Post-CTA (Corporate Transparency Act) — new BOI reporting requirements
- Constant tax law changes require staying updated
- 640 U.S.-listed companies reported material weaknesses tied to talent shortage (WSJ 2024)

## Key Pain Points for Landing Page
1. **Document chasing** — CPAs spend hours emailing clients for missing W-2s, K-1s, 1099s
2. **Tax season overwhelm** — 60-80 hr weeks, 82% burnout rate, compressed deadlines
3. **Client questions interrupting deep work** — "What's my refund status?" breaks concentration
4. **Deadline management** — hundreds of clients, multiple filing deadlines (4/15, 9/15, 10/15)
5. **Staffing shortage** — can't hire enough, remaining staff overworked
6. **Engagement letter / onboarding** — annual re-engagement, collecting signatures, intake forms
