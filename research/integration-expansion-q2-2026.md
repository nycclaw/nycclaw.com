_Created: March 21, 2026_
_Based on: GSC performance data (Feb 21 – Mar 20), GA4 traffic (Mar 20-21), Ahrefs keyword research_

---

## Executive Summary

We have **51 integration pages** live and **16 vertical pages** — 67 of ~103 total pages are generating impressions in Google. The integration directory is working: pages like Jobber (pos 2.7), Clio (3.8), Clay (4.3), Follow Up Boss (4.2), and Xero (5.4) are already on page 1 and clicking.

**The problem:** Many existing pages rank well but get zero clicks (bad titles/meta descriptions), and we're missing entire verticals with proven search demand. This plan addresses both.

**The opportunity:** Nobody owns "AI agent for [tool]" keywords yet. We're first movers. The playbook is proven — now we scale it into new verticals and fix the pages that are already ranking.

### Title/Meta Formula That Works

Looking at our pages that DO get clicks, the pattern is:

**Clicking titles:** Specific, benefit-oriented, include "AI" prominently
- ✅ `Xero + AI Agent: Automate Your Bookkeeping | NYC Claw`
- ❌ `Xero AI Integration | Connect Xero to Your AI Assistant — NYC Claw` (generic)

**Recommended meta formula:**
`[Pain point question]? Set up an AI agent that [specific action] in [Tool]. Professional OpenClaw setup — book a free call.`

## Part 3: How This Fits Into the Current Strategy

### Current State (March 2026)

```
103 pages live
├── 16 vertical pages (/for/*)           → targeting "AI for [industry]"
├── 51 integration pages (/integrations/*) → targeting "[tool] AI integration"
├── 8 claude-cowork pages                → targeting "claude cowork [topic]"
├── 4 core pages (home, pricing, about, integrations index)
├── privacy, terms
└── 2 hub pages
```

**What's working:**
- Integration pages are our best SEO asset — they rank fast (most within 2-4 weeks)
- Vertical pages drive branded searches ("openclaw [industry]")
- The combination of vertical + integration creates topical clusters that Google loves

**What's missing:**
- **Dental, vet, PT/chiro, and trades verticals** have no supporting integration pages
- **High-volume messaging integrations** (Telegram 350/mo, WhatsApp 200/mo) don't exist
- **Core tools** (Stripe, Shopify, Google Workspace) missing — these are table-stakes credibility pages
- **Financial advisor vertical** exists but has no tool pages (Redtail, Wealthbox)
