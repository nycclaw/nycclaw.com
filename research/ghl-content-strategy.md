# GoHighLevel + OpenClaw Content Strategy

## Overview

GoHighLevel (GHL) is the dominant all-in-one CRM/marketing platform for agencies and SMBs — $97-$497/mo, used by tens of thousands of businesses (real estate agents, coaches, agencies, contractors, etc.). It has built-in AI features ("AI Employee"), workflows, webhooks, and a full REST API with 50+ webhook event types.

**The opportunity:** GHL users are actively searching for ways to supercharge their CRM with AI agents. GHL's built-in AI is limited to basic chatbots and workflow triggers. OpenClaw can sit on top of GHL as a true AI operator — reading leads, responding intelligently, updating pipelines, managing follow-ups, and acting across GHL + email + calendar + SMS simultaneously.

**Nobody is targeting "OpenClaw + GoHighLevel" as a keyword pair yet.** This is greenfield SEO territory. We own the narrative.

## Competitive Landscape

**Who's playing in this space:**
- **GHL's own ecosystem** — built-in AI Employee ($97-$497/mo plans), plus their marketplace of workflow templates
- **Zapier / Make.com / n8n** — general automation connectors, no AI reasoning layer
- **Generic AI chatbot vendors** — Drift, Intercom, etc. — widget-only, no CRM operation
- **GHL agencies/consultants** — setup services for GHL itself, but NOT offering AI agent integration

**Our edge:** Nobody is offering "AI agent that operates your GHL for you." The consultants set up GHL workflows. The AI tools are siloed chatbots. OpenClaw + NYC Claw is the only play that says: "We'll give your GHL a brain."
