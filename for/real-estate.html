Leads go cold while you're showing houses

A Zillow inquiry hits your inbox at 2pm. You're mid-showing until 6pm. By the time you respond, that buyer has already talked to three other agents. The average agent takes 1 hour 45 minutes to respond to an inbound lead. After just 5 minutes, your odds of qualifying that lead drop by 80%.

Every deal buries you in 17+ hours of paperwork

Disclosures, compliance docs, signatures, lender follow-ups, title coordination, inspection scheduling. Post-NAR settlement, buyer agent compensation is no longer automatic. You need written agreements before touring homes, sellers aren't always offering commission, and you have to justify your value at every turn. More paperwork, more competition, less margin for wasted time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw respond to my leads?
OpenClaw monitors your email, web forms, and connected lead sources. When a new inquiry comes in, it sends a personalized response within seconds — qualifying the lead on budget, timeline, and area preferences. You get a summary notification so you can call back prepared.
Will this replace my CRM or transaction coordinator?
OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools, not against them. It connects to your email, calendar, and CRM to handle the follow-up and tracking that falls through the cracks. Think of it as the layer that ties everything together — so you don't have to manually copy info between systems.
How long does setup take?
Most real estate setups are completed in a single session (2-3 hours), with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. You'll have a working AI assistant handling leads and tracking deals within the first day.
Is my client data private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Your client information, deal details, and communications never leave your control. It's open-source, so there's full transparency into how it works.
What happens after the NAR settlement — does this help?
