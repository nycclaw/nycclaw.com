Leads go cold while you're in court

A potential client fills out your contact form at 10am. You're in court until 4pm. By the time you respond, they've already called three other firms. 64% of prospective clients receive no follow-up at all. The first firm to respond wins up to 78% of the time. Every hour of delay costs you cases.

Half your day disappears into admin

You bill under 3 hours of an 8-hour day. The rest goes to email triage, scheduling, document chasing, and data entry. At $250/hr, every non-billable hour is $250 gone. That's $65,000/year in lost revenue from just one hour per day. You went to law school to practice law, not manage an inbox.

Missed deadlines risk malpractice claims

Failure to calendar a known deadline is behind 7% of all malpractice claims. Litigation errors including missed deadlines account for one-third of all claims. A single missed statute of limitations can end a case, trigger a malpractice judgment, increase your premiums for years, and damage your reputation permanently.

Clients feel ignored between updates

Lack of communication is the #1 complaint filed with state bar associations. Clients hire you during the most stressful moments of their lives — then hear nothing for weeks. You're not neglecting cases maliciously. You're just too busy. But one "never returned my calls" Google review can cost dozens of future clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my client data secure with OpenClaw?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices — your client information, case details, and communications never leave your control. It doesn't store case data; it routes, reminds, and organizes. It's open-source, so there's full transparency into how it works. Attorney-client privilege is maintained because nothing is sent to external servers.
Does this replace my practice management software (Clio, MyCase, etc.)?
No — OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools, not against them. It connects to your email, calendar, and practice management software to handle the operational layer — monitoring, reminders, follow-up — that PMS tools require manual input for. Think of it as the layer that ties everything together.
Can OpenClaw handle intake for my specific practice area?
Yes. The intake qualification flow is customized during setup for your practice area — personal injury, family law, criminal defense, immigration, estate planning, real estate law, and more. We configure the qualifying questions, response templates, and scheduling flow for your specific needs.
What about legal ethics and unauthorized practice of law?
OpenClaw doesn't give legal advice or make legal decisions. It handles purely operational tasks: scheduling, reminders, document organization, and follow-up communications that you approve. It's an administrative assistant, not a legal advisor.
How long does setup take, and will it disrupt my practice?
Remote setup is completed in about a week, working around your schedule. There's no downtime and no migration — we connect to your existing email and calendar. You'll have a working AI assistant handling intake and tracking deadlines within the first day.
