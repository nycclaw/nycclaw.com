What OpenClaw does for your restaurant

Not another tablet cluttering the host stand. OpenClaw runs in the background and handles the work that keeps falling through the cracks.

Reservation Management

OpenClaw monitors your booking channels (OpenTable, Resy, website, phone, email) and confirms every reservation automatically. It sends reminders at 24 hours and 2 hours before the seating. Cancellations trigger your waitlist so tables stay filled during peak hours.

Review Response Automation

New reviews on Google, Yelp, or TripAdvisor get a draft response within minutes, written in your voice. Positive reviews get a warm thank-you. Negative ones get a thoughtful reply and flag your manager immediately so nothing festers in public.

Supplier Ordering

Build orders from par levels, sales forecasts, and what's actually in the walk-in. OpenClaw drafts purchase orders for each supplier, tracks delivery confirmations, and flags price changes. No more guessing how much salmon to order for the weekend.

Menu Updates & 86'd Items

When you 86 a dish mid-service, OpenClaw updates your online menus, delivery platforms, and reservation notes in real time. Seasonal menu changes get pushed to your website, Google Business Profile, and third-party apps in one step instead of five.

Staff Scheduling

OpenClaw builds draft schedules based on availability, labor targets, and expected covers. When someone calls out, it texts available staff and fills the gap. Shift swaps get tracked automatically. You spend minutes on scheduling instead of hours.

Delivery Coordination

Orders from DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and your own site get consolidated into one view. OpenClaw tracks prep times, flags delays, and sends pickup-ready notifications. Your kitchen sees one clean queue instead of four competing tablets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle reservation management?
OpenClaw connects to your reservation system (OpenTable, Resy, Yelp Reservations, or your own booking page) and monitors incoming requests. It confirms bookings, sends reminders, manages waitlists during peak hours, and handles cancellation rebooking automatically. Your host and managers get notified of every change.
Does this replace my POS or reservation system?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools, including Toast, Square, Clover, OpenTable, Resy, and others. It connects via email, calendar, and API integrations to automate the operational tasks that pull your team away from guests. Your current systems stay exactly where they are.
How long does setup take?
Most restaurant setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours. You'll have reservation management, review responses, and supplier ordering running by the end of day one. Fine-tuning happens over the following two weeks as we dial in menu updates, staff scheduling preferences, and delivery workflows.
Is my restaurant data kept private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Supplier pricing, recipes, financial data, and customer information never leave your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how everything works.
Can this work for a restaurant group with multiple locations?
Yes. OpenClaw can be configured across multiple locations with location-specific menus, supplier lists, staffing rules, and reservation settings. Each location gets its own workflows while you maintain centralized oversight of the whole operation.
