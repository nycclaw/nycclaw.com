Sound familiar?

These aren't hypothetical problems. They're the reality of running an insurance agency in 2026.

Producers are buried in quoting, not selling

Every new prospect means gathering info, entering it into multiple carrier portals, comparing quotes, and preparing proposals. Commercial lines can take 1-2 hours per submission across 5-8 carriers. Your $80K+ producers are doing $15/hour data entry work. 30-40% of their time is spent rekeying the same data.

Renewals slip through the cracks — and clients leave

Tracking hundreds of renewal dates, reaching out 60-90 days before expiration, remarketing when rates spike, following up on outstanding renewals. At 84% retention, an agency with 1,000 policies loses 160 per year. That's ~$23,000 in lost annual commission. Top agencies hit 95% by being proactive — but who has the time?

Cross-sell opportunities sit untouched

The auto client without an umbrella. The homeowner without life insurance. The business owner with no cyber liability. You know these gaps exist, but identifying them and acting on them requires time nobody has. Clients with 1.8+ policies stay at 95% retention — single-policy clients churn far more. Every missed cross-sell is a retention risk.

Clients only hear from you when there's a bill

No proactive check-ins, no coverage reviews, no life-event triggers. When rates go up, that's the only time your name shows up. In a commoditized market where anyone can compare rates online, the relationship is your only moat. But if it's purely transactional, clients will leave for Free/year savings.

What OpenClaw does for your agency

Not another dashboard to ignore. OpenClaw works in the background — you interact with it the same way you text a colleague.

Renewal Tracker

Tracks all renewal dates from your AMS, sends 90/60/30-day reminders, drafts personalized client outreach, flags policies with rate increases that need remarketing, and follows up on clients who haven't responded. Never miss a renewal again.

Quoting Prep

Monitors incoming quote requests via email, extracts key info — business type, coverage needs, property details — and pre-fills submission templates. Reduces quoting prep time dramatically so agents focus on the consultative sale, not data entry.

Cross-Sell Engine

Analyzes your book of business to identify coverage gaps per client. Generates personalized recommendations — "John has home + auto but no umbrella" — drafts outreach, and tracks who's been contacted. Turns cross-selling from a someday task into a system.

Client Nurture

Proactive touchpoints — birthday messages, policy anniversary check-ins, coverage review reminders, seasonal tips. Monitors client emails for claims-related language and alerts you immediately. Stay top of mind without the manual effort.

Document Handling

Organizes incoming documents by client and policy, extracts key data from dec pages and endorsements, flags incomplete files, and generates certificates of insurance on request. Keeps your digital file room clean without manual sorting.

Claims Support

Monitors claim-related emails and alerts you immediately. Drafts initial client communications, tracks claim status updates from carriers, and sends proactive updates so clients are never left wondering. You handle decisions; OpenClaw handles the cadence.

Every setup is configured for your specific workflow, AMS, and book of business.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw integrate with my agency management system?
OpenClaw works alongside Applied, Hawksoft, EZLynx, and other popular AMS platforms via email monitoring and data extraction. No complex API integrations needed to start — it connects to your existing workflows and enhances them without replacing your systems.
Will OpenClaw send emails to my clients without my approval?
Never. OpenClaw drafts and suggests — you review and approve before anything goes to a client. You stay in full control of every client communication. Think of it as a tireless assistant that prepares everything for your review.
How is this different from the automation in my AMS?
Your AMS stores data. OpenClaw acts on it — proactively identifying renewals at risk, cross-sell opportunities, and client communication gaps. It's the difference between a filing cabinet and an assistant who reads the files and tells you what to do next.
Is my client data secure?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Your client information, policy details, and communications never leave your control. It's open-source, so there's full transparency into how it works.
How long does setup take?
Most agencies are up and running within a single session (2-3 hours), with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. We start with your highest-impact pain point — usually renewals or quoting — and expand from there.
