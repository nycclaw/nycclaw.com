What AI does for your PI firm

OpenClaw handles the repetitive, time-sensitive work that bogs down your staff. Integrates with Filevine, Litify, Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, CASEpeer, SmartAdvocate, and CloudLex.

Medical Records Automation

OpenClaw generates records requests, sends them to providers, tracks which ones have been returned, and follows up automatically when responses are overdue. Your paralegals stop spending half their day on hold. The system flags when records are incomplete so nothing gets missed before demand letters go out.

Demand Letter Drafting

OpenClaw pulls medical records, treatment summaries, and special damages from the case file to draft a demand letter. Your attorney reviews, edits, and sends. What used to take an associate 3-4 hours of compilation and writing gets reduced to a 30-minute review. The facts are organized. The math is done. Off-the-shelf demand tools exist, but they don't know your firm's templates, your negotiation style, or how you structure arguments for your jurisdiction. This is built around the way you actually practice.

Speed-to-Lead Intake

Missed call or web form submission? OpenClaw responds in seconds. It qualifies the case: accident type, injuries sustained, date of incident, statute of limitations. Captures every detail, then routes to the right attorney with a full summary. Under 60 seconds from first contact to qualified lead.

Case Valuation at Intake

Know what a case is worth before you sign it. OpenClaw pulls from verdict and settlement data, factors in jurisdiction, injury type, and insurance coverage to give your intake team an estimated case value during the first call. Stop wasting months on cases that won't justify the work. Focus on the ones that will.

Client Status Updates

Automated case status messages go out on a schedule you control. When a case moves from treatment to demand, clients get notified. When there's nothing new, they still get a check-in so they know you haven't forgotten them. Fewer "what's happening?" calls. Happier clients. Better reviews.

Lien Tracking and Resolution

Health insurance liens, Medicare/Medicaid liens, hospital liens. OpenClaw tracks every lien on every case, sends reduction requests, follows up on outstanding balances, and calculates net settlement figures. No more scrambling at settlement time to figure out who gets paid what.

Mass Tort Intake and Referral Management

Mass tort campaigns flood your intake with hundreds of similar inquiries. OpenClaw screens each one against your qualification criteria and sorts them instantly. For cases outside your practice area, it manages your referral network: tracks which firms you've sent cases to, what referral fees are owed, and follows up on outcomes.

Every setup is configured for your firm's specific case types, tools, and workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw work for personal injury lawyers?
OpenClaw connects to your phone system, web forms, email, and case management software. When a potential client calls or fills out a form, OpenClaw qualifies the case in seconds, gathering accident type, injury details, and statute of limitations info, then routes it to the right attorney with a full summary. It also automates medical records requests, client status updates, and deadline tracking across your active caseload.
Does this replace my case management software?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools. It integrates with Filevine, Litify, Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, CASEpeer, SmartAdvocate, and CloudLex to handle the repetitive work that bogs down your staff. Your case management system stays the source of truth.
How long does setup take?
Most PI firm setups take 2-3 hours for the initial deployment, with two weeks of fine-tuning afterward. You'll have intake automation running within the first day. More complex setups involving multiple practice areas or mass tort workflows may take a second session.
Is client data secure and compliant?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Client information, case details, and communications never leave your infrastructure. It's open-source software, so your IT team can audit exactly how it works. No third-party cloud storage, no data sharing.
Can OpenClaw handle mass tort intake?
Yes. Mass tort campaigns generate high volumes of repetitive inquiries that need consistent qualification. OpenClaw screens each potential claimant against your criteria (exposure type, diagnosis, timeline) and sorts them into qualified, needs-follow-up, or does-not-qualify. Your intake team only touches the cases that matter.
