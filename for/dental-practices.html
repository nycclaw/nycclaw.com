These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what dental practices deal with every single day.

New patients call while your front desk is with someone else

A potential patient calls at 10am to book a cleaning. Your front desk is checking in Mrs. Johnson, verifying insurance for the next patient, and printing a treatment plan. The call goes to voicemail. That patient calls the practice down the street instead. Studies show 35% of dental calls go unanswered during business hours. Each missed new patient call costs you $1,000+ in lifetime value.

No-shows leave empty chairs that cost you $500+ each

Without consistent reminders, the average dental practice sees a 28% no-show rate. That's 5-6 empty appointment slots per week. At $200-$500 per appointment, you're leaving $4,000-$12,000 on the table every month. Your team tries to confirm appointments manually but they're already stretched thin handling check-ins, insurance, and treatment coordination.

Recall patients disappear for years at a time

Your hygiene schedule should be booked 6 weeks out. Instead, you've got 2,000 patients overdue for their 6-month cleaning. Sending postcards costs $1-2 each and gets a 1-2% response rate. Your front desk is supposed to call them but never gets through the list. Those patients aren't switching dentists on purpose. They just forgot, and nobody reminded them enough times.

Insurance verification eats 10+ hours per week

Before every appointment, someone on your team needs to verify coverage, check remaining benefits, confirm frequencies, and flag any issues. For a practice seeing 20 patients a day, that's 15-20 minutes per patient just on insurance. It's the most tedious part of running a dental office, and getting it wrong means denied claims and awkward conversations about unexpected bills.

What OpenClaw does for your practice

Instant Patient Response

New patient inquiries from your website, Google Business Profile, or email get a response in seconds. OpenClaw collects insurance info, preferred times, and reason for visit, then books them into your next available slot. No more lost calls.

Insurance Verification Support

Review Generation

Treatment Follow-Up

Patients who got a treatment plan but haven't scheduled? OpenClaw follows up with specific, personalized messages about their recommended procedures. No generic blasts. Just the right nudge at the right time to get them back in the chair.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle patient scheduling?
OpenClaw monitors your incoming calls, web forms, and emails. When a patient requests an appointment, it checks your practice management system for open slots, confirms the booking, and sends reminders automatically. You and your front desk get notified of every new appointment.
Does this integrate with my practice management software?
OpenClaw connects to your existing tools including Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and others via email, calendar, and API integrations. It works alongside your current systems rather than replacing them.
How long does setup take for a dental practice?
Most dental practice setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. Your practice will have automated scheduling, recall, and patient communication running within the first day.
Is patient data kept private and HIPAA-compliant?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Patient records, insurance information, and communications never leave your control. The software is open-source, giving you full transparency into how data is handled.
Can this work for a DSO with multiple locations?
Absolutely. OpenClaw can be configured across multiple practice locations with location-specific scheduling rules, provider availability, and recall protocols. Each location gets its own workflows while you maintain centralized oversight.
