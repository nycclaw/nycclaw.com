Support tickets are crushing your team

75% of customer service reps saw record-high ticket volume in 2024. A store doing 200 orders/day gets 30-50 support tickets daily. At 10 minutes each, that's 5-8 hours/day — essentially a $35-50K/year hire just for "where is my order?" and sizing questions. Most founders answer tickets at midnight.

70% of your carts are abandoned

Nearly 3 out of 4 shoppers add items and leave without buying. A store with 1,000 add-to-carts/month is losing ~730 potential orders. Abandoned cart emails sent within 1 hour see a 16% conversion rate, but only 40% of retailers send that first email in time. Most brands set up one Klaviyo flow and forget it.

Returns are eating your margins alive

E-commerce return rates hit 17-20% in 2024 — up from 8% in 2019. On a product with 40% margins, a 20% return rate wipes out nearly half the profit. Every return means customer communication, label generation, tracking, refund processing, and inventory updates. For a 2-person team, 20 returns/day is paralyzing.

Content creation is a never-ending treadmill

43% of small business owners spend 6+ hours/week on social media alone. Add email campaigns, product descriptions, and blog content — that's 11+ hours/week just on content. Skip a week and the algorithm punishes you. You started this brand because you love the product, not because you wanted to be a full-time content creator.

What OpenClaw does for your store

Not another dashboard to ignore. OpenClaw works in the background — you interact with it the same way you text a colleague.

Customer Service Autopilot

Monitors incoming support channels — email, chat widget, social DMs. Instantly handles WISMO queries by pulling tracking info, answers common product questions from your knowledge base, and escalates complex issues to you with full context. You handle 20% of tickets instead of 100%.

Cart Recovery Agent

Monitors cart abandonment events in real-time, triggers personalized multi-channel follow-ups (email, SMS, DMs), adjusts messaging based on cart value and customer history, and reports recovered revenue weekly. Not just a template — an active recovery agent.

Returns & Refunds Processor

Automates return authorization based on your store policy, generates return labels, sends status updates to customers at each stage, processes refunds when items are received, and flags patterns like high-return products or repeat returners. You set the rules, OpenClaw executes.

Content Engine

Drafts social media posts from product photos and brand voice guidelines, writes email campaign copy, generates product descriptions for new SKUs, creates blog content for SEO, and schedules everything. You review and approve — the blank page problem disappears.

Inventory & Stockout Alerts

Monitors inventory levels across connected channels, sends proactive alerts when stock drops below thresholds, flags SKUs trending toward stockout based on velocity, and triggers reorder notifications to suppliers. Not replacing your inventory system — watching it so you don't have to.

Multi-Channel Hub

Monitors orders across Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, and TikTok Shop. Syncs key information, flags inconsistencies like price mismatches and listing errors, routes customer inquiries from any channel to one place, and coordinates product launches across platforms. One conversation instead of 4 dashboards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw connect to my Shopify or WooCommerce store?
Integration via API keys, Shopify webhooks, or email forwarding. No code required. We configure everything during setup so your store data flows automatically.
Can OpenClaw actually respond to my customers, or does it just draft messages?
Both. You set the autonomy level — fully automated for routine queries (WISMO, return status), human-in-the-loop for complex issues. You always have final say.
I already use Klaviyo, Gorgias, or Zendesk — does this replace those tools?
It works alongside them. OpenClaw acts as the operator that uses your existing tools more effectively — triggering flows, responding to tickets, and filling the gaps between your automations.
How long does setup take for an e-commerce store?
Most stores are up and running within 1-2 weeks. We learn your brand voice, product catalog, and policies during onboarding, then configure automations specific to your workflow.
What if OpenClaw makes a mistake with a customer?
You set guardrails — dollar thresholds for refunds, escalation rules, restricted topics. Complex or high-value interactions get routed to you for approval before sending.
