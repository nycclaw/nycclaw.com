These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what event planners deal with every single week.

Vendor coordination chaos across 15+ contacts

Your florist replies at 11pm. Your caterer takes 3 days. The photographer ghosts you until the week before. The venue coordinator only checks email on Tuesdays. You're juggling florists, caterers, photographers, DJs, rental companies, lighting teams, and bakeries, all with different communication habits and response times. One missed confirmation can cascade into a day-of disaster that your client blames you for.

Timeline management across dozens of moving parts

The caterer needs to set up at 3pm but the florist won't be done until 3:30. The band needs a sound check during cocktail hour. The photographer wants 45 minutes for portraits but the couple only budgeted 20. Every change triggers a cascade of updates to 5+ vendors. You spend 8+ hours a week just keeping timelines current, and one version-control mistake means someone shows up at the wrong time.

Clients wanting constant updates (and changing their minds)

The couple texts you at 9pm asking if the florist confirmed the upgraded centerpieces. The mother of the bride emails about table assignments for the fourth time this week. The corporate client wants a status report every Monday. You're spending hours writing update emails that could be automated, and every time a client changes their mind on colors, menu, or layout, you're relaying those changes to half a dozen vendors manually.

Budget tracking scattered across spreadsheets and invoices

The deposit to the venue was on a credit card. The florist invoice came via email. The DJ sent a Venmo request. The rental company hasn't invoiced yet but the balance is due in two weeks. You're toggling between spreadsheets, bank statements, and email threads trying to figure out what's been paid, what's outstanding, and whether you're still on budget. By the time you spot an overage, it's too late to course-correct.

What OpenClaw does for your events

Not another app your team ignores. OpenClaw runs in the background and handles the work that keeps falling through the cracks.

Automated Vendor Follow-Up

OpenClaw tracks vendor confirmations, deposit deadlines, and outstanding questions. When a vendor hasn't replied in 48 hours, it sends a polite follow-up. You get a daily digest of what's confirmed and what still needs attention, without writing a single email.

Timeline Management

Maintains your master event timeline and sends prep reminders to each vendor at the right intervals. When one thing shifts, OpenClaw flags the downstream conflicts so you can adjust before they become problems.

Client Communication Hub

Sends automatic updates to clients when vendors confirm, payments clear, or milestones are reached. Your clients stay informed without you writing recap emails at midnight. They feel taken care of; you get your evenings back.

Budget Tracking & Alerts

Monitors payments, flags overages, and tracks deposits vs. remaining balances across all vendors. When a category is running over budget, you'll know before the next invoice hits. No more piecing together numbers from five different sources.

Guest List & RSVP Management

Tracks RSVPs as they come in, monitors dietary restrictions and plus-ones, and keeps seating assignments up to date. When a guest changes their RSVP or adds a dietary need, the caterer and venue get notified automatically.

Day-Of Coordination Support

Sends morning-of vendor call sheets with arrival times, contact numbers, and setup details. Throughout the event, it pushes timing updates so every vendor knows when they're on. You stay focused on the room instead of your phone.

Every setup is configured for your specific business, tools, and event types.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle vendor coordination?
OpenClaw monitors your email, calendar, and project management tools. When a vendor confirmation is overdue or a deposit deadline is approaching, it sends follow-ups automatically. You get a daily summary of what's confirmed, what's pending, and what needs your attention.
Does this replace my project management software?
No. OpenClaw connects to tools you already use like Aisle Planner, HoneyBook, Dubsado, Google Sheets, and others via email, calendar, and API integrations. It works alongside your current systems and automates the repetitive tasks within them.
How long does setup take for an event planning business?
Most event planning setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. You'll have automated vendor follow-ups, client updates, and timeline management running within the first day.
Is client data kept private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Client details, vendor contracts, budgets, and guest lists never leave your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how data is handled.
Can this handle multiple events simultaneously?
Yes. OpenClaw tracks each event as a separate workflow with its own vendor list, timeline, budget, and client communication thread. You can manage 10+ concurrent events without anything falling through the cracks.
