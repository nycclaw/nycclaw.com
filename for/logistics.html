These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what freight brokers deal with every single day.

Carrier communication is a full-time job

A shipper sends you a load at 9am. You start calling carriers. The first five don't answer. Two call back an hour later but their rates are too high. Three more need details you already sent. By noon you've made 20+ calls, sent a dozen emails, and the load still isn't covered. Meanwhile, two more loads came in. This is the cycle every single day, and every missed callback is margin left on the table.

Rate tracking across lanes is chaos

What did you pay on that Dallas-to-Atlanta lane last month? It's in a spreadsheet somewhere. Or maybe an email chain. Or your coworker's head. When a shipper asks for a quote, you're guessing based on memory instead of working from real data. Old rate confirmations are scattered across inboxes and shared drives. By the time you find the right comp, the load's been booked by someone faster.

Shipment visibility gaps frustrate your shippers

Your shipper calls asking where their freight is. You don't know because the carrier hasn't responded to your last two check calls. So you call the driver, leave a voicemail, and tell the shipper you'll get back to them. An hour later you're still chasing the update. Your shipper's already wondering if they should use a different broker. This happens multiple times a day, and each one chips away at the relationship.

Compliance and document management drowns your team

BOLs, PODs, insurance certificates, carrier packets, broker-carrier agreements. Every load generates a stack of paperwork. Half the time you're chasing carriers for documents they were supposed to send three days ago. Insurance expires without warning. A carrier's authority gets revoked and nobody catches it until there's a problem. The back office spends 8+ hours a week just keeping up with documentation.

What OpenClaw does for your brokerage

Not another app your team ignores. OpenClaw runs in the background and handles the work that keeps falling through the cracks.

Automated Carrier Outreach

Posts loads to your carrier network, sends rate requests via email and text, and follows up on non-responses automatically. Carriers who match your lane and equipment needs get contacted first. You stop playing phone tag and start getting rate confirmations in your inbox.

Rate Intelligence & Tracking

Tracks lane rates over time, flags when a carrier's rate is above market, and maintains a searchable rate history. When a shipper asks for a quote, you've got real data instead of guesswork. Rate comparisons are ready before you pick up the phone.

Shipment Status Updates

Sends check-call messages to carriers at scheduled intervals, collects ETAs, and auto-updates your shipper with tracking info. Your customers get proactive updates instead of calling you for answers you don't have yet.

Document Collection & Management

Requests BOLs, PODs, and insurance docs from carriers automatically. Flags missing paperwork before it becomes a problem. No more chasing drivers for documents three days after delivery. Everything's collected, organized, and accessible when you need it.

Load Matching Support

Monitors incoming load requests and matches them against carrier capacity and lane preferences in your network. When a new load comes in, you already know which carriers are likely to take it and at what rate. Less guessing, faster coverage.

Compliance Monitoring

Tracks carrier insurance expiration dates, authority status, and safety ratings. Alerts you before issues arise so you're never caught off guard. When a carrier's insurance is about to lapse, you'll know days in advance instead of finding out during an audit.

Every setup is configured for your specific brokerage, tools, and freight operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle carrier communication?
OpenClaw monitors your incoming load requests and automatically reaches out to carriers in your network based on lane preferences and capacity. It sends rate requests via email or text, follows up on non-responses, and collects rate confirmations. You get notified when a carrier accepts so you can finalize the booking.
Does this replace my TMS?
No. OpenClaw connects to your existing TMS, whether that's DAT, Tai, McLeod, Aljex, or another platform. It works alongside your current systems by pulling load data and pushing updates back in. Think of it as an extra set of hands that never stops working your boards.
How long does setup take for a freight brokerage?
Most brokerage setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. Your carrier outreach, rate tracking, and check-call workflows will be running within the first day.
Is shipper and carrier data kept private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Shipper contracts, carrier rates, lane data, and all communications stay under your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how data is handled. Nothing gets sent to third-party servers.
Can this work for a brokerage with multiple offices or agents?
Absolutely. OpenClaw can be configured across multiple offices or individual broker desks with agent-specific carrier networks, lane preferences, and shipper accounts. Each agent gets their own workflows while you maintain visibility across the whole operation.
