Drafts initial proposal sections from templates and project-specific requirements. Organizes past project data, team resumes, and compliance checklists for quick insertion. Tracks RFP deadlines and submission requirements. You refine the design narrative — OpenClaw handles the grunt work.

Can it integrate with our existing project management tools?
OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools — Monograph, Deltek, Monday.com, or whatever you use — by connecting to your email, calendar, and files. No migration needed. It reads from where you already work and fills the gaps your PM tool doesn't cover.
How long does setup take?
Most architecture firm setups are completed in a single session (2-3 hours), with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. You'll have a working AI assistant tracking deadlines and drafting communications within the first day.

OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Your client information, project details, and design files never leave your control. It processes communication and scheduling metadata — not your design work itself. It's open-source, so there's full transparency into how it works.
How is this different from hiring a project coordinator?
A human project coordinator costs $4,000-6,000 per month, has limited hours, and requires training on your specific workflows. OpenClaw runs 24/7, never forgets a deadline, and costs a fraction of a hire. It handles the routine operational tasks so you can invest in people who add creative value.
