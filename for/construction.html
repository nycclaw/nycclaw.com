These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what construction teams deal with on every single project.

RFIs and submittals keep falling through the cracks

You're managing five active projects. An RFI comes in via email on the hospital job, another one gets texted from the foreman on the retail build, and a submittal response is sitting in someone's inbox from two weeks ago. Nobody has a clear picture of what's pending, what's overdue, or what's blocking the next pour. One missed RFI can delay a project by weeks and cost tens of thousands in idle crews.

Change orders disappear into email threads

The owner requested a scope change three weeks ago. Your PM approved it verbally on-site. The sub started the work. But the change order was never formally documented, priced, or signed. Now you're eating $40K because there's no paper trail. This happens on nearly every project. 70% of construction projects go over budget, and poorly tracked change orders are a major reason why.

Daily logs are always three days behind

Your supers are on-site from 6am to 5pm managing crews, inspections, and deliveries. The last thing they want to do at the end of the day is write up a detailed log of weather conditions, manpower, equipment, work completed, and safety observations. So logs get batched on Friday afternoon from memory, miss critical details, and become useless when you need them for a dispute six months later.

Subcontractor coordination eats your entire day

You've got 15 subs on a commercial build. The electrician can't start until the framer is done. The inspector needs 48 hours notice. The concrete delivery is scheduled for Thursday but the forms won't be ready until Friday. You're spending half your day on the phone playing traffic cop, and one miscommunication means an entire crew shows up to a site that isn't ready for them.

What OpenClaw does for your projects

Not another app your team ignores. OpenClaw runs in the background and handles the work that keeps falling through the cracks.

Automated Daily Log Generation

Your super sends a quick voice memo or text at the end of the day. OpenClaw turns it into a formatted daily log with weather, manpower, equipment, work completed, and safety notes. Logs are complete, consistent, and ready the same day they happen.

RFI & Submittal Tracking

Every RFI and submittal gets logged, assigned, and tracked automatically. OpenClaw sends reminders when responses are overdue, flags items blocking critical path work, and gives you a single view of what's pending across all your projects.

Budget & Change Order Monitoring

OpenClaw tracks every change order from request to approval, flags scope changes that haven't been documented, and keeps a running budget comparison against your original estimate. You'll see cost overruns developing before they become disasters.

Schedule Coordination

Coordinate crews, subs, inspections, and deliveries across multiple jobsites. OpenClaw flags scheduling conflicts, sends automated reminders to subs before their start dates, and notifies you when upstream work is running behind and will affect downstream trades.

Safety Compliance Documentation

Toolbox talks, incident reports, inspection checklists, and safety observations get documented and filed automatically. OpenClaw tracks expiring certifications, flags overdue safety training, and keeps your compliance records audit-ready at all times.

Project Communication Hub

Emails, texts, and calls about a project get organized into one searchable thread per jobsite. When the owner asks about a decision made four months ago, you can find it in seconds instead of digging through hundreds of emails and text chains.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this replace our project management software?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing tools like Procore, PlanGrid, Buildertrend, and others. It connects via email, calendar, and API integrations to pull data from those systems and automate the repetitive work around them. Think of it as a layer on top of your current stack, not a replacement.
How long does setup take?
The initial setup takes 2-3 hours. We configure your project templates, RFI workflows, daily log formats, and notification rules in that session. Over the following two weeks, we fine-tune everything based on how your team actually uses it on active projects.
Can it handle multiple active jobsites?
Yes. OpenClaw supports site-specific workflows, so each jobsite gets its own daily log templates, RFI tracking, schedule rules, and notification settings. You get a single dashboard view across all projects while keeping everything organized per site.
Is project data secure?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Project documents, budgets, change orders, and communications never leave your control. The software is open-source, so you have full visibility into how your data is handled.
Does it work for specialty trades too?
Yes. OpenClaw is fully configurable for any trade: electrical, plumbing, HVAC, concrete, or steel. The workflows, templates, and tracking rules get customized to match how your specific trade operates.
