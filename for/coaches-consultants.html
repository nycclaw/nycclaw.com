Prospects ghost you after the first inquiry

Someone fills out your contact form at 9pm on a Tuesday. You're with a client all Wednesday morning. By the time you respond Thursday, they've already booked a discovery call with another coach. Speed matters. The coaches who respond within an hour close 3x more discovery calls than those who wait a day.

Session prep eats into your billable hours

Before every session, you're reviewing notes from last time, checking what homework they committed to, and pulling together relevant frameworks. It takes 15-20 minutes per client. Multiply that by 20 clients and you've lost an entire workday each week just getting ready for sessions you're already booked for.

Follow-up falls through the cracks

You know you should send a check-in email between sessions. You know that post-engagement follow-up is how you get referrals. But when you're running back-to-back coaching calls, those "quick follow-ups" pile up until they feel awkward to send. Clients notice the silence, even if they don't say anything.

You're the bottleneck in your own business

Invoicing, proposals, contract renewals, content creation, LinkedIn posts, newsletter drafts. You can't hire a full-time assistant for a solo practice, and VAs need constant direction. So everything lands on you. Your capacity tops out at 15-20 clients not because of session time, but because of everything around it.

What OpenClaw does for your practice

Not another SaaS tool collecting dust. OpenClaw works in the background and talks to you like a colleague via text or chat.

Instant Client Intake

A prospect fills out your form or sends an email. OpenClaw responds in seconds, asks about their goals, current challenges, and timeline. Then it books them directly on your calendar and sends you a pre-call brief. You show up prepared, they feel heard.

Session Prep Briefs

Before each session, OpenClaw pulls together a one-page brief: what you covered last time, commitments the client made, progress on their goals, and suggested talking points. Delivered to your inbox 30 minutes before the call starts.

Between-Session Follow-Up

Automated mid-week check-ins, accountability nudges, and resource sharing. OpenClaw drafts personalized messages based on what each client is working on. You review before they send. Clients feel supported without you writing 20 individual emails.

Referral & Re-Engagement

When an engagement wraps up, OpenClaw keeps the relationship warm. Quarterly check-ins, relevant article shares, anniversary touchpoints. Past clients remember you when a colleague asks "know any good coaches?" because you stayed present without being pushy.

Proposals & Invoicing

Tell OpenClaw about a new engagement and it drafts the proposal from your templates, tracks contract status, sends payment reminders, and flags overdue invoices. No more chasing payments at 10pm on a Sunday.

Content & Thought Leadership

OpenClaw turns your session insights (with client details removed) into LinkedIn posts, newsletter drafts, and blog outlines. You've got a goldmine of expertise from every coaching conversation. Now it actually gets published.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle client intake for coaches?
OpenClaw monitors your inquiry channels: website forms, email, LinkedIn messages. When a potential client reaches out, it responds within seconds with a personalized message, asks qualifying questions about their goals and timeline, and books a discovery call on your calendar. You get a brief with everything they shared before the call starts.
Will my clients know they're talking to an AI?
That's your call. Most coaches use OpenClaw for the initial response and scheduling, then take over personally once a conversation is underway. The handoff is seamless. You can also have OpenClaw draft messages for your review before anything sends, so every touchpoint still sounds like you.
How long does setup take?
Most coaching practice setups wrap up in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over two weeks. You'll have automated intake, session prep, and follow-up workflows running within the first day.
Is my client data private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Session notes, client details, and private conversations never leave your control. It's open-source, so you can verify exactly how it handles your information.
I'm a solo consultant. Is this overkill for a one-person practice?
It's the opposite. Solo practitioners benefit the most because there's no team to delegate to. OpenClaw handles the admin work that eats into your billable hours: intake, scheduling, follow-ups, session prep. So you can focus on the work clients actually pay you for.
