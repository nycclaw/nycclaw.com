You're the CEO, recruiter, support rep, and office manager

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a solo founder — is OpenClaw worth it before I have a team?
Yes — solo founders benefit the most because there's literally no one to delegate to. OpenClaw handles the ops work that would otherwise eat your product and sales time. It acts as your AI chief of staff from day one.
How is this different from a virtual assistant?
VAs need training, management, and work business hours. OpenClaw is always on, learns your workflows automatically, and costs a fraction of a human VA ($800-2000/mo). It runs on your own devices with full privacy — no third-party access to your data.
Can OpenClaw actually draft my investor updates?
Yes — it pulls metrics from your connected tools (Stripe revenue, user counts, runway from banking data) and drafts structured updates. You review and send. What used to take 6 hours becomes 20 minutes of review.
What tools does OpenClaw integrate with?
Email (Gmail/Outlook), calendar, Slack, Notion, Stripe, Intercom, ATS platforms, and more. We configure integrations during setup based on your specific stack.
Is my data private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Your company data, investor communications, and customer information never leave your control. It's open-source, so there's full transparency into how it works.
