Internet leads go cold because nobody calls fast enough

You're getting 200 internet leads a month from AutoTrader, Cars.com, your website, and manufacturer programs. Your BDC is supposed to call every one within 15 minutes. In reality, only 40% get contacted within the first hour. The rest sit in a queue while your salespeople work the floor. Your service department loses customers the day their warranty expires

23% of service customers leave after their warranty runs out. They start going to independent shops for oil changes and brake jobs. Nobody at your dealership is tracking warranty expirations or reaching out proactively. That's $1,200 in average profit per customer walking out the door over the next five years. Multiply that across hundreds of customers, and your service department is quietly bleeding revenue every month.

Your BDC can't tell which leads are actually hot

A customer who configured a specific vehicle on your website and requested a quote is getting the same follow-up as someone who filled out a generic "more info" form. Your team treats every lead the same because the CRM doesn't give them a clear picture of buyer intent. The high-intent leads get buried under the volume, and the ones ready to buy today end up waiting for a callback that comes too late.

F&I paperwork slows down every deal

The customer said yes two hours ago, but they're still sitting in the F&I office. Credit applications, lender submissions, product menus, and contract printing all take time. Your F&I manager is juggling three deals at once. Customers get frustrated. Some walk out before signing. The ones who stay leave with a bad taste, which means fewer referrals, fewer repeat buyers, and worse reviews online.

Review Generation

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this replace our DMS?
No. OpenClaw integrates with your existing DMS, including CDK, Reynolds & Reynolds, DealerSocket, and others. It pulls data from your current systems and works alongside them. Nothing gets replaced.
How long does setup take?
The initial setup takes 2-3 hours. Over the following two weeks, we fine-tune lead routing, service reminder timing, and follow-up sequences based on how your team actually works. Your dealership will have automated lead response running on day one.
Can it handle multiple rooftops?
Yes. OpenClaw supports location-specific workflows, so each rooftop gets its own lead routing, inventory feeds, and service schedules. You keep centralized visibility across all locations.
Is customer data secure?
OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Customer records, deal information, and communications stay under your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how data is handled.
Does it work for used-only dealers too?
Yes. OpenClaw is configurable for any dealership type, including franchise, independent, used-only, and specialty lots. The workflows adapt to your specific inventory, lead sources, and sales process.
