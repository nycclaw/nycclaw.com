Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle leads when I'm on a job site?
OpenClaw monitors your incoming web forms, emails, and lead sources like Google, Angi, and Thumbtack. When a homeowner requests a quote, it responds in seconds with your availability and asks qualifying questions about the job. You get notified of every new lead so you can follow up when you're off the roof.
Does this replace my CRM or field service software?
No. OpenClaw connects to your existing tools like Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and others via email, calendar, and API integrations. It works alongside your current systems rather than replacing them.
How long does setup take for a contracting business?
Most contractor setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. You'll have automated lead response, estimate follow-ups, and scheduling running within the first day.
Can this work for a company with multiple crews?
Yes. OpenClaw can be configured for multiple crews with crew-specific scheduling rules, job assignments, and availability. Each crew gets their own notifications while you maintain oversight of the full operation.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Customer records, job details, and communications never leave your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how data is handled.
