These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what veterinary clinics deal with every single day.

New clients call while your staff is triaging an emergency

A dog owner calls at 2pm to schedule a wellness exam. Your receptionist is on another line with a panicked cat owner, the vet tech is restraining a nervous Labrador, and two clients are waiting at the front desk to check out. That call goes to voicemail. The dog owner finds another clinic with online booking instead. Each lost new client represents $2,000+ in lifetime value when you factor in annual exams, vaccines, and dental cleanings.

No-shows leave exam rooms empty and vets standing idle

Without consistent reminders, veterinary clinics see a 15-20% no-show rate. That's 3-4 empty appointment slots per day in a busy practice. At $150-$300 per visit, you're losing $2,000-$5,000 every week. Your team tries to call and confirm appointments manually, but they're already juggling prescription refills, lab results, and walk-in emergencies.

Vaccination reminders fall through the cracks constantly

Hundreds of patients in your system are overdue for rabies boosters, DHPP, bordetella, or feline FVRCP. Postcards cost $1-2 each with a tiny response rate. Your staff is supposed to call these clients but the list keeps growing because there's never enough time. These pets aren't switching vets. Their owners just forgot, and nobody followed up enough.

Prescription refills and lab results create a communication bottleneck

Mrs. Garcia calls to refill her cat's thyroid medication. Your tech writes a sticky note. The vet approves it two hours later. Someone needs to call the pharmacy and then call Mrs. Garcia back. Meanwhile, bloodwork results came in for three patients and those owners are waiting to hear back. Each of these tasks takes 5-10 minutes of phone tag, and they pile up fast.

What OpenClaw does for your clinic

Not another app your team ignores. OpenClaw runs in the background and handles the work that keeps falling through the cracks.

Appointment Scheduling

Client inquiries from your website, Google Business Profile, or email get a response in seconds. OpenClaw collects patient species, reason for visit, and preferred times, then books them into the right slot with the right provider. No more lost calls.

Vaccination & Prescription Reminders

Pets overdue for vaccines, heartworm prevention, or prescription refills trigger automatic outreach. OpenClaw sends personalized reminders at the right intervals via text and email. Clients book directly from the reminder.

Client Communication

Automated appointment confirmations, post-visit follow-ups, and discharge instruction summaries. Clients get clear updates without your staff playing phone tag all afternoon. Happy clients leave better reviews, too.

Lab Results Delivery

When bloodwork or diagnostic results come back, OpenClaw drafts a client-friendly summary for vet review and sends it once approved. No more sticky notes, no forgotten callbacks. Clients get their pet's results the same day instead of waiting 48 hours.

Telemedicine Support

For follow-up checks, minor concerns, or post-surgery monitoring, OpenClaw can schedule and coordinate virtual consultations. It sends the video link, collects pre-visit notes from the client, and files everything in the patient record automatically.

Inventory Tracking

OpenClaw monitors stock levels for medications, vaccines, and supplies. When inventory drops below your set thresholds, it flags the item and can draft reorder requests to your distributor. No more discovering you're out of Rimadyl mid-appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle appointment scheduling for a vet clinic?
OpenClaw monitors your incoming calls, web forms, and emails. When a pet owner requests an appointment, it checks your practice management system for open slots based on appointment type and provider availability, confirms the booking, and sends reminders automatically. Your front desk and veterinarians get notified of every new appointment.
Does this replace my practice management software?
No. OpenClaw connects to your existing tools like Cornerstone, AVImark, eVetPractice, and others via email, calendar, and API integrations. It works alongside your current systems. Think of it as an extra team member that handles the repetitive tasks your staff shouldn't be spending time on.
How long does setup take for a veterinary clinic?
Most veterinary clinic setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. Your clinic will have automated scheduling, vaccination reminders, and client communication running within the first day.
Is client and patient data kept private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Client records, patient histories, and communications never leave your control. The software is open-source, so you have full transparency into how data is handled.
Can this work for a multi-location veterinary group?
Absolutely. OpenClaw can be configured across multiple clinic locations with location-specific scheduling rules, provider availability, and reminder protocols. Each location gets its own workflows while you maintain centralized oversight.
