Sound familiar?

These aren't hypothetical problems. They're what home services companies deal with every single day.

Leads call while your techs are on a job

A homeowner's AC goes out at 2pm in July. They call three HVAC companies. You're on a roof. The plumber down the street is knee-deep in a water heater install. Their calls go to voicemail. The homeowner books with the company that picked up. According to ServiceTitan data, 62% of homeowners hire the first company that responds. Every missed call is a $300-$2,000 job walking to your competitor.

Quotes sit in your truck for days before going out

You visit a property, take notes, snap some photos. Then you drive to the next job. By the time you sit down to write up the quote, it's 9pm and you've got six more to send. Some don't go out for three or four days. By then, the customer's already signed with someone faster. Speed-to-quote is one of the biggest conversion factors in home services, and most companies are terrible at it.

Seasonal work dries up because you forgot to market

You're slammed from May through September. October hits and the phone stops ringing. You never sent out furnace tune-up reminders. You never followed up with last year's gutter cleaning customers. Spring landscaping clients didn't hear from you in February. The busy season kept you too busy to plan for the slow season, and now you're scrambling.

Great work, zero reviews to show for it

You installed a perfect HVAC system. The customer was thrilled. But nobody asked for a review, and the moment passed. Your competitor with mediocre work has 200+ Google reviews because they have a system. You've got 23 reviews from the last four years. On Google Maps, review count and recency determine who shows up first. No reviews means no visibility, no matter how good your work is.

Furnace tune-ups in October. AC maintenance in March. Gutter cleaning in November. OpenClaw sends targeted outreach to past customers at the right time based on their service history. You stay booked through the slow months instead of scrambling.

Quote Follow-Up

Sent a quote three days ago and heard nothing? OpenClaw follows up automatically with personalized messages. It tracks which quotes are pending, which need a nudge, and which customers went cold. Your close rate goes up without you chasing anyone.

Review Generation

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OpenClaw handle dispatching and scheduling for home services?
OpenClaw monitors your incoming calls, web forms, and emails. When a customer requests service, it checks your crew availability, assigns the right technician based on location and skill set, confirms the booking, and sends reminders automatically. You and your dispatcher get notified of every new job.
Does this replace my field service management software?
No. OpenClaw connects to your existing tools like ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, and others via email, calendar, and API integrations. It works alongside your current systems, handling the communication and follow-up layer that most FSM platforms don't do well on their own.
How long does setup take?
Most home services setups are completed in a single session of 2-3 hours, with fine-tuning over the following two weeks. Your business will have automated dispatching, customer follow-up, and review requests running within the first day.
Is my customer data kept private?
Yes. OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Customer records, job history, and communications never leave your control. The software is open-source, giving you full transparency into how data is handled.
Can this work for a company with multiple crews or service areas?
Absolutely. OpenClaw can be configured across multiple crews and service zones with location-specific routing rules, technician availability, and follow-up protocols. Each crew gets its own workflows while you maintain centralized oversight.
