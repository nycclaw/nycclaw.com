These aren't hypothetical problems. They're the reality of running a private practice in 2026.

No-shows are bleeding your revenue dry

The average outpatient no-show rate is 23-33%. A solo practitioner seeing 20 patients/day with a 25% no-show rate loses 5 patient slots daily. At $150-$300 per visit, that's $187,000-$375,000/year in lost revenue. Empty chairs, idle providers, wasted time that can't be recovered.

Missed calls mean missed patients

Practices miss 42% of incoming calls during business hours. When your front desk is checking in a patient, the phone rings — and the new patient on the other end hangs up and calls the next provider. In dental, 68% of calls go unanswered. Each missed new patient call costs $850 in immediate revenue and up to $8,000 in lifetime value.

Admin is eating your clinical hours alive

Physicians spend 2 hours on EHR and admin for every 1 hour of direct patient care. Only 27% of office time goes to face-to-face care. 89% of practices say prior authorization is "very or extremely burdensome." That's 7+ hours every week a solo practitioner could be seeing 20-30 more patients — $3,000-$9,000/week left on the table.

Patients drift away without systematic follow-up

Patient lifetime value is $12,000-$15,000. Acquiring a new patient costs 5-8x more than retaining one. But most small practices have no systematic recall or nurture process. Patients don't come back for their 6-month cleaning, their annual physical, their therapy check-in. A practice losing 10 patients/month to attrition loses $120,000-$150,000 in lifetime value monthly.

What OpenClaw does for your practice

Not another dashboard to ignore. OpenClaw works in the background — you interact with it the same way you text a colleague.

Missed Call Recovery

When your front desk can't answer, OpenClaw catches the call. It answers common questions (hours, location, insurance accepted), captures new patient info, schedules appointments directly, and routes urgent matters to the on-call provider. No more patients lost to voicemail.

No-Show Prevention

Automated reminders via text and email at optimal intervals — 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before appointments. Handles confirmations and reschedules conversationally. Fills cancelled slots by contacting waitlisted patients. Practices see no-show rates drop 30-50%.

Digital Intake & Forms

Send intake forms to patients before their appointment via text or email. Pre-populate returning patient data, collect insurance info and consent forms digitally, and sync with your EHR. Patients arrive ready to see the doctor, not ready to fill out a clipboard.

Patient Retention & Nurture

Automated recall reminders for cleanings, annuals, and therapy check-ins. Birthday messages, health tips, satisfaction surveys, and Google review requests after positive visits. Builds loyalty without lifting a finger — every message feels personal.

Follow-Up Automation

Post-visit follow-ups, treatment plan adherence check-ins, prescription refill reminders, and lab result notifications — all automated. Personalizes messages based on visit type and patient history. Escalates to the provider when clinical judgment is needed.

Insurance & Billing Support

Pre-verify insurance eligibility before appointments, draft prior authorization requests, track claim statuses, flag denials for follow-up, and handle routine patient billing inquiries. You focus on clinical decisions; OpenClaw handles the paper trail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OpenClaw HIPAA-compliant?
OpenClaw takes HIPAA seriously. We help practices configure communication workflows that align with HIPAA requirements — including secure messaging, appropriate data handling, and patient consent management. OpenClaw does not store clinical records; it orchestrates communications through your existing compliant systems.
Can OpenClaw integrate with my EHR or practice management system?
OpenClaw connects with popular practice management systems and EHRs via API integrations, webhooks, and email monitoring. During setup, we configure it to work with your existing tools — whether that's Dentrix, Athenahealth, DrChrono, Jane App, or others.
Will patients know they're talking to an AI?
OpenClaw communicates on behalf of your practice in a natural, professional tone. For scheduling confirmations, reminders, and routine inquiries, most patients can't tell the difference. For clinical questions or anything requiring provider judgment, OpenClaw escalates to your team immediately.
How quickly can OpenClaw be set up for my practice?
Most practices are fully configured within 1-2 weeks. The remote setup covers integration with your existing systems, customization of patient communication workflows, and staff training. We handle the technical work — you just tell us how your practice operates.
What happens to my existing phone system and processes?
OpenClaw layers on top of your existing systems — it doesn't replace them. Your phone number, your EHR, your scheduling software all stay the same. OpenClaw fills the gaps: catching missed calls, sending reminders your staff forgets, following up when the front desk is too busy.
