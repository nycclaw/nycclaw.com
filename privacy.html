Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use NYC Claw services, we may collect:

  • Email address (when you subscribe to our newsletter or book a service)
  • Name and contact information (when you book a setup service)
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe; we do not store card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and deliver our AI setup services
  • Send you our newsletter (if you subscribed)
  • Communicate about your service appointments
  • Improve our services

3. Data Storage

Newsletter subscriber data is stored securely on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Cloudflare for website hosting and data storage
  • Google Fonts for typography

5. Your Rights

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.

6. OpenClaw Setup Privacy

During our setup service, we configure OpenClaw on your devices. We do not retain access to your AI accounts, API keys, conversations, or any data processed by OpenClaw after the setup is complete. OpenClaw itself is open-source and runs locally on your devices.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach out via our Discord community or email us at privacy@nycclaw.com.
