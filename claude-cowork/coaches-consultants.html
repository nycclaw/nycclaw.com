The real problem with coaching and consulting in 2026

Your billable hours disappear into admin work that doesn't require your expertise.

Client onboarding eats your non-billable hours

Welcome packets, intake questionnaires, contract generation, scheduling setup, access provisioning. Every new client means 2-3 hours of admin before the real work starts. Multiply that by 5 new clients a month and you've lost a full working day.

Session notes sit unfinished in your notebook

You take great notes during calls. But turning raw notes into structured summaries, action items, and follow-up emails takes another 30 minutes per session. With 15-20 sessions a week, that's 10+ hours of post-call admin.

You create content once and it dies on one platform

A 60-minute coaching call or workshop contains enough material for a blog post, three LinkedIn updates, a newsletter section, and a podcast outline. But repurposing takes time, so most of that content never gets reused.

Proposals take too long for what they are

Every prospective client wants a custom proposal. Scoping, pricing, deliverables, timelines, case studies. You know the structure by heart, but writing each one still takes 1-2 hours. Some of those prospects don't even convert.

How Claude Cowork solves it

Here's what Cowork actually does for coaches and consultants, week to week.

Client Onboarding Documents

Cowork generates the complete onboarding package for each new client: welcome email, intake questionnaire, service agreement, scheduling instructions, and resource access guide. Give it the client name, engagement type, and start date. Everything else follows your templates. Two hours of admin done in minutes.

Session Note Synthesis

After a coaching call, drop your raw notes (or a Fireflies transcript) into Cowork. It produces a structured session summary: key discussion points, client commitments, your action items, and a follow-up email draft. The summary follows your format, not a generic template. Fifteen minutes of post-call work done in seconds.

Content Repurposing from Calls

Cowork takes a session transcript or workshop recording transcript and extracts publishable content: a long-form blog post, 3-5 LinkedIn updates, a newsletter section, and a podcast episode outline. Each piece matches your voice and style because we build that into the custom skill. One call becomes a week of content.

Proposal Generation

Give Cowork the prospect's name, business context, goals, and engagement type. It drafts a complete proposal: executive summary, scope of work, deliverables, timeline, pricing options, and relevant case studies pulled from your portfolio. Your standard structure, populated with prospect-specific details. Review and send, instead of write from scratch.

Course Material Creation

Cowork helps you build course content from your existing material. It reads your session notes, frameworks, worksheets, and past presentations, then organizes them into module outlines, lesson plans, student handouts, and assessment questions. Whether you're building an online course or a live workshop curriculum, Cowork structures what you already know into teachable format.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Cowork match my writing voice and coaching style?
Yes. We build custom skills that encode your specific voice, terminology, frameworks, and formatting preferences. When Cowork drafts a LinkedIn post or client email, it sounds like you because we've trained the skill on examples of your actual writing. Not generic coaching language.
How does Cowork handle client confidentiality?
Cowork runs in an isolated environment on your local computer. Client session notes and personal information stay on your machine. Conversation history is stored locally, not on Anthropic's servers. We configure folder access so Cowork only reads data you explicitly share with it.
Can I use Cowork to build and sell online courses?
Cowork helps you organize and structure course content from your existing material. It reads your session notes, frameworks, and past content, then produces module outlines, lesson scripts, handouts, and quiz questions. The content creation is yours. Cowork handles the organization and formatting.
Does Cowork work with Fireflies or other transcription tools?
Yes. Cowork connects to Fireflies via its MCP connector, giving it direct access to your meeting transcripts. After a coaching session, Cowork can automatically pull the transcript and produce session notes, action items, and follow-up emails without you needing to copy anything manually.
How long does setup take for a coaching or consulting practice?
Initial setup takes one session. We connect your tools, install plugins, and build custom skills for your most common workflows: session notes, proposals, and content repurposing. Over two weeks, we refine the skills based on your actual output preferences and train you on the day-to-day workflows.
