The real problem with mortgage brokerage in 2026

Your time goes to document assembly instead of client advising.

Rate comparisons eat into client time

Pulling rates from multiple lenders, formatting comparison sheets, and explaining the differences to borrowers. Every client wants options. Building those comparison docs by hand means less time actually advising clients.

Pre-approval packets are repetitive assembly work

Gathering income docs, employment verification, credit pulls, and asset statements into a formatted packet for each lender. The requirements are similar across lenders but the formats differ. You're assembling the same puzzle with slightly different pieces, over and over.

Client follow-up falls through the cracks

Borrowers go quiet. Rate locks expire. Documents need updating. Keeping track of who needs what, when, across 30+ active files is a full-time job on its own. Missed follow-ups cost deals.

Compliance documentation keeps growing

RESPA disclosures, state licensing requirements, fair lending documentation, HMDA reporting. Regulators add requirements faster than you can update your processes. One missed disclosure can trigger an audit.

How Claude Cowork solves it

These are real workflows we build and configure for mortgage brokerages.

Rate Comparison Documents

Cowork pulls rate data from your lender sheets or exports, builds side-by-side comparison tables (rate, APR, points, closing costs, monthly payment), and formats them as client-ready PDFs. Each comparison includes a plain-language summary explaining which option fits the borrower's situation. Updated rates mean updated docs in minutes, not hours.

Pre-Approval Packet Prep

Give Cowork your borrower's documents and the target lender's requirements. It organizes everything into the correct format, flags missing items, and generates the cover sheet with borrower details and loan parameters. When you're submitting to three lenders at once, Cowork builds three properly formatted packets from the same source documents.

Client Follow-Up Sequences

Cowork drafts follow-up emails based on where each borrower sits in the pipeline. Missing documents, rate lock deadlines, application status updates, closing date reminders. Set this up as a scheduled task and Cowork generates your follow-up list every morning with ready-to-send drafts.

Compliance Documentation

Build a custom skill that generates required disclosures based on loan type, state, and borrower profile. Cowork populates the correct forms, checks for completeness, and flags anything that needs manual review. RESPA disclosures, state-specific notices, and fair lending documentation produced consistently every time.

Referral Partner Communications and Market Updates

Cowork generates weekly market update reports for your referral network: real estate agents, financial planners, CPAs. Rate trends, program changes, market conditions. Each report positions you as the informed broker your partners want to work with. Scheduled to run automatically every Monday.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Cowork pull live mortgage rates automatically?
Cowork can process rate sheets and lender exports that you receive. It reads PDFs, spreadsheets, and email attachments containing rate data. While it doesn't connect to live rate feeds directly, it can process updated rate sheets as soon as they arrive and build comparison docs within minutes.
How does Cowork handle different lender requirements for pre-approval packets?
We build separate skills for each lender you work with regularly. Each skill knows that lender's specific document requirements, formatting preferences, and submission guidelines. Your staff runs the same command, and Cowork assembles the packet correctly for whichever lender you specify.
Is borrower data safe with Claude Cowork?
Cowork runs in an isolated environment on your local computer. Borrower documents stay on your machine. Conversation history is stored locally, not on Anthropic's servers. We configure the setup to work within your existing data handling policies and connect only to the systems you approve.
Can Cowork help with HMDA reporting?
Yes. We can build custom skills that compile HMDA data from your loan files, check for completeness and accuracy against reporting requirements, and format the data for submission. The skill flags any entries that need manual review before filing.
How long does setup take for a mortgage brokerage?
Initial setup takes one session. We connect your tools, install plugins, and build your first custom skills for rate comparisons and pre-approval packets. Over two weeks, we add compliance workflows, set up scheduled tasks, and train your team on the day-to-day usage.
