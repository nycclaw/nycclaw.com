The real problem with marketing in 2026

Every channel demands content. Your team can't keep up.

Content production is a bottleneck

Blog posts, social captions, email campaigns, ad copy, weekly reports. Your team is expected to produce across every channel, every week. The volume keeps growing, but headcount doesn't. Something always slips, and it's usually the content that takes the most thought.

SEO optimization is manual and slow

Checking rankings, finding keyword opportunities, auditing pages for schema markup, building internal links. Each task requires pulling data from Ahrefs or SimilarWeb, analyzing it in a spreadsheet, and deciding what to do. By the time you've finished the audit, the data is already stale.

Brand voice is inconsistent across channels

Three people write for the same brand, and it sounds like three different companies. The brand guide exists in a PDF nobody opens. New hires take months to internalize the voice. Freelancers never quite get it right. The result is a fragmented presence that erodes trust with your audience.

Competitive monitoring is reactive

You find out about a competitor's new campaign after your sales team asks why they're losing deals. Tracking competitor content, traffic changes, and new launches is tedious. Most teams do it quarterly if at all, which means you're always a step behind instead of ahead.

How Claude Cowork solves it

These are real workflows running in marketing teams today. Not demos.

SEO Automation via Ahrefs

The Ahrefs connector pulls keyword data, backlink profiles, and content gaps directly into Cowork. Run /seo-audit on any page and get specific action items: schema to add, internal links to build, titles to rewrite. A Reddit user (u/rebelytics) built self-improving skills for trending topics research, product feed optimization, and image alt text generation at scale.

Content Calendar Scheduling

Cowork runs weekly scheduled tasks that research trending topics in your niche, classify them by content potential and business impact, and build a plan across channels. Each run accesses previous results so content builds logically instead of repeating itself. You review the plan, then Cowork drafts the actual posts.

Campaign Planning

Give Cowork a campaign brief and it generates a full execution plan with deliverables across email (Klaviyo), social, and paid channels. Timelines, copy drafts, audience segments, A/B test variants. Your team reviews and refines instead of starting from a blank document every time.

Brand Voice Skills

We create custom SKILL.md files that encode your brand's tone, terminology, and style rules. Claude applies them automatically to every piece of content it creates. Blog posts, emails, social captions, ad copy. Every team member and freelancer gets the same consistent voice without memorizing a brand guide.

Competitive Intelligence

SimilarWeb and Ahrefs connectors feed scheduled monitoring tasks. Cowork generates weekly reports on competitor moves: traffic changes, new content published, keyword shifts, backlink gains. You get a brief in Slack every Monday morning instead of spending hours pulling reports manually.
