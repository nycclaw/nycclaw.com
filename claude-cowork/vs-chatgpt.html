Where Cowork Wins

Claude Cowork is built for people who need an AI agent that actually does work on their computer. Not just answers questions. Does the work.

Real File System Access

Cowork reads, edits, creates, and organizes files in folders you specify on your machine. A real estate agent can point Cowork at a folder of property photos and listing data, and it'll generate formatted MLS descriptions. A lawyer can drop a stack of contracts in a folder and get a risk assessment memo. ChatGPT's agent runs in a cloud sandbox. It can't touch the files sitting on your desktop.

Scheduled Recurring Tasks

Set up a Monday morning pipeline summary. A Friday afternoon timesheet reminder. A monthly client report draft. Cowork runs these automatically at the cadence you choose: daily, weekly, or monthly. Each scheduled task has access to all your connectors and plugins. ChatGPT can schedule messages, but it can't execute multi-step workflows on a timer.

38+ Business Tool Connectors

Cowork connects to HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Linear, Asana, Gmail, and dozens more through the MCP protocol. All OAuth-based, all respecting your existing account permissions. ChatGPT has some integrations, but fewer connectors and less structure around how they work.

Privacy by Architecture

Cowork runs in an isolated virtual machine on your local computer. Conversation history stays on your machine. It doesn't appear in Anthropic's audit logs, compliance API, or data exports. For law firms handling privileged documents, financial advisors with client portfolios, or any business with sensitive data, that's not a nice-to-have. It's a requirement.

Structured Plugin System

Cowork's plugins bundle skills, connectors, slash commands, and sub-agents into a single package. There are 11 official plugins (Sales, Legal, Marketing, Finance, and more), and you can build custom ones for your specific workflows. Everything is file-based: markdown and JSON, no code. ChatGPT has GPTs, but they're more limited in what they can actually do on your behalf.

Where ChatGPT Wins

ChatGPT isn't the wrong choice. For certain workflows, it's the better one. Here's where OpenAI is ahead.

Web Browsing, Built In

ChatGPT browses the web natively. No extensions, no extra configuration. Ask it to research a competitor, pull recent news, or check a website, and it just works. Cowork can do web research through the Claude in Chrome extension or a web search tool, but it requires setup. For quick lookups and live research, ChatGPT is faster to start with.

Image Generation with DALL-E

Need a social media graphic or a product mockup? ChatGPT generates images directly in the conversation. Cowork doesn't generate images. If visual content creation is a core part of your marketing workflow, ChatGPT handles that in one place.

Massive GPT Ecosystem

There are millions of custom GPTs in OpenAI's store. For niche tasks, there's probably a GPT already built for it. Cowork's plugin marketplace is growing but much smaller. The 11 official plugins cover the major business roles, and you can build custom ones, but the number of pre-built options favors ChatGPT.

No Desktop App Required

ChatGPT works entirely in your browser. Nothing to download or install. Cowork requires Claude Desktop on macOS or Windows (and it doesn't support Linux or web-only access). If your team uses Chromebooks or heavily restricted machines, ChatGPT is the easier option.

Advanced Voice Mode

ChatGPT's voice mode lets you have natural spoken conversations, hands-free. Cowork doesn't have voice input. If you think better out loud or need to multitask while giving instructions, that matters.

Who Should Use Which?

The answer depends on what you're actually trying to accomplish day to day.

Choose Cowork If You...

  • Work with local files daily (contracts, reports, spreadsheets, documents)
  • Need recurring automated tasks (weekly reports, daily file sorting, monthly summaries)
  • Handle sensitive client data that can't leave your machine
  • Use business tools like HubSpot, Jira, Salesforce, Slack, or Google Drive
  • Want autonomous multi-step execution, not just answers
  • Want to assign tasks from your phone that execute on your desktop

Choose ChatGPT If You...

  • Primarily need quick web research and information lookups
  • Create images and visual content regularly
  • Want a browser-only tool with no desktop app
  • Use AI mostly for casual questions and brainstorming
  • Prefer voice conversations over typed instructions
  • Need access from Chromebooks or restricted devices

Many teams use both. ChatGPT for quick research and image generation, Cowork for the heavy lifting: file management, recurring deliverables, and multi-tool workflows that connect your CRM to your project tracker to your documents folder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Claude Cowork browse the web like ChatGPT?
Not natively. Cowork can access the web through the Claude in Chrome extension or a web search tool, but it requires extra setup. ChatGPT has built-in web browsing with no configuration needed. If web research is your primary use case, ChatGPT is simpler out of the box.
Is Claude Cowork more private than ChatGPT?
Yes. Cowork runs in an isolated virtual machine on your local computer. Your files and conversation history stay on your machine, not on Anthropic's servers. ChatGPT processes everything in OpenAI's cloud. For businesses handling sensitive client data, contracts, or financial records, that difference matters.
Can I use both Claude Cowork and ChatGPT?
Yes. Many users keep ChatGPT for quick web lookups and image generation, then use Cowork for file-heavy workflows, recurring tasks, and tool integrations. They solve different problems well.
Does ChatGPT have anything like Cowork's plugins and connectors?
ChatGPT has GPTs (custom assistants) and can connect to some tools via custom actions. But Cowork's plugin system is more structured: each plugin bundles skills, connectors, slash commands, and sub-agents into a single package. Cowork also connects to 38+ business tools via OAuth through the MCP protocol, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, Slack, and Google Drive. Read the full plugins guide.
