The real problem with legal work in 2026

Your billable hours are spent on work that doesn't require a law degree.

60%+ of your time goes to document review and admin

Reading contracts, comparing redlines, organizing files, formatting briefs. This is the bulk of legal work, and most of it is pattern matching. You went to law school to practice law, not to manually cross-reference clause libraries in Word documents.

NDA volume is crushing your team

Firms receive dozens of NDAs per week. Each one needs review against your standard terms, risk flagging, and redlining. A junior associate spends 30 to 60 minutes per NDA. Multiply that by 25 NDAs a week, and you've burned an entire associate's capacity on routine triage.

Regulatory compliance changes constantly

New regulations, updated guidelines, revised reporting requirements. Tracking what changed and what it means for your clients is a full-time job. Most firms rely on manual monitoring or expensive third-party services that still require human review of every alert.

Citation formatting eats hours on every brief

Bluebook citations have hundreds of rules. Getting them right is tedious, getting them wrong is embarrassing. Associates spend hours on each brief just making sure every pinpoint cite, id. reference, and supra note follows the correct format. It's mechanical work that still requires careful attention.

How Claude Cowork solves it

These aren't hypothetical demos. These are real workflows running in law firms today.

Contract Markup at the XML Level

Cowork opens .docx files at the XML layer and writes exact tracked changes markup, attributed to the reviewing lawyer's name. Your counterparty gets a standard Word document with proper redlines they can open natively. No copy-paste between tools. No reformatting. Real tracked changes.

NDA Batch Triage

Run /triage-nda all-ndas-in-folder and Cowork processes every NDA in the folder. It reads each document, flags non-standard clauses, produces redlined versions, generates a risk matrix, and organizes everything into a structured report. Twenty NDAs triaged in minutes.

Bluebook Citation Reformatting

Cowork writes code to parse and reformat every citation in a brief against Bluebook rules. Pinpoint cites, id. references, supra notes, short forms. What takes an associate hours is done in seconds. You review the output, not the formatting rules.

Compliance Monitoring

Set up scheduled tasks that monitor regulatory changes relevant to your practice areas. Cowork checks for updates, summarizes what changed, flags which clients are affected, and drafts the documentation you need. You stay current without manual monitoring.

Custom Analytical Frameworks

We encode your firm's specific judgment into custom skills: preferred contract formats, review checklists, writing voice, risk thresholds. Every lawyer on the team can run the same command and get output that matches your firm's standards, not generic boilerplate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Claude Cowork handle contract markup with tracked changes?
Cowork opens .docx files at the XML level and writes exact tracked changes markup, attributed to the reviewing lawyer's name. The result is a standard Word document with redlines your counterparty can open and review natively. No copy-paste, no manual formatting.
Can Claude Cowork batch-process NDAs?
Yes. You can run a single command like /triage-nda on an entire folder of NDAs. Cowork reads each one, flags non-standard clauses, generates a risk matrix, produces redlined versions, and organizes everything into a structured report. A stack of 20 NDAs that would take a junior associate days can be triaged in minutes.
Is client data safe with Claude Cowork?
Cowork runs on Anthropic's infrastructure with enterprise-grade security. Your data is not used to train models. For firms with strict data governance requirements, we configure the setup to work within your existing security policies and connect to approved document management systems like Box or Egnyte.
What legal-specific plugins and skills does Cowork support?
Cowork includes the official /legal plugin from Anthropic, which provides legal research and document analysis capabilities. Beyond that, we build custom skills that encode your firm's specific analytical frameworks, preferred contract formats, review checklists, and writing voice. These skills run as reusable commands your entire team can use.
How long does it take to set up Claude Cowork for a legal team?
Initial setup takes one session. We configure your connectors (Slack, Box, Egnyte, Microsoft 365, Jira), install relevant plugins, and build your first custom skills. Over the following two weeks, we refine the setup based on real usage and train your team on the workflows that matter most.
