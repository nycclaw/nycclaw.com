What a Training Session Covers

We adjust the agenda based on your team's tools and workflows. Here's what we typically cover.

Cowork Fundamentals

How Cowork differs from Chat and Code. Task delegation, monitoring progress, and understanding what Claude can and can't do. We cover the core workflow: describe the outcome, review the plan, let Claude execute.

Plugin Installation and Customization

Installing official Anthropic plugins for your role: Sales, Marketing, Legal, Finance, or any of the 11 available. Then customizing them with your company's terminology, processes, and preferred output formats.

Custom Skill Creation

Building custom skills for your specific workflows. Each repetitive task becomes a SKILL.md file with step-by-step instructions, templates, and examples. We build at least one together during the session.

Connector Setup

Connecting Cowork to your existing tools: Slack, HubSpot, Google Drive, Jira, Salesforce, and 35+ others. OAuth authentication, scoped permissions, and verifying that Claude can read and write to the right places.

Scheduled Task Configuration

Setting up recurring tasks that run daily, weekly, or monthly. Morning email briefings, file organization, revenue reports, competitive monitoring. We configure at least one scheduled task live during the session.

Security Best Practices

Which folders to grant access to (and which to keep off-limits). How to scope connector permissions. Prompt injection risks and how to reduce them. Safe setup for scheduled tasks that run without monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a typical training session?
Most sessions run 60 to 90 minutes. That's enough time to cover Cowork fundamentals, install and configure plugins, set up connectors, and build at least one custom skill. Larger teams or more complex setups may need a second session.
Do you offer remote training or only in-person?
Both. Remote training happens over screen-share (Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams). In-person training is available on-site in New York City, and we travel for larger engagements. The content is identical either way.
What does my team need before the session?
Each team member needs Claude Desktop installed on macOS or Windows, plus a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise). We handle everything else during the session, including plugin installation, connector setup, and instruction configuration.
Can you train just one person or does it have to be a full team?
We train individuals and teams of any size. Solo sessions are common for founders and freelancers. Team sessions work well for departments rolling out Cowork across a group of 5 to 50 people.
What if we need help after the training session?
We offer ongoing support packages for teams that want continued help. This includes plugin updates as Anthropic evolves the platform, new connector setup, and workflow optimization based on how your team actually uses Cowork. We also offer full done-for-you consulting if you'd rather have us build everything.
