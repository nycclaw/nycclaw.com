The real problem with finance ops in 2026

Your team is spending most of its time on work that's mechanical, not analytical.

Month-end close takes 5 to 10 business days

Reconciling bank statements, matching transactions, posting journal entries, generating reports. The same sequence every month. Your team knows the steps by heart, but each one still requires manual effort. The close stretches across a week or more because the volume of repetitive work is too high for the headcount.

Invoice processing is manual and inconsistent

Every vendor sends invoices in a different format. PDFs, emails, scanned documents. Someone on your team has to open each one, extract the vendor name, date, amount, and line items, then enter it all into a spreadsheet or accounting system. Different formats mean different errors. Missing fields get caught late.

Variance analysis reports arrive too late to matter

Budget vs. actual reporting takes hours to compile. Pulling data from multiple sources, calculating variances, writing narrative explanations for significant differences. By the time the report reaches leadership, the month is already over. The analysis that should drive decisions becomes a retrospective exercise.

Audit prep means weeks of documentation gathering

When auditors come knocking, your team scrambles to gather documentation from scattered systems. Supporting schedules live in one place, source documents in another, workpapers in a third. Cross-referencing everything takes weeks. Every audit cycle, someone says "we should organize this better," and every year it's the same scramble.

How Claude Co-Work solves it

These are real workflows running in finance teams today, not concept demos.

Journal Entries and Reconciliation

Co-Work reads bank statements, matches transactions to GL accounts, and flags discrepancies for review. One user built a GnuCash MCP and now does books for a few small businesses in minutes. We configure the same approach for your accounting stack.

Financial Statement Generation

Co-Work connects to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Databricks, pulls the numbers, and generates formatted income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. You review the output instead of building spreadsheets from scratch every period.

Variance Analysis

Budget vs. actual reporting with narrative explanations of significant variances. Co-Work pulls the data, calculates the differences, and writes the commentary. Schedule it monthly so reports are ready on the first business day, not the tenth.

Audit Support

Co-Work compiles documentation, cross-references supporting schedules, and organizes workpapers by audit section. When auditors request a schedule, you run a command instead of digging through folders. The documentation is structured and consistent every time.

Invoice Processing

Co-Work extracts key fields from invoices in any format: vendor name, date, amount, line items. It compiles everything into a master billing spreadsheet, handles varying layouts across vendors, and flags missing fields before they become problems downstream.

Scheduled Stripe Reporting

Automated recurring revenue reports that pull from Stripe on a schedule. MRR, churn, expansion revenue, cohort analysis. The numbers land in your Slack channel or inbox before your morning coffee. No one has to remember to run the report.

Every setup is configured for your team's specific accounting stack, reporting cadence, and internal workflows.
