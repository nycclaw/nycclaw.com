The real problem with e-commerce operations in 2026

You're sitting on a mountain of customer data. Most of it goes unread.

Review volume overwhelms manual analysis

Thousands of reviews across Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and your own storefront. Nobody has time to read them all. Sentiment patterns, recurring complaints, and product issues get buried. You're making decisions about products and marketing without knowing what your customers are actually saying.

Product listings are inconsistent and unoptimized

Titles, descriptions, and bullet points differ across channels. Amazon's algorithm wants different things than Shopify's search. Nobody has time to A/B test copy across hundreds of SKUs. Your listings were written once, maybe twice, and haven't been updated since. Meanwhile, competitors are optimizing weekly.

Competitive pricing requires constant monitoring

Competitors change prices daily. By the time you notice, you've either lost margin by staying too low or lost sales by staying too high. Manual price checks on 50 competitors across 200 SKUs isn't a real strategy. It's a full-time job that nobody has bandwidth for.

Ad creative briefs take hours to write

Every product launch needs creative briefs. Someone pulls product specs, audience data, competitor positioning, and review highlights. Then they write value props, audience hooks, and visual direction. It's largely mechanical work, but it still takes hours per brief. Multiply that by your product catalog and you're buried.

How Claude Cowork solves it

Real workflows your e-commerce team can run today. Not demos. Not mockups.

Review Aggregation and Sentiment Analysis

Cowork pulls reviews from multiple platforms, categorizes by sentiment, product, and issue type. Recurring complaints and praise patterns that you'd never catch manually come through clearly. Weekly scheduled reports show sentiment trends over time. Think of it as Mouthology-style creative intelligence: analyzing your customers' actual language so you can use it in ad copy and product decisions.

Product Listing Optimization

Cowork reads your current listings, compares them against top-performing competitors, and rewrites titles, descriptions, and bullet points optimized for each platform's algorithm. Run it as a batch command across your entire catalog. Amazon, Shopify, Etsy: each gets platform-specific copy, not generic descriptions pasted everywhere.

Competitive Pricing Analysis

A scheduled task monitors competitor pricing via web research. It generates daily or weekly pricing reports showing where you're priced above or below market. Cowork flags products where margin could increase and products where you're losing on price. You get the data you need before your Monday pricing meeting, not after.

Ad Creative Briefs

Give Cowork a product and target audience. It pulls product specs, review sentiment data, competitor positioning, and generates a complete creative brief: value props, audience hooks, USP framing, visual direction suggestions. Mouthology-style creative intelligence means your briefs use the exact phrases your customers already use to describe your products.

Inventory and Fulfillment Reporting

Cowork reads inventory data files, flags low-stock items, calculates reorder points, and generates fulfillment status reports. Scheduled daily to catch issues before they become stockouts. You get a morning briefing on what needs attention, not a surprise email from your 3PL.

Customer Support Triage

Incoming support tickets analyzed, categorized by urgency and issue type, with draft responses generated automatically. Connects to Intercom or HubSpot via connectors. Your support team reviews and sends instead of writing from scratch every time.

Can Cowork optimize listings for Amazon, Shopify, and Etsy at the same time?
Yes. Cowork reads your current listings across platforms, compares them against top-performing competitors on each channel, and rewrites titles, descriptions, and bullet points optimized for each platform's search algorithm. It runs as a batch command across your entire catalog, so you don't need to update each SKU manually.
