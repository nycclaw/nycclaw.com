Co-Work uses more tokens than Chat

This is the most important thing to understand about Claude Co-Work pricing. In regular Chat, Claude reads your message and writes one response. In Co-Work, Claude breaks your task into steps, reads and edits files, searches the web, calls connectors, and checks its own work. Each of those steps consumes tokens.

A single Co-Work task might use 10 to 50 times more tokens than a Chat message, depending on complexity. That's why Pro users often hit their limits within a few tasks. It's not a bug. Multi-step, agentic work is compute-intensive by nature.

Light tasks (fewer tokens)

  • • Rename and organize a folder of files
  • • Draft a single email from notes
  • • Create a document from a template

Heavy tasks (more tokens)

  • • Research a topic across multiple web sources
  • • Analyze a spreadsheet and generate a report
  • • Pull CRM data, cross-reference, and draft outreach

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Claude Co-Work free?
Co-Work is included with every paid Claude plan. There's no separate add-on fee. You get access on Pro ($20/mo or $17/mo annual), Max 5x ($100/mo), Max 20x ($200/mo), Team ($20/seat/mo), and Enterprise (custom pricing). The difference between plans is how much you can use Co-Work before hitting usage limits.
Why does Co-Work use more of my usage allocation than regular Chat?
In regular Chat, Claude reads your message and writes one response. In Co-Work, Claude breaks your task into steps, reads and edits files, searches the web, calls connectors, and checks its own work. Each of those steps uses tokens. A single Co-Work task might use 10 to 50 times more tokens than a Chat message, depending on complexity.
Which Claude plan is best for daily Co-Work use?
Max 5x at $100/mo is the sweet spot for most people who use Co-Work regularly. It gives you 5 times the usage of Pro, which is enough for several multi-step tasks per day. If Co-Work is your primary work tool and you're handing off tasks throughout the day, Max 20x at $200/mo gives you the headroom you need.
Can my team share a single Claude plan for Co-Work?
No. Each person needs their own seat. For teams of 5 to 75, the Team plan at $20/seat/mo is the best option. It includes the Slack connector, self-serve management, and shared plugin marketplaces. For larger organizations, Enterprise plans offer admin controls and org-wide plugin distribution.
Are Co-Work plugins and connectors included in the plan price?
Yes. All 11 official Anthropic plugins, all 38+ connectors, scheduled tasks, and mobile task assignment are included at no extra cost on every plan. You just need an active subscription to the tools you want to connect (like HubSpot, Slack, or Jira). Claude's connectors use OAuth to link your existing accounts.
