The real problem with property management in 2026

You're managing buildings, but most of your day is spent managing documents.

Lease renewals slip through the cracks

With 50+ units, tracking which leases expire when, what terms to offer, and when to start negotiations is a spreadsheet nightmare. One missed renewal means a tenant goes month-to-month or leaves. Either way, you're losing money or scrambling to fill a vacancy you didn't plan for.

Maintenance requests pile up without triage

Tenants submit requests by email, text, portal, and phone call. Sorting by urgency, assigning to the right vendor, and tracking completion is manual and error-prone. A leaking pipe sits in the same queue as a squeaky door. By the time you get to it, the leak has caused real damage.

Financial reporting per property is a manual assembly job

Owners want monthly P&L by property. You're copying data from accounting software, formatting spreadsheets, and writing variance explanations. Every month. For every property. It's the same process each time, but it still takes hours because the data lives in five different places.

Tenant communications are repetitive but can't be generic

Lease violation notices, move-in packets, seasonal reminders, rent increase letters. Each needs to reference the specific unit, tenant, and terms. Templates only get you halfway. You still spend time filling in details, double-checking clause numbers, and making sure notice periods are correct.

How Claude Cowork solves it

Workflows property managers can run right now. Tested in real portfolios.

Lease Analysis and Renewal Tracking

Cowork reads your lease documents, extracts key terms (rent, expiration, renewal options, escalation clauses), and builds a tracking spreadsheet. A scheduled task runs monthly, flags leases expiring in 30, 60, or 90 days, and drafts renewal offers based on market rates and current terms. No more spreadsheet archaeology.

Maintenance Request Triage

Incoming maintenance requests are analyzed and categorized by urgency: emergency, urgent, or routine. Cowork drafts a tenant acknowledgment, assigns to the appropriate vendor based on issue type, and creates tracking records. Connects via Slack, email, or your property management platform.

Tenant Communications

Cowork generates unit-specific and tenant-specific communications: lease violation notices that reference exact clause numbers, rent increase letters with correct notice periods, move-in packets with property-specific rules. Each one pulls from your templates and lease data. No more manual fill-in-the-blank.

Financial Reporting Per Property

Monthly scheduled task: Cowork pulls income and expense data, generates P&L by property, calculates occupancy rates, flags unusual expenses, and writes variance explanations. Owners get a formatted report without you touching a spreadsheet. Same quality every month, zero assembly time.

Vendor Management and Invoice Processing

Cowork processes vendor invoices: extracts line items, matches to work orders, flags discrepancies, and compiles into payment batches. It also tracks vendor performance over time, including response time, completion rates, and cost per job type. You see which vendors deliver and which don't.

Occupancy and Market Rate Analysis

Scheduled weekly: Cowork researches comparable rental rates in your properties' areas, compares to your current rents, and identifies units priced below or above market. You get a summary showing revenue optimization opportunities, so renewal conversations start with data, not guesswork.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Cowork read and extract data from lease documents?
Cowork reads lease documents in PDF, Word, and scanned formats. It extracts key terms including rent amounts, expiration dates, renewal options, escalation clauses, security deposits, and special conditions. The extracted data populates a structured spreadsheet you can sort, filter, and act on. For scanned documents, Cowork uses OCR to read the text before extraction.
Can Cowork connect to my property management software?
Yes. Cowork connects to your existing tools through Slack, Microsoft 365, Google Drive, and email. For property management platforms like AppFolio, Buildium, or Yardi, we configure connectors that pull data from exports or API integrations where available. Your existing workflows don't change. Cowork works alongside them.
How does the maintenance triage workflow handle emergencies?
Cowork categorizes incoming maintenance requests into three tiers: emergency (water leaks, no heat, gas smell, electrical hazards), urgent (broken appliances, HVAC issues, plumbing problems), and routine (cosmetic repairs, minor fixes). Emergency requests trigger immediate notifications to your on-call team and the tenant gets an instant acknowledgment. You define the rules for what counts as each tier.
What financial reports can Cowork generate automatically?
Cowork generates monthly P&L statements by property, occupancy rate reports, rent roll summaries, expense variance analyses, and owner distribution statements. Each report includes written explanations of significant variances, not just numbers. Reports are formatted and delivered on schedule to property owners or your internal team.
How long does setup take for a property management company?
Initial setup takes one session. We configure your connectors, build your first lease extraction and maintenance triage skills, and set up scheduled tasks for reporting. Over the following two weeks, we refine the workflows based on real usage, add additional skills for your specific portfolio, and train your team on every workflow.
