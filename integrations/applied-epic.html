Renewals pile up and slip through

You've got 200 policies renewing next month. Each one needs outreach, a coverage review conversation, maybe a remarket. Epic shows you the list. OpenClaw + Applied Epic Integration

OpenClaw connects to Epic through the REST API. It reads your client and policy data, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Automated renewal outreach

OpenClaw pulls upcoming renewals from Epic's policy API, checks the client's contact info and coverage details, and sends personalized outreach 60 to 90 days before expiration. It logs every touchpoint as an activity in Epic. Your CSRs focus on the clients who respond, not the ones who need reminding.

Activity logging without the typing

OpenClaw creates activity records in Epic automatically. Phone call with a client? It logs it with notes, the topic discussed, and the follow-up needed. Email sent? Logged and attached to the right client record. Your E&O documentation stays complete without anyone spending time on data entry.

Instant new business response

When a new prospect comes in (from your website, a referral, or a lead source), OpenClaw creates the client record in Epic and sends an immediate response. It asks qualifying questions about their coverage needs, timeline, and current carrier. By the time your producer opens Epic the next morning, there's a qualified lead with notes already waiting.

Document and attachment management

OpenClaw uploads documents to the correct client and policy records in Epic through the attachments API. Certificates of insurance, claim photos, signed applications. Everything gets filed where it belongs without someone manually navigating Epic's folder structure for each document.

Cross-sell gap identification

OpenClaw reads your client's policy portfolio through the API and identifies coverage gaps. A commercial client with property and GL but no cyber? OpenClaw flags it, drafts the outreach, and logs the activity. It turns your book of business into a pipeline of warm cross-sell opportunities you'd otherwise miss.

Claims intake and tracking

A client calls about a fender bender at 6 PM. OpenClaw takes the initial details, creates the claim record in Epic, attaches any photos they send, and notifies your claims team. The client gets a fast, professional response. Your team gets a complete claim file when they arrive the next morning.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

P&C Agency

The renewal that doesn't slip

A homeowner's policy is 75 days from renewal. Open enrollment prep on autopilot

It's September. You've got 40 group benefits clients heading into open enrollment. OpenClaw reads each client's policy data from Epic, identifies which plans are renewing, and sends a templated outreach tailored to each group's size and plan type. Commercial Lines

After-hours claim, morning-ready file

A contractor calls at 8:30 PM about water damage at a job site. OpenClaw collects the details (date of loss, location, description, photos) and creates the claim record in Epic attached to the right policy. It sends the client a confirmation with their claim number and next steps. By 8 AM, your claims handler has a complete file with photos, policy details, and client contact info. No voicemail tag, no morning scramble.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Applied Epic?
No. OpenClaw connects to Applied Epic through its REST API and operates it on your behalf. Your Epic database, client records, policies, and workflows stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence on top of the system you already use. Think of it as giving your AMS a brain, not replacing it with a different one.
What Applied Epic features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Applied Epic's full REST API: clients, contacts, policies, activities, documents, attachments, claims, and commission data. If Epic exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Applied Epic integrations are fully running within 5 to 7 days. The API connection and credential setup happen in the first day or two (including approval through the Applied Dev Center). The remaining time is spent configuring OpenClaw for your agency's specific renewal cycles, activity templates, and servicing workflows.
Do I need special API access from Applied Systems?
Yes. You'll need API credentials from the Applied Dev Center, which requires an active Applied Epic subscription with API access enabled. We walk you through the entire credential process during setup. Most agencies get approved within a few business days.
Will this work with my existing Epic workflows and templates?
Yes. OpenClaw works alongside your existing Epic setup. Your activity templates, document categories, policy structures, and workflows all stay intact. OpenClaw reads from and writes to the same system your team uses every day. Nothing gets overwritten or replaced.
