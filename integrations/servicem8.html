ServiceM8 stops where your back office starts.

Field ops run smoothly inside ServiceM8. The problem is everything that happens after.

Job completion doesn't trigger anything useful

A tech marks a job done in ServiceM8. Then what? Someone in the office still needs to create the invoice in Xero or QuickBooks, send a follow-up email to the client, and update the project tracker. ServiceM8 knows the job is finished, but it can't tell your other systems. So your admin team becomes the messenger.

Accounting sync is limited and brittle

ServiceM8 has native Xero and MYOB integrations, but they only handle basic invoice push. Custom line items, split billing, multi-entity accounting, conditional tax rules: none of that works out of the box. If your accounting needs go beyond a simple invoice, you're back to doing it manually or building workarounds in spreadsheets.

Client communication is an afterthought

ServiceM8 sends basic job notifications via email and SMS. That's about it. You can't send a personalized thank-you message after a job, follow up on a quote that went cold three days ago, or ask for a review at the right moment. Your communication with clients lives outside ServiceM8, and there's no bridge between the two.

Reporting requires exporting everything

Want to see revenue by job type this month? Technician performance over the last quarter? Average job value by service area? You'll need to export CSVs from ServiceM8, pull matching data from your accounting software, and build the report yourself. There's no way to get a cross-platform view without leaving ServiceM8 entirely.

What OpenClaw adds on top of ServiceM8

ServiceM8 stays your field operations hub. OpenClaw connects it to everything else and adds the automation ServiceM8 can't handle alone.

Job completion triggers accounting entries

A technician marks a job complete in ServiceM8. OpenClaw picks up the webhook, reads the job details (services performed, materials used, photos attached), and creates the correct invoice in QuickBooks, Xero, or MYOB. Custom line items, tax calculations, and client-specific pricing rules are all handled automatically. No one in the office needs to touch it.

Two-way client sync

Client updates in ServiceM8 flow to your accounting software, your email marketing platform, and your CRM. And it works the other direction too. A client updates their billing address in Xero? OpenClaw syncs it back to ServiceM8. No more maintaining the same contact record in four different systems.

Automated quote follow-ups

You sent a quote in ServiceM8 three days ago. The client hasn't responded. OpenClaw notices the status hasn't changed, drafts a follow-up email referencing the original quote details, and sends it from your business email. If the client approves the quote, OpenClaw schedules the job and notifies your dispatch team. The whole cycle runs without manual intervention.

Cross-platform reporting

OpenClaw pulls job data from ServiceM8, revenue data from your accounting software, and client feedback from your review platform. It compiles everything into a single report: revenue by job type, technician utilization rates, average job value by service area, outstanding invoices. Delivered weekly to your inbox or a shared Google Sheet. No exports required.

Photo and document routing

Technicians take before-and-after photos in ServiceM8. OpenClaw automatically routes those photos to the right place: a Google Drive folder organized by client and job, an email to the property manager, or an attachment on the invoice in your accounting software. Badge form data gets extracted and stored wherever you need it, not just inside ServiceM8.

Recurring job automation

ServiceM8 handles recurring jobs, but everything around them is still manual. OpenClaw adds the missing pieces: automatic reminder emails to clients before their next service, pre-populated job notes from the last visit, invoice generation that matches the recurring schedule, and alerts when a recurring client hasn't booked their next appointment on time.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging, accounting, file storage. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

For ServiceM8 users, OpenClaw is the layer between your field operations and your back office. It listens for job status changes, quote approvals, and new bookings in ServiceM8, then takes action in your accounting software, email, file storage, and reporting tools. The work that used to require someone in the office watching a screen all day now happens on its own.

Common questions

Does this replace ServiceM8?
No. ServiceM8 stays your field operations hub. OpenClaw is the layer that connects ServiceM8 to your accounting software, communication tools, and reporting systems. Your jobs, staff schedules, and client records stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw just makes them talk to everything else.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. Day one covers the ServiceM8 API connection and webhook configuration. The remaining time goes toward mapping your job completion workflows, connecting your accounting and communication tools, and testing the full chain from job status change to external action.
Will this break my existing Xero or MYOB connection?
No. OpenClaw works alongside ServiceM8's native accounting integrations. If you want to keep using the built-in Xero sync for basic invoicing, you can. OpenClaw adds the logic that the native sync can't handle: conditional invoice routing, multi-entity accounting, custom line item mapping, and triggered actions based on payment status.
Can OpenClaw handle multiple staff members and crews?
Yes. OpenClaw reads staff assignments, GPS check-in data, and job completion status for every technician in your ServiceM8 account. You can build automations per staff member, per crew, or across your whole team. Common setups include per-technician performance reports, automated timesheet sync, and crew-specific notification routing.
What happens if a webhook fires while OpenClaw is processing another job?
OpenClaw queues incoming webhooks and processes them in order. Nothing gets dropped. If three jobs complete at the same time, all three will trigger their accounting entries, client notifications, and follow-up actions. Processing typically takes under 60 seconds per event.
