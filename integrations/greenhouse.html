Great ATS. Still losing candidates.

Greenhouse organizes your hiring process. But it can't do the thinking for you.

Scorecards sit empty for days

An interviewer finishes a call at 2 PM. The scorecard is still blank at 5 PM Friday. You send a reminder. Nothing. You send another one Monday. They fill it out from memory, which is now fuzzy. Meanwhile, the candidate is wondering why they haven't heard back. This happens every week with every role.

Candidates go cold in your pipeline

You've got 47 active candidates across six roles. Some are waiting on feedback. Some need a follow-up email. Some applied three days ago and haven't heard a word. Greenhouse shows you the data, but it doesn't send the emails or make the decisions. That's still you, scrolling through pipeline views every morning.

Interview scheduling is a full-time job

Coordinating availability between three interviewers, the candidate, and a conference room across two time zones. You send a Calendly link. They respond with times that don't work. You go back to the interviewers. Two days pass. The candidate takes another offer. The coordination overhead is real, and it costs you hires.

Hiring managers don't live in Greenhouse

Your hiring managers check Greenhouse once a day, maybe. They miss stage transitions, forget to submit feedback, and don't notice when a strong candidate is waiting on their input. You end up being the relay between the system and the people who are supposed to use it.

OpenClaw + Greenhouse Integration

OpenClaw connects to Greenhouse through the Harvest API and webhooks. It watches your pipeline, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Instant candidate acknowledgment

New application hits Greenhouse? OpenClaw picks it up via webhook within seconds. It reads the candidate's resume data, checks the job requirements, and sends a personalized acknowledgment. Not a generic "thanks for applying" template. An actual message that references the role and their background.

Scorecard follow-up on autopilot

Interview ends. OpenClaw checks if the scorecard was submitted. If not, it sends a reminder to the interviewer after an hour. Then again the next morning if it's still blank. When the scorecard comes in, OpenClaw reads it, updates the candidate's activity feed, and flags strong or weak signals for the hiring manager.

Interview scheduling without the back-and-forth

OpenClaw reads interviewer availability, checks the candidate's preferred times, and proposes slots that work for everyone. It creates the scheduled interview in Greenhouse, sends confirmations, and handles reschedule requests. No more five-email chains to book a 30-minute call.

Pipeline stages advance automatically

OpenClaw uses the Harvest API to advance or reject applications based on scorecard results, approval status, and your defined criteria. All four interviewers gave a "strong yes"? Candidate moves to offer stage. Two "no" scores? Rejection email goes out with a thoughtful, non-generic message. No manual dragging required.

Candidate nurture between stages

Candidates sitting in a stage for more than two days get a check-in message. OpenClaw reads where they are in the process, what's pending, and sends an appropriate update. "We're waiting on one more interviewer's feedback, expect to hear from us by Thursday." Specific. Honest. Keeps them engaged instead of ghosting them.

Activity feed stays current without data entry

Every action OpenClaw takes gets logged to the candidate's activity feed in Greenhouse. Notes from conversations, scheduling changes, status updates. When a recruiter opens a candidate profile, the full picture is already there. No digging through email threads or Slack messages to figure out where things stand.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, ATS, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a career page. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks your recruiting team keeps putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Greenhouse?
No. OpenClaw sits on top of Greenhouse and operates it through the Harvest API. Your Greenhouse account, jobs, candidate data, and interview plans stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence to the system your team already uses. Think of it as giving your ATS a brain, not replacing it with a different one.
What Greenhouse features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Greenhouse's full Harvest API: candidates, applications, jobs, offers, scorecards, scheduled interviews, activity feeds, approvals, departments, and offices. It also receives real-time webhooks for events like new applications, stage changes, scorecard submissions, and offer updates. If Greenhouse exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Greenhouse integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The Harvest API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring how OpenClaw handles your specific hiring stages, screening criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's processing applications on its own.
Which Greenhouse plan do I need?
You need a Greenhouse plan that includes Harvest API access. Most Greenhouse customers have this available by default. Your admin can generate API credentials in Configure > Dev Center > API Credential Management. That's the only requirement on the Greenhouse side.
Will OpenClaw interfere with our existing interview plans?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing interview plans, approval flows, and stage configurations. It reads what's happening in your pipeline and takes action based on your rules. Your team keeps using Greenhouse the same way they always have. OpenClaw just handles the follow-ups, scheduling, and stage transitions that used to require manual work.
