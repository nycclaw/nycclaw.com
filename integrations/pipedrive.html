Beautiful pipeline. Incomplete data.

Pipedrive is built for sales teams. But it can't make your team actually use it consistently.

The pipeline goes stale after week one

You set up stages, move deals in on day one, and it looks perfect. Then life happens. Deals sit in "Contacted" for three weeks because nobody updated the stage after the call. Your pipeline view stops reflecting reality, and you're making decisions based on stale data. The board looks the same as it did two Mondays ago.

Activities don't get logged

Your reps had four calls and sent twelve emails today. How many of those are in Pipedrive? Maybe half, if you're lucky. Logging activities feels like busywork when there are more deals to close. So the activity history stays empty, and you can't tell who actually followed up and who just says they did.

Follow-up reminders pile up and get ignored

Pipedrive lets you set activity reminders. That's helpful until you have 47 overdue activities and the notification badge becomes background noise. Reps start ignoring them. The whole point of the reminder system breaks down when everything is overdue. At that point, it's just a list of things nobody's doing.

Deals rot in stages with no one noticing

A deal has been in "Proposal Sent" for 23 days. Is the prospect considering it? Did they ghost? Nobody knows because nobody checked. Pipedrive shows you the deal is there, but it won't tap you on the shoulder and say "this one's dying." By the time someone notices, the prospect moved on weeks ago.

OpenClaw + Pipedrive Integration

OpenClaw connects to Pipedrive through the REST API and webhooks. It watches your pipeline, logs what's happening, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

New leads get a response in seconds

A new person or lead lands in Pipedrive. OpenClaw picks it up through a webhook, reads the available data (source, notes, custom fields), and sends a follow-up email within 60 seconds. Not a generic template. A response based on what's actually in the record.

Activities get logged automatically

OpenClaw monitors your connected email and call data, then creates the corresponding activities in Pipedrive. Call happened? Logged with duration and a note summary. Email sent? Linked to the deal. Your activity timeline stays complete without anyone typing a thing.

Deals move through stages on their own

OpenClaw watches for real signals. A prospect replies positively? The deal moves to "Engaged." A meeting gets scheduled? It moves to "Demo Booked." No more dragging cards manually every morning. The pipeline reflects what's actually happening, updated in real time.

Stalled deals get flagged before they die

OpenClaw tracks how long each deal has been in its current stage and when the last activity happened. If a deal sits too long without movement, it flags it. You get a notification with context: which deal, how long it's been stuck, and what the last interaction was. No more deals rotting in silence.

Notes and fields stay current

After every interaction, OpenClaw writes a note on the deal or person record with what happened and what was discussed. It also updates custom fields (deal value, expected close date, qualification status) based on conversation content. When you open a deal, the context is already there.

Follow-ups that actually happen

Instead of setting a reminder you'll ignore, OpenClaw handles the follow-up itself. A prospect said "check back next week"? OpenClaw schedules the follow-up, sends it when the time comes, and logs the activity. If the prospect responds, it picks up the conversation and updates the deal.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Pipedrive?
No. OpenClaw connects to Pipedrive through its REST API and operates your existing account. Your deals, contacts, pipelines, and data stay in Pipedrive. OpenClaw just works inside it so you don't have to do everything manually. Think of it as giving your CRM an operator, not swapping it for something else.
What Pipedrive features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Pipedrive's full REST API: deals, persons, organizations, activities, notes, leads, products, and pipelines. It also listens to webhooks for real-time updates on every entity change. If Pipedrive exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Pipedrive integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The remaining time goes toward configuring OpenClaw for your specific deal stages, activity types, and follow-up rules. Within a week, it's handling your pipeline on its own.
Which Pipedrive plan do I need?
Any Pipedrive plan with API access works. The Advanced plan and above include full API and webhook access. If you're on the Essential plan, API access is included but some webhook events may be limited. We'll confirm everything fits during the discovery call.
Will OpenClaw mess up my existing pipeline data?
No. OpenClaw follows rules you define. It won't move deals, add notes, or update fields unless you've told it to. During setup, we configure exactly which actions it can take and which stages, fields, and activities it should touch. You stay in control of what gets changed.
