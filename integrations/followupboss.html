Follow Up Boss is great. Your agents are the bottleneck.

The CRM gives you everything. The follow-through still depends on humans showing up.

Agents ignore leads in the system

A new lead comes in from Zillow at 2:30 PM. FUB distributes it to the right agent. The agent is showing a property. Then they're at a closing. Then it's 6 PM and they forgot. The lead sits there, untouched, while your competitor called them back in five minutes. This happens more than anyone wants to admit.

Speed-to-lead still depends on someone picking up

Every study says the same thing: respond within five minutes or your conversion rate drops by 80%. FUB can notify your agents instantly. It can't make them stop what they're doing and call. The gap between "lead assigned" and "lead contacted" is where deals die.

Action plans are templates, not conversations

FUB's action plans send drip emails and text reminders on a schedule. Useful. But when a lead replies asking about a specific neighborhood, the action plan doesn't know what to do. It just keeps sending the next scheduled message. That's not follow-up. That's a mail merge with a timer.

Team leads can't monitor every agent's pipeline

You've got 12 agents with 40 leads each. Somewhere in there, deals are stalling because nobody moved them to the next stage, nobody sent the follow-up, nobody updated the notes. You find out a month later when you run reports. By then, those leads are gone.

OpenClaw + Follow Up Boss Integration

OpenClaw connects to FUB through the REST API and webhooks. It watches your account, responds to leads, and keeps your pipeline moving. Here's what that looks like.

Responds to new leads in seconds

A new person lands in FUB from any source (Zillow, Realtor, your website, a referral). OpenClaw picks it up through a webhook, reads their details and tags, and sends a personalized text or email within 60 seconds. Not a template. A real message based on what they're looking for.

Carries real conversations over text

When a lead texts back with questions about a listing, a neighborhood, or timing, OpenClaw responds like a knowledgeable agent. It qualifies on your criteria (pre-approval, budget, timeline, location) and keeps the conversation going until the lead is ready to meet or you need to step in.

Updates stages and deals automatically

OpenClaw uses FUB's deals and stages API to keep your pipeline current. Lead confirmed they're pre-approved? Stage updated to "Qualified." Went dark for two weeks? Moved to "Needs Follow-up" with a task created for the assigned agent. No more manual card-dragging.

Creates tasks and alerts for your team

OpenClaw writes tasks directly into FUB when human attention is needed. A lead wants to see a property this weekend? Task created for the assigned agent with the address and time preference. A deal hits a price objection OpenClaw can't handle? Task created with full conversation context.

Tags, notes, and custom fields stay current

As OpenClaw learns about a lead through conversation, it writes notes back to the person record in FUB. Tags get added (Buyer, Seller, Pre-approved, Investor). Custom fields get populated. When an agent opens the contact, every detail is already there. Zero data entry.

Revives cold leads on your terms

OpenClaw queries your FUB database for leads that went cold (no activity in 30, 60, 90 days, whatever you set). It re-engages them with a natural check-in message. Not a drip campaign. A specific, context-aware text based on what they originally wanted. Some of those leads come back to life.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Follow Up Boss?
No. OpenClaw connects to Follow Up Boss through the API and works inside it. Your FUB account, your data, your action plans all stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw is the operator that reads what's happening and takes action so you don't have to. Think of it as giving your CRM a brain, not swapping it for a different one.
What Follow Up Boss features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to FUB's REST API: people, deals, tasks, notes, calls, texts, emails, tags, stages, and custom fields. It also listens to webhook events for new leads, stage changes, and incoming messages. If FUB exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Follow Up Boss integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The rest of the time goes into configuring OpenClaw for your specific qualifying criteria, deal stages, market, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Will this work with my existing action plans?
Yes. OpenClaw works alongside your existing action plans and automations. It doesn't disable or overwrite anything you've already built. Your drip sequences keep running. OpenClaw adds real-time, conversational follow-up on top of what's already there. They complement each other.
Do my agents need to learn anything new?
No. OpenClaw works inside Follow Up Boss, so your agents use the same interface they already know. They'll just notice that leads are being responded to faster, notes are more detailed, and stages are up to date. The only change is that their CRM starts doing more of the work for them.
