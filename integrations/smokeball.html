Auto-time tracking is great. It's also not enough.

Smokeball handles the clock. But a law practice has a lot more moving parts than billable hours.

Intake leads wait while you're in court

A potential client fills out your contact form at 2 PM. You're in a deposition until 4:30. By then they've called two other firms and hired one of them. Smokeball logged your deposition time perfectly. It did nothing about the lead that came in during it.

Document assembly waits for someone to start it

Smokeball's document automation is powerful. Templates, form assembly, merge fields from matter data. But someone still has to open the matter, pick the right template, and initiate the process. For routine documents (engagement letters, demand letters, court filings), that's time you shouldn't need to spend.

Clients call to ask "what's happening?"

You filed a motion last Tuesday. Your client doesn't know that unless someone tells them. Most firms don't send case status updates proactively because it takes time nobody has. So clients call. Or email. Or leave a review about poor communication. All preventable.

Post-consultation follow-ups don't happen

You had a great consultation. The prospective client seemed interested. You told yourself you'd follow up tomorrow. That was a week ago. Smokeball tracked the 45 minutes you spent on the call. It didn't send the follow-up email with the retainer agreement.

OpenClaw + Smokeball Integration

OpenClaw connects to Smokeball through the API and handles the work that falls between the cracks. Here's what that looks like.

Responds to intake leads in seconds

New inquiry comes in through your website form, referral partner, or directory listing. OpenClaw picks it up, reads the details, and sends a personalized response within 60 seconds. It asks qualifying questions (practice area, timeline, prior representation) and keeps the conversation going until you're available.

Initiates document assembly from matter data

When a new matter reaches a certain stage, OpenClaw pulls the contact and matter details from Smokeball and kicks off the right document template. Engagement letters on intake. Demand letters when liability is established. It uses the data already in your system instead of waiting for someone to start the process manually.

Sends case status updates to clients

OpenClaw monitors matter activity in Smokeball. When a filing is completed, a task is marked done, or a key date passes, it sends your client a plain-language update. "Your motion was filed with the court on Tuesday. We expect a response within 14 days." No client calls asking for updates. No staff time spent on status emails.

Follows up after every consultation

After a consultation ends, OpenClaw sends a follow-up email the same day. It references what was discussed, attaches the retainer agreement if appropriate, and checks in again three days later if the prospect hasn't responded. Consistent follow-up without you remembering to do it.

Creates tasks and updates matter status

OpenClaw creates tasks in Smokeball based on what's happening in a matter. Deadline approaching? Task created for the responsible attorney. New document received from opposing counsel? Task to review it. Matter status fields stay current without anyone manually updating them.

Manages scheduling and reminders

OpenClaw reads your calendar and Smokeball deadlines, then handles the coordination. It sends appointment reminders to clients, reschedules when conflicts arise, and makes sure nothing falls off the calendar. Court dates, filing deadlines, client meetings. All tracked, all communicated.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, practice management, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Smokeball?
No. OpenClaw connects to Smokeball through the API and works alongside it. Your matters, documents, time entries, and billing all stay in Smokeball. OpenClaw handles the communication, follow-ups, and task initiation that Smokeball doesn't do on its own. Think of it as a second layer that covers the gaps.
What Smokeball features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Smokeball's API for matters, contacts, tasks, documents, time entries, and billing data. It can read matter details, create tasks, pull contact information, and use document templates. Anything Smokeball exposes through its API is available to OpenClaw.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Smokeball integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection happens on day one. The remaining time goes toward configuring OpenClaw for your practice areas, intake criteria, document templates, and communication preferences. Within a week, it's handling intake and follow-ups on its own.
Which Smokeball plan do I need?
You need a Smokeball plan that includes API access. Check with Smokeball support to confirm your plan includes it. That's the only technical requirement on their side. Everything else (OpenClaw, configuration, the integration itself) is handled during setup.
Will OpenClaw interfere with Smokeball's automatic time tracking?
Not at all. Smokeball's background time tracking continues exactly as it does now. OpenClaw operates through the API, handling intake, communication, and task management. The two systems work in parallel without any conflict. Smokeball tracks your time; OpenClaw handles everything around it.
