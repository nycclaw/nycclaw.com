Clay is powerful. You're still doing the work.

The platform can pull data from everywhere. But getting it to run without you? That's a different story.

Building tables takes hours you don't have

Every new campaign needs a new Clay table. Import a list, configure enrichment columns, set up waterfall logic for each field, test the results, fix the ones that didn't resolve. By the time your table is ready, you've spent half a day on setup instead of selling.

Enrichment gaps need babysitting

Waterfall enrichment is brilliant in theory. Provider A misses the email, so it falls to Provider B, then C. But when 30% of your records still have gaps after the waterfall runs, someone has to decide what to do next. Try a different provider? Use the AI research agent? Skip the record? That someone is you, row by row.

Outreach sequences need constant feeding

You enrich a batch of leads on Monday. By Wednesday, you need another batch. Each one needs to be filtered, scored, and pushed to your outreach tool. The enrichment is automated. Everything around it still isn't. So your pipeline dries up every time you get busy with actual client work.

The CRM sync breaks and nobody notices

Clay pushes enriched records to Salesforce or HubSpot. Until a field mapping changes, a required field goes blank, or a duplicate check fails. Records pile up in Clay but never make it to your CRM. Your sales team works off stale data and doesn't know why. You find out two weeks later.

OpenClaw + Clay Integration

OpenClaw connects to Clay through the API and manages your enrichment, research, and outreach workflows. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Automated waterfall enrichment

OpenClaw triggers Clay's waterfall enrichment on new leads as they come in. It monitors which providers return data, handles fallback logic when gaps remain, and flags records that need manual review. You get fully enriched records without touching the table.

AI research on every prospect

Clay's AI research agent can find information that structured providers miss. OpenClaw uses it to answer specific questions about each prospect: recent funding rounds, job changes, tech stack, company size. The research runs in the background. Results land in your table columns automatically.

Outreach sequences that stay full

OpenClaw keeps your outreach pipeline fed. It filters enriched leads by your scoring criteria, personalizes messaging using Clay's enrichment data, and pushes qualified records to your sequencing tool. New leads enter your sequences daily without you importing a single CSV.

CRM sync that actually stays in sync

OpenClaw monitors the Clay-to-CRM pipeline. When a push fails (bad field mapping, duplicate record, missing required field), it catches the error, fixes what it can, and alerts you on the rest. Your CRM always reflects what Clay knows.

Lead scoring from enrichment data

OpenClaw reads the enrichment results and scores each lead based on your criteria. Company size, funding stage, tech stack, job title seniority. High-scoring leads go straight to outreach. Low-scoring ones get tagged and parked. No manual review of spreadsheets.

Scheduled list building

Tell OpenClaw your ideal customer profile once. It builds new prospect lists on a schedule, pulls them into Clay, runs enrichment, scores the results, and pushes winners to your outreach. Monday morning, fresh leads are already in your sequences. You didn't do a thing.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Clay?
No. OpenClaw connects to Clay through its API and operates it for you. Your Clay tables, enrichment providers, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a decision-making layer so your enrichment and outreach workflows run without manual intervention.
What Clay features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw works with Clay's full platform: table creation and management, waterfall enrichment across 100+ data providers, the AI research agent, HTTP API integrations, webhook triggers, CRM push, and outreach sequence management. If Clay exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Clay integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and initial table setup happen on day one. The remaining time goes into configuring waterfall enrichment logic, lead scoring criteria, and outreach templates specific to your business and ICP.
Do I need a specific Clay plan?
You'll need a Clay plan with API access and enough enrichment credits for your volume. The Pro plan and above include API access and higher credit allocations. We'll review your expected volume during the discovery call and recommend the right tier.
Can OpenClaw work with my existing CRM alongside Clay?
Yes. Clay already integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs. OpenClaw can manage the entire flow: enrich leads in Clay, push qualified records to your CRM, and trigger outreach sequences. The data moves between systems automatically, and OpenClaw monitors the sync to catch errors before they pile up.
