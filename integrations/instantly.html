The outreach is automated. The follow-up isn't.

Instantly handles sending. But the part that actually converts leads? That's still on you.

Replies pile up faster than you can read them

You're sending thousands of emails a month. Even a 3% reply rate means dozens of responses every day. Some are interested. Some are "not now." Some are angry. You need to sort through all of them, figure out which ones matter, and respond before the warm ones go cold. That backlog grows every hour you're not in your inbox.

Interested leads don't get followed up on

A prospect replies "Tell me more about pricing." That's a buying signal. But you were in a meeting, or it was Friday afternoon, or it just got buried under 40 other replies. By Monday, they've already talked to someone else. The lead was there. The follow-up wasn't.

You can't tell what's working without digging

Instantly gives you open rates and reply rates. But understanding which campaigns are generating qualified leads versus tire-kickers requires reading through replies, cross-referencing with your CRM, and making judgment calls. That analysis happens once a week if you're lucky. Usually it doesn't happen at all.

Scaling outreach means scaling your inbox time

Every new campaign you launch means more replies to manage. More email accounts to monitor. More leads to sort. Instantly makes it easy to send more, but it doesn't make it easier to handle what comes back. Your outreach scales linearly. Your time doesn't.

OpenClaw + Instantly Integration

OpenClaw connects to Instantly through the V2 API and webhooks. It reads replies, qualifies leads, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Reads and classifies every reply

When a prospect replies to your Instantly campaign, OpenClaw picks it up through a webhook. It reads the message and classifies it: interested, not interested, out of office, wrong person, asking a question. No manual sorting. The interested ones get flagged immediately.

Responds to warm leads in minutes

A prospect says "This sounds interesting, can you send more info?" OpenClaw drafts a reply based on the campaign context, your offering, and the prospect's company. It sends it through Instantly's email API so it stays in the same thread. The prospect gets a thoughtful reply within minutes, not hours.

Tags and labels leads automatically

OpenClaw uses Instantly's lead labels and custom tags to organize your pipeline. Interested leads get labeled. Objections get categorized. "Not now" replies get scheduled for re-engagement next quarter. When you open Instantly, everything is already sorted.

Monitors campaign health in real time

OpenClaw pulls analytics from the API and watches for problems. Bounce rate spiking on one email account? It pauses that account and alerts you. Open rates dropping on a campaign? It flags the issue before you burn through your lead list. Deliverability problems get caught early, not after the damage is done.

Syncs qualified leads to your CRM

When OpenClaw identifies a qualified lead, it pushes the contact data to your CRM through Instantly's CRM actions API. Lead name, company, email, conversation summary, and qualification notes. Your sales team sees a ready-to-close lead with full context, not a raw email address.

Manages your block list and email verification

Prospects who unsubscribe or ask to be removed get added to the block list automatically. OpenClaw also triggers email verification for new lead lists before they go into campaigns, catching invalid addresses before they hurt your sender reputation.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Instantly?
No. OpenClaw sits on top of Instantly and operates it through the API. Your campaigns, email accounts, lead lists, and analytics stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence to the platform you already use. Think of it as giving your cold outreach a brain, not swapping the tool.
What Instantly features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Instantly's full V2 API: campaigns, leads, email accounts, lead lists, analytics, webhooks, custom tags, block lists, lead labels, email verification, inbox placement tests, subsequences, and CRM actions. Over 30 endpoint categories. If Instantly exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Instantly integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The rest is configuring the AI for your specific outreach style, qualifying criteria, and reply templates. Within a week, it's handling replies on its own.
Which Instantly plan do I need?
You need a Growth plan or higher, which includes API access. Growth starts at $30 per month. If you're on the free or Starter plan, you'll need to upgrade. The API key is generated in your Instantly workspace settings with scoped permissions, so you control exactly what OpenClaw can access.
Will OpenClaw mess up my sending reputation?
No. OpenClaw respects your existing sending limits, warmup schedules, and account rotation settings. It works within the guardrails you've already configured in Instantly. It doesn't send emails outside your campaign structure or bypass deliverability safeguards. If anything, it helps your reputation by catching bounces and managing your block list faster.
