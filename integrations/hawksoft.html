Renewals show up, but nobody follows up

HawkSoft tells you which policies are coming up for renewal. That's helpful. But then you need to pull up each client, check their coverage, draft a personalized email, and send it. Multiply that by 30 or 40 renewals a month. Some get done. Most get a generic template. A few slip through entirely.

New leads sit while you service existing clients

A referral comes in or someone fills out a quote request. You see it in HawkSoft. But you're on the phone with a client about a claim, and by the time you circle back to the new lead it's been four hours. Or a day. The prospect already called the agency down the street.

Log notes pile up but nobody reads them

Your team dutifully logs every call, every email, every policy change. That's the right thing to do. But when a client calls in, nobody has time to read through 18 months of log notes before picking up. So you ask the client to repeat things they've already told you. It doesn't feel great for either side.

Cross-sell opportunities are invisible

You have a client with an auto policy and no umbrella. Another with a home policy but no flood coverage in a flood zone. HawkSoft has all the data to spot these gaps. But running reports, filtering results, and then drafting outreach for each one? That's a project nobody starts because there's always something more urgent.

OpenClaw + HawkSoft Integration

OpenClaw connects to HawkSoft through the Partner API. It reads your client and policy data, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Renewal outreach on autopilot

OpenClaw monitors upcoming renewals through HawkSoft's policy data. Sixty days out, it pulls the client's coverage details, drafts a personalized email referencing their specific policies, and sends it. Not a mass blast. An individual message that sounds like it came from their account manager.

New leads get a response in minutes

When a new prospect lands in HawkSoft, OpenClaw picks it up right away. It reads the contact details, sends a personalized email or text within minutes, and starts qualifying them on coverage needs. You're not losing leads to response time anymore.

Log notes that write themselves

Every action OpenClaw takes gets written back to HawkSoft as a log note through the two-way API. Emails sent, conversations had, follow-ups scheduled. When you open a client record, the full history is already there. No manual data entry required.

Coverage gap detection

OpenClaw scans your book of business through HawkSoft's policy data and identifies clients missing obvious coverage. Home without umbrella. Auto without roadside. Commercial property without business interruption. Then it drafts targeted outreach for each one, ready for your review or sent automatically.

Client context at your fingertips

Before a client call, OpenClaw pulls their full HawkSoft record and gives you a summary: active policies, recent claims, last conversation, upcoming renewal dates, and any open tasks. Two sentences instead of scrolling through months of log notes.

Task and activity management

OpenClaw creates and tracks tasks in HawkSoft based on what's actually happening. Client didn't respond to a renewal email? Task created for a phone follow-up in three days. New policy bound? Task created for a 30-day check-in. Your team sees a clean task list every morning with real priorities.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace HawkSoft?
No. OpenClaw connects to HawkSoft through the Partner API and operates it for you. Your HawkSoft data, policies, and client records stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence on top of the system you already use. Think of it as giving your agency management system a brain, not replacing it with a different one.
What HawkSoft features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to HawkSoft's Partner API v3: client records, policy data, activities, log notes, attachments, and tasks. It can read contact and policy information, write log notes back to client records, and track activities across your book of business. If HawkSoft exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can work with it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most HawkSoft integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your agency's specific workflows, renewal timelines, and communication preferences. Within a week, it's handling follow-ups on its own.
Do I need a specific HawkSoft plan?
You need a HawkSoft account with Partner API access enabled. Most current HawkSoft plans include this through the HawkSoft Marketplace. If you're unsure, check your Marketplace settings or contact HawkSoft support to confirm API access is turned on. That's the only requirement on the HawkSoft side.
Can OpenClaw work alongside my existing HawkSoft integrations?
Yes. Many agencies already use HawkSoft API partners for rating, VoIP, or marketing automation. OpenClaw works alongside those integrations without interfering. It reads what's in HawkSoft, makes decisions, and writes back through the same Partner API your other tools use. Your existing tech stack stays intact.
