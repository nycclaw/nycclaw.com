OpenClaw + Buildium Integration

OpenClaw connects to Buildium through the Open API and webhooks. It reads your property data, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Instant maintenance triage

When a tenant submits a maintenance request, OpenClaw picks it up through a webhook within seconds. It reads the description, assesses urgency (burst pipe vs. squeaky door), responds to the tenant with a realistic timeline, and flags emergencies for your immediate attention. No more requests sitting unread overnight.

Automated rent follow-ups

OpenClaw checks outstanding balances through Buildium's ownership account transactions API. On day 3, it sends a friendly reminder. On day 7, a firmer notice. On day 14, a formal communication. Each message is tailored to the tenant's history and your policies. You set the rules once.

Lease renewal tracking

OpenClaw monitors lease expiration dates across your portfolio via the leases API. Sixty days out, it starts the renewal conversation with the tenant. It can present new terms, answer questions about rent adjustments, and flag tenants who seem likely to leave so you can plan ahead.

Property records stay current

OpenClaw writes notes, updates unit details, and logs communication history directly in Buildium through the API. When you open a tenant or property record, everything is already there. Move-in notes, maintenance history, payment conversations. No data entry on your end.

Accounting entries without the busywork

Buildium's API exposes the full general ledger, bank accounts, bills, and transaction history. OpenClaw can create charges, log payments, record vendor bills, and reconcile transactions. When a maintenance job is completed, it logs the cost against the right property automatically.

HOA and association management

For association managers, OpenClaw handles owner communications, processes architectural requests, tracks board member changes, and monitors ownership account balances. It reads the association data through dedicated API endpoints and acts on it the same way it does for rental properties.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

What this looks like in practice

Three real scenarios where OpenClaw and Buildium work together.

Property Manager

The midnight pipe burst

A tenant submits a maintenance request at 11:43 PM: "Water is pouring from under the kitchen sink." OpenClaw picks it up through a Buildium webhook, recognizes "water pouring" as an emergency, texts the tenant within 30 seconds with instructions to shut off the valve under the sink, and alerts your on-call plumber. It creates notes in the Buildium property record and sends you a summary. You wake up to a resolved situation, not a crisis.

Portfolio Owner

Rent collection across 40 units

It's the 5th. OpenClaw pulls outstanding balances from Buildium's transactions API and finds 8 tenants who haven't paid. It checks each tenant's payment history. Three are usually a few days late (gentle reminder). Two are first-time late (friendly check-in). Three are chronic (formal notice with late fee reference). Each gets the right message. You review the summary over coffee instead of spending an hour writing individual texts.

HOA Manager

Architectural request processing

An owner submits an architectural request through Buildium to replace their front door. OpenClaw reads the request details via the API, checks it against your association's guidelines, and sends the owner an acknowledgment with the expected review timeline. It flags the request for the next board meeting and adds the relevant details to the agenda notes. The owner feels heard. You didn't lift a finger.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Buildium?
No. OpenClaw connects to Buildium through its Open API and operates it for you. Your Buildium account, data, and workflows stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence so you spend less time clicking around inside the platform and more time on the work that actually grows your portfolio.
What Buildium features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Buildium's full Open API: rental properties, units, tenants, leases, maintenance requests, ownership accounts, the general ledger, bank accounts, bills, transactions, associations, board members, architectural requests, and more. Buildium also supports webhooks, so OpenClaw gets real-time notifications when data changes. Over 200 API endpoints are available.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Buildium integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhook subscriptions go live on day one. The rest is configuring the AI for your specific properties, escalation rules, communication style, and accounting preferences. Within a week, it's handling operations on its own.
Which Buildium plan do I need?
You need Buildium's Premium plan or higher. The Essential and Growth plans don't include Open API access. If you're not sure which plan you're on, check your Buildium account settings or contact their support team. The Premium plan is the only requirement on the Buildium side.
Can OpenClaw handle multiple properties and associations?
Yes. The Buildium API gives OpenClaw access to every property, unit, and association in your account. It manages communications and operations across your entire portfolio from a single integration. Whether you have 10 units or 500, the setup is the same.
