OpenClaw + Kareo (Tebra) Integration

OpenClaw connects to Tebra through the SOAP API, polls for changes on a regular interval, and takes action. Here's what that looks like for your practice.

Denied claims, caught and flagged fast

OpenClaw polls Tebra's claims data every few minutes. When a denial comes back, it reads the reason code, identifies the fix (missing info, coding error, eligibility issue), and alerts your billing team with specific next steps. No more claims sitting unnoticed for days.

Patient records stay clean automatically

New patient comes in? OpenClaw reads the intake data from Tebra, checks for duplicate records, verifies that required fields are complete, and flags anything missing before the patient's first appointment. Your staff stops playing detective with incomplete charts.

Appointment follow-up that actually happens

OpenClaw monitors your Tebra appointment schedule. When a patient hasn't confirmed, it sends a follow-up. When someone no-shows, it flags the opening and can reach out to waitlisted patients. When a recall is due, it handles the outreach. All based on your practice's actual schedule data.

Payment posting and AR tracking

OpenClaw reads payment data from Tebra and tracks your accounts receivable. It identifies which balances are aging past 30, 60, 90 days, and generates prioritized follow-up lists for your billing team. The numbers that matter are always current, without anyone pulling a report manually.

Insurance eligibility verification

Before each appointment, OpenClaw can pull patient insurance data from Tebra and cross-reference it. If coverage has lapsed or the plan has changed, your front desk knows before the patient walks in. No more surprise denials after services are rendered.

Daily practice briefings

Every morning, OpenClaw pulls data from Tebra and sends you a summary: today's appointments, outstanding claims, overdue balances, patients who need follow-up. One message with everything you need to know. No logging in and clicking through five different reports.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

What this looks like in practice

Three real scenarios where OpenClaw and Tebra work together.

Primary Care Practice

The denied claim that gets fixed in hours

A payer denies a claim for an E&M visit because the modifier was wrong. OpenClaw catches it within minutes through the API, identifies the correct modifier based on the encounter data, and alerts your biller with the specific fix. The corrected claim goes out the same day instead of sitting in a queue for two weeks. That's $280 back in your revenue cycle, times however many denials you get per month.

Mental Health Practice

No-show recovery that works

A therapy patient misses their Tuesday 2 PM session. OpenClaw sees the no-show in Tebra's appointment data within minutes. It sends a non-judgmental follow-up message asking if they'd like to reschedule. If the patient responds, OpenClaw checks available slots in Tebra and offers three options. The appointment gets rebooked without your receptionist making a single call.

Multi-Provider Clinic

Morning briefing for every provider

At 7 AM, each provider gets a personalized summary from OpenClaw: their schedule for the day, which patients have outstanding balances, any insurance flags from eligibility checks, and notes on patients who were recently seen by other providers in the practice. All pulled directly from Tebra. No one has to log in and click through reports before their first patient.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Kareo (Tebra)?
No. OpenClaw connects to Tebra through its SOAP API and operates it for you. Your Tebra account, patient records, and billing data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw handles the repetitive tasks so your staff can focus on patients instead of data entry.
What Kareo/Tebra features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Tebra's full SOAP API: patients, appointments, encounters, claims, charges, payments, providers, insurance companies, and practice data. It can read records, create new entries, and update existing ones across your entire practice management system.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Tebra integrations are running within 5 to 7 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your practice's specific workflows, billing rules, and patient communication preferences. By the end of the first week, it's handling tasks on its own.
Is this HIPAA compliant?
OpenClaw runs on your own infrastructure, so patient data never leaves your control. Tebra itself is HIPAA compliant. Since OpenClaw operates through Tebra's authenticated API with your credentials, data stays within your existing security perimeter. We configure everything with PHI handling in mind.
Do I need a specific Tebra plan?
You need a Tebra plan that includes API access. Your system administrator will need to generate a customer key and set the appropriate security permissions. Most Tebra Practice Management plans include this capability. We'll verify during the discovery call so there are no surprises.
