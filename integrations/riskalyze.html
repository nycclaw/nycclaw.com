OpenClaw + Riskalyze (Nitrogen) Integration

OpenClaw connects to Nitrogen through the API, reads your client and portfolio data, and acts on it. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Instant questionnaire follow-up

When a prospect completes their risk questionnaire, OpenClaw detects it through the API, reads their Risk Number, and sends a personalized follow-up within minutes. "Thanks for completing the assessment, Sarah. Your Risk Number came in at 54, which tells us you're comfortable with moderate growth. Let's talk about what that means for your portfolio." Specific. Timely. Not a template.

Portfolio drift alerts with context

OpenClaw monitors your client portfolios through the Nitrogen API and checks them against their Risk Numbers and 95% Historical Range. When a portfolio drifts outside tolerance, OpenClaw sends you a summary with the specific holdings causing the drift and a suggested talking point for the client. You reach out before they reach out to you.

Proposal workflow automation

After a discovery meeting, OpenClaw reads the client's Risk Number and current portfolio from Nitrogen, compares it against your model portfolios, and drafts an email to the client with a summary of findings. You review it, hit send, and skip the 30 minutes of manual proposal prep. The data was already there. OpenClaw just assembled it.

CRM sync from Nitrogen data

OpenClaw reads Risk Numbers, questionnaire completion dates, and portfolio analytics from Nitrogen, then writes that data to your CRM (Redtail, Wealthbox, or others). When a client's Risk Number changes from 62 to 45 after a life event, your CRM reflects it within minutes. No manual entry. No stale data in your meeting prep.

Review meeting prep on autopilot

Before each client review, OpenClaw pulls the client's current Risk Number, portfolio holdings, and any drift data from Nitrogen. It generates a one-page meeting brief with talking points: where the portfolio sits relative to the 95% Historical Range, any allocation changes since last review, and whether a new risk questionnaire is overdue. Delivered to your inbox before you walk into the meeting.

Questionnaire reminders that work

OpenClaw checks which clients haven't completed their annual risk questionnaire. Instead of a generic batch email, it sends personalized reminders that reference the client's last assessment date and current portfolio context. "Hi Mark, your last risk assessment was 14 months ago, and the market has shifted since then. Takes about 5 minutes." Completion rates go up when messages feel personal.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Riskalyze (Nitrogen)?
No. OpenClaw connects to Nitrogen through its API and operates alongside it. Your Risk Numbers, client data, proposals, and questionnaire results stay in Nitrogen. OpenClaw reads that data and acts on it, handling the follow-up and monitoring work that currently falls on you.
What Nitrogen features can OpenClaw access through the API?
OpenClaw connects to the Nitrogen API using OAuth 2.0 authorization. It can read client records, Risk Number scores, portfolio holdings, questionnaire completion status, and proposal data. It uses this information to trigger follow-ups, flag portfolio drift, and keep your CRM updated with the latest risk assessment data.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Riskalyze integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your specific client segments, follow-up sequences, drift thresholds, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling the operational work on its own.
Does this work with my CRM too?
Yes. Most advisory practices use Nitrogen alongside a CRM like Redtail or Wealthbox. OpenClaw can connect to both systems, pulling risk data from Nitrogen and syncing it to your CRM automatically. We also offer dedicated integrations for Redtail and Wealthbox.
Which Nitrogen plan do I need?
You need a Nitrogen plan that includes API access. The platform offers API connectivity for firms and enterprise users. If you're on a standard advisor plan, check with Nitrogen to confirm API access is available for your account. We can help you figure this out on the discovery call.
