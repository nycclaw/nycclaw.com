Eaglesoft knows everything. It does nothing about it.

Your practice management system holds all the data. The problem is what happens (or doesn't) after the data gets entered.

Recall lists pile up with no one to call them

Eaglesoft's InContact generates the overdue recall list every day. It's accurate. It's detailed. And it sits there while your front desk handles check-ins, insurance verifications, and the phone that won't stop ringing. By the time someone gets to the recall list, it's 3 PM and half the patients are unreachable. Those hygiene appointments stay empty.

Treatment plans get presented, then forgotten

A patient needs a crown. You present the treatment plan in Eaglesoft. They say "let me think about it" and walk out. Three weeks later, nobody has followed up. The treatment plan is in the system. The patient's contact info is in the system. But Eaglesoft won't send a text asking if they're ready to schedule. That's still someone's job.

Cancellations create holes nobody fills

A patient cancels their 2 PM cleaning. Eaglesoft's QuickFill feature has a list of patients who want earlier appointments. But someone still has to work through that list, make the calls, and confirm the replacement. If the cancellation comes in at 7 AM, you have seven hours to fill the slot. If it comes at noon, you have two. Most of the time, the chair sits empty.

MoneyFinder spots revenue you never collect

Eaglesoft's MoneyFinder tool identifies unscheduled treatment, outstanding claims, and patients with remaining insurance benefits. It's telling you exactly where the money is. But acting on that information requires a human to review each item, contact the patient or insurance company, and follow through. The tool finds it. Nobody picks it up.

OpenClaw + Eaglesoft Integration

Recall outreach on autopilot

OpenClaw reads Eaglesoft's InContact recall lists and reaches out to overdue patients by text or email. It doesn't send a generic "time for your cleaning" message. New patient intake handled before they arrive

When a new patient books through your website or calls in, OpenClaw sends intake forms, collects insurance information, and enters it into Eaglesoft before the appointment. The patient shows up and everything is already in the system. Your front desk isn't scrambling with clipboards and data entry while the waiting room fills up.

Practice analytics turned into action

Eaglesoft's SnapShot tool shows production trends and practice performance. OpenClaw reads those analytics and acts on them. What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Eaglesoft?
No. OpenClaw connects to Eaglesoft through Patterson's API Tools and operates alongside it. Your patient records, schedules, and billing data stay in Eaglesoft. OpenClaw reads that data and takes action on it, like sending follow-ups or flagging overdue treatment plans. It adds a layer of intelligence to the system you already run your practice on.
What Eaglesoft data can OpenClaw access?
Through Patterson's API Tools and the PIC platform, OpenClaw can access patient demographics, appointment schedules, treatment plans, insurance and billing records, clinical notes via SmartNote, and practice analytics from SnapShot. It can also coordinate with Eaglesoft's 55+ authorized integrations like Vyne Trellis, RevenueWell, and Weave for patient communication.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Eaglesoft integrations are running within 5 to 7 days. The API Tools connection and initial data mapping happen in the first two days. The remaining time goes toward configuring the AI for your specific recall protocols, follow-up timing, and communication preferences. Within two weeks, it's fully autonomous.
Which version of Eaglesoft do I need?
Eaglesoft version 20.1 or higher is required for the API Tools integration. If you're on an older version, Patterson can help with the upgrade. An active Eaglesoft Service Club membership includes free upgrades and unlimited U.S.-based support, so there's typically no extra cost to get current.
Is patient data safe with this integration?
OpenClaw connects through Patterson's authorized integration channels, the same secure pathways used by all 55+ approved Eaglesoft partners. Patient data stays in your Eaglesoft database. OpenClaw reads what it needs, takes action, and doesn't store PHI outside your practice systems. We follow HIPAA requirements for all dental integrations.
