Buildertrend manages the build. Everything else is manual.

Your construction data lives in Buildertrend. Your financial data lives somewhere else. And you're the bridge between them.

Estimates and budgets live in Buildertrend, but your books live in QuickBooks

You build detailed estimates in Buildertrend with line items, cost codes, and markups. Then you re-enter those same numbers in QuickBooks for invoicing. Every draw schedule update means manual double-entry between systems. It's slow, error-prone, and gets worse the more projects you're running.

Change orders create ripple effects nobody tracks

A homeowner approves a $12,000 kitchen upgrade in Buildertrend. The price changes in BT, but your accounting system doesn't know about it. The draw schedule doesn't update. The lender doesn't get notified. Someone has to manually chase each of those updates across three different systems.

Client selections are scattered across emails and portals

Clients make selections in Buildertrend's portal, but that data doesn't automatically flow to purchasing or scheduling. Your project manager still has to manually create purchase orders for the selected fixtures and update the schedule to account for lead times. Selection data stays trapped in one system.

Daily logs and time clock data don't reach payroll

Your crew clocks in and out through Buildertrend. Daily logs get filed with photos and notes. But when it's time to run payroll, someone exports hours from BT and manually enters them into Gusto, ADP, or QuickBooks Payroll. Field data stays in Buildertrend while your back office runs on a completely separate system.

What OpenClaw adds on top of Buildertrend

Buildertrend stays your construction management platform. OpenClaw connects it to everything else your business runs on.

Estimate-to-invoice sync

Buildertrend estimates and draw schedules sync directly to QuickBooks or Xero for invoicing. When you update a line item or approve a draw in BT, the corresponding invoice updates in your accounting system. No re-entry, no version mismatches, no chasing down discrepancies at month end.

Change order propagation

A change order gets approved in Buildertrend. OpenClaw picks it up and updates the budget in QuickBooks, adjusts the draw schedule, sends the updated scope to the lender, and notifies your project manager in Slack. One approval triggers every downstream update across your stack.

Selection tracking across systems

When a client picks their countertop or tile in Buildertrend's portal, OpenClaw auto-generates the purchase order for your supplier and updates the project timeline to reflect lead times. Selections stop being a data entry task and start being a trigger for the purchasing and scheduling workflow.

Daily log and time clock to payroll

Field hours logged in Buildertrend's time clock flow directly to ADP, Gusto, or QuickBooks Payroll. Daily log entries with crew assignments and hours worked get formatted and pushed to your payroll system on the schedule you set. No more exporting CSVs from BT and re-keying hours every pay period.

Lead-to-project handoff

A new lead comes in through Buildertrend. OpenClaw picks up the event and creates a contact in your CRM, triggers an email welcome sequence, adds a follow-up task for your sales rep, and logs the lead source for marketing attribution. The handoff from lead to active project happens across systems without anyone manually copying data.

Warranty and closeout automation

When a project hits completion in Buildertrend, OpenClaw triggers the closeout workflow: generates warranty documents, sends the final invoice through QuickBooks, emails a review request to the homeowner, and archives project files to Google Drive. The transition from active build to warranty period runs itself.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging, accounting, file storage. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

For Buildertrend users, OpenClaw is the bridge between your construction management platform and the accounting, payroll, and client communication tools residential builders depend on. It handles the data transfers, cross-system updates, and multi-tool workflows that Buildertrend can't reach on its own.

Common questions

Does this replace Buildertrend?
No. Buildertrend stays your construction management platform. OpenClaw sits on top as the orchestration layer that connects Buildertrend to your accounting software, payroll system, and other external tools. Your projects, schedules, client portal, and daily logs stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw just makes them talk to everything else.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. Day one covers the Buildertrend API connection, webhook configuration, and external tool authentication. The remaining time goes toward mapping your specific cross-platform workflows (estimates to invoices, time clock to payroll, change orders to accounting) and testing the full chain from Buildertrend event to external action.
Can OpenClaw handle multiple Buildertrend projects at once?
Yes. Multi-project orchestration is one of the most common setups. OpenClaw connects to your Buildertrend account and can read data, trigger actions, and generate reports across all your active projects. Cross-project budget tracking, consolidated scheduling views, and centralized weekly reports are all standard configurations.
Does this work with Buildertrend's client portal?
Yes. When a homeowner approves a change order, makes a selection, or sends a message through the Buildertrend client portal, those events can trigger automations in OpenClaw. Portal activity flows into your accounting, scheduling, and communication tools automatically. The client keeps using the portal they're familiar with; the data just reaches more places.
What accounting systems does this connect to?
OpenClaw connects Buildertrend to QuickBooks (Online and Desktop), Xero, Sage, and FreshBooks. The most common setup by far is Buildertrend-to-QuickBooks, where estimates, change orders, and draw schedules sync automatically for invoicing and expense tracking. We configure the mapping to match your chart of accounts and cost code structure.
