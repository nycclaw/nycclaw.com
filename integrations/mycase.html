MyCase holds everything. You're still doing everything.

The software is good. The problem is that it still needs you for every decision and every update.

Case documents live in five different places

The retainer agreement is in MyCase. The opposing counsel's letter came through email. The client sent photos via the portal. Your paralegal saved notes on a shared drive. When you need to find something fast, you're opening four tabs and digging through folders. MyCase is supposed to be the single source of truth. It's not, because nobody has time to file everything properly.

Clients call because they can't see what's happening

MyCase has a client portal. It's great in theory. But if nobody updates the case status or posts a note, clients see the same thing they saw last week. So they call. Or email. Or both. You spend 15 minutes on a call that could've been a two-line status update in the portal. Multiply that by 30 active cases.

Time entries disappear because nobody logs them

You spent 12 minutes reviewing a document and responding to the client. Then you moved on to the next thing. At the end of the day, you can barely remember what you worked on, let alone how long each thing took. MyCase's time tracking is right there. You just never open it in the moment. That's billable time your firm never recovers.

Deadlines sneak up because nobody's watching the calendar

You entered the filing deadline in MyCase three months ago. It's sitting in the calendar. But between new intakes, court appearances, and client calls, you forgot to check. The reminder pops up the day before, and now you're scrambling. MyCase tracks the dates. It just can't tell you when to start worrying about them.

OpenClaw + MyCase Integration

OpenClaw connects to MyCase through the API, reads what's happening across your cases, and takes action. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Case records stay current without you

OpenClaw reads incoming emails and client portal messages, then updates the relevant case record in MyCase. New document from opposing counsel? Filed to the right case. Client sent an address change? Contact updated. You open MyCase and everything is already where it should be.

Client portal messages get real answers

A client asks "What's the status of my case?" through the MyCase portal at 8 PM. OpenClaw checks the case record, reads recent activity, and sends a clear update. Not a canned response. An actual summary of where things stand. Your client gets an answer in minutes instead of waiting until morning.

Time entries logged from your communications

OpenClaw tracks when you review a document, respond to a client email, or have a portal conversation. It creates time entries in MyCase with the right case, activity type, and duration. No more reconstructing your day at 6 PM. The billable hours are already there.

Deadline alerts before they're urgent

OpenClaw monitors your MyCase calendar and sends alerts at the right time. Filing deadline in two weeks? You get a heads-up now, while there's still time to prepare. Discovery due Friday? OpenClaw flags it Monday morning. It calculates working days backward so nothing catches you off guard.

Invoice follow-ups that actually happen

An invoice hits 30 days overdue. OpenClaw sends a polite follow-up to the client through email or the portal. Still unpaid at 45 days? Another reminder with the balance and a payment link. You set the schedule and tone once. OpenClaw handles the awkward conversations about money so you don't have to.

Task tracking with built-in nudges

OpenClaw reads the task list in MyCase and watches for things falling behind. A paralegal's task is overdue by two days? OpenClaw sends them a reminder. A task depends on something the client hasn't provided? OpenClaw messages the client asking for it. Tasks don't sit in limbo because nobody noticed.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace MyCase?
No. OpenClaw connects to MyCase through its API and operates it for you. Your cases, contacts, documents, and billing all stay in MyCase exactly where they are. OpenClaw just handles the busywork so you can focus on practicing law.
What MyCase features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to MyCase's API for cases, contacts, documents, time entries, invoices, tasks, and calendar events. It can also interact with the client portal for secure messaging. If MyCase exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most MyCase integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The rest is configuring OpenClaw for your practice areas, case types, client communication style, and billing preferences. Within a week, it's handling the routine work on its own.
Will OpenClaw access confidential client information?
OpenClaw runs on your own infrastructure, so your data never leaves your control. It accesses MyCase through the same API your team uses. You decide exactly which cases, contacts, and data OpenClaw can see and act on. Nothing goes to third-party servers.
What if my firm already has processes in MyCase?
OpenClaw works with your existing setup. It doesn't change your case types, task templates, or billing structures. It just automates the repetitive parts: updating records, logging time, sending reminders, following up on invoices. Your processes stay the same. The manual work goes away.
