Lofty's Smart Plans can drip emails and texts on a schedule. That works until a lead replies with a real question. "What's the HOA fee on that listing?" Your Smart Plan doesn't know. So the lead waits for you. And if you're showing houses or in a meeting, they wait a long time.

Lead scores pile up with no one acting on them

Lofty scores your leads based on website behavior, property views, and contact validity. Great. But a lead score of 85 means nothing if nobody calls them within the hour. OpenClaw + Lofty Integration

OpenClaw connects to Lofty through the REST API and acts on your leads in real time. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Instant lead response from any source

Zillow inquiry, Realtor.com lead, IDX registration, Facebook ad form. Doesn't matter where it comes from. OpenClaw picks up the new lead through Lofty's API, reads the source and details, and sends a personalized text or email within seconds. Not a template. Qualifies leads through real conversation

When a lead texts back, OpenClaw keeps the conversation going. It asks about their timeline, budget, pre-approval status, and neighborhood preferences. OpenClaw reads the lead's behavior and conversation context, then enrolls them in the appropriate Lofty Smart Plan. Once a lead is qualified, OpenClaw checks your calendar and suggests times. The lead picks one, gets a confirmation, and you get a notification with everything you need to know: who they are, what they're looking for, budget, pre-approval status, and the full conversation history.

Updates lead records as it learns

OpenClaw adds tags, updates custom fields, and writes notes to lead records in Lofty as conversations happen. When you open a lead's profile, their budget, timeline, neighborhood preferences, and qualification status are already filled in. No data entry on your end.

Acts on IDX browsing behavior

Lofty tracks every property view and saved search on your IDX site. OpenClaw reads that activity and follows up based on what the lead actually looked at. "I noticed you saved three listings near Prospect Park. Want me to set up showings this weekend?" Specific, timely, and relevant.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Lofty?
No. OpenClaw connects to Lofty through the API and operates it for you. Your Lofty account, leads, Smart Plans, and IDX website stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence on top of the system you already pay for. Think of it as giving your CRM a brain, not swapping it out.
What Lofty features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects through Lofty's REST API using OAuth 2.0. It can read and update leads, trigger and manage Smart Plans, track IDX website activity, handle conversations via text and email, update lead scores and tags, and coordinate with integrations like Zillow, Realtor.com, and Dotloop. If Lofty exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Lofty integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The rest of the time is spent configuring the AI for your specific lead sources, qualifying criteria, and follow-up style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which Lofty plan do I need?
Any Lofty plan that includes API access works. You can find your API key under Settings > Integrations > API in your Lofty account. If you're unsure whether your plan includes it, check with Lofty support or ask us during the discovery call. We'll confirm before you commit to anything.
What about Lofty's built-in AI Assistant?
Lofty's AI Assistant handles basic text follow-ups within the platform. OpenClaw goes further: it reads lead behavior across your entire pipeline, makes decisions about which Smart Plan to trigger, updates records based on real conversation context, and coordinates actions across Lofty and your other tools like email and calendar. They can work alongside each other.
