Lofty does a lot. You're still doing the hard part.

The platform handles lead capture and drip campaigns. But the real work happens between those steps.

Smart Plans send messages. They can't read replies.

Lofty's Smart Plans are solid for drip campaigns. They'll send a text on day 1, an email on day 3, another text on day 7. But when a lead replies "I'm looking in the $500K range, what's available in Greenpoint?" the Smart Plan has no idea what to do with that. It just keeps dripping. Someone has to actually read the reply and respond. That someone is usually you, hours later.

Lead routing gets them there. Then what?

Lofty's round-robin and performance-based lead routing is useful for teams. The lead lands in the right agent's queue. But "in the queue" and "actively being worked" are two different things. If that agent is showing a house, on a call, or just having lunch, the lead sits. And sits. Studies show response time is the single biggest factor in lead conversion. Every minute counts.

Your IDX site captures leads. It doesn't convert them.

Someone registers on your Lofty IDX site at 11 PM because they're browsing listings from their couch. They get a registration confirmation email. Maybe a Smart Plan kicks in tomorrow. But right now, while they're actively looking, nobody's engaging them. They save three properties, look at mortgage calculators, and by morning they've registered on two other sites too.

Transaction management is manual data entry in disguise

Lofty has transaction management tools, but moving a deal from "Under Contract" to "Inspection" to "Clear to Close" still requires you to update the record by hand. Add in the notes, update the timeline, notify the right people. It's bookkeeping dressed up as technology. And it falls behind the moment you get busy with the next deal.

OpenClaw + Lofty Integration

It's not running a script. It's having a real conversation with your business logic built in.

Smart Plans triggered by behavior, not timers

Lofty's Smart Plans run on schedules. OpenClaw can switch leads between plans based on what's actually happening. A lead who just viewed 15 listings in one session gets moved to your "hot buyer" plan immediately. A lead who hasn't opened an email in 30 days gets switched to a re-engagement sequence. The right plan at the right time, without you checking in.

Lead routing with actual follow-through

Lofty routes leads to agents. OpenClaw makes sure they actually get worked. If a lead is assigned to an agent and nobody responds within your defined window (say, 10 minutes), OpenClaw steps in and starts the conversation. The agent gets notified with full context when they're available. No lead falls through the cracks because someone was at a showing.

Listing alert follow-up that converts

Lofty sends listing alerts based on saved searches. Most go unopened. OpenClaw monitors which alerts a lead actually clicks on, then follows up on those specific properties. "I noticed you looked at the new listing on 5th Street. It's in your price range and has the garage you mentioned. Want to see it this weekend?" That kind of follow-up is what turns browsers into buyers.

Transaction stages that update themselves

When a deal moves forward, OpenClaw updates the transaction record in Lofty. Offer accepted? Stage moves to "Under Contract" and the buyer gets a congratulations message with next steps. Inspection scheduled? Stage updates, notes get added, and the relevant parties get notified. You stop spending 15 minutes per deal on status updates that could happen automatically.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Lofty?
No. OpenClaw connects to Lofty through its API and operates it for you. Your IDX website, CRM data, Smart Plans, and lead routing rules all stay in place. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence on top, handling the follow-up and decision-making that Lofty's built-in tools can't do on their own.
How is OpenClaw different from Lofty's built-in AI Assistant?
Lofty's AI Assistant handles basic chatbot responses on your IDX website. It can answer simple property questions and capture contact info. OpenClaw goes further: it reads the full CRM record, continues conversations over SMS and email, qualifies leads on your specific criteria, updates pipeline stages, triggers Smart Plans based on behavior, and coordinates across tools outside of Lofty. They're complementary. Lofty's AI captures the lead. OpenClaw works it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Lofty integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The rest of the time is spent configuring OpenClaw for your specific lead routing rules, Smart Plan triggers, qualifying criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which Lofty plan do I need?
You need a Lofty plan that includes API access. The Platform plan and above include it. If you're on a team plan managed by your brokerage, check with your admin to confirm API access is enabled for your account. That's the only requirement on the Lofty side.
Will OpenClaw interfere with my existing Smart Plans?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing Smart Plans and drip campaigns. It can trigger them, pause them, or switch leads between plans based on how conversations are going. But it won't delete or overwrite anything you've already set up. Your current automations keep running. OpenClaw just makes smarter decisions about when to use them.
