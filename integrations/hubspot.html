OpenClaw + HubSpot Integration

OpenClaw connects to HubSpot through the API and webhooks. It reads your CRM data, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Instant response to form submissions

Someone fills out a HubSpot form at 11 PM. OpenClaw picks it up through a webhook within seconds, reads the form fields, and sends a personalized email based on what they submitted. Not a template with merge tags. An actual response that addresses their specific situation.

Deals move through the pipeline automatically

OpenClaw reads deal activity, email replies, meeting outcomes, and ticket status. When a prospect confirms they want a proposal, OpenClaw moves the deal to "Proposal Sent" and updates the deal amount. When they go quiet, it flags the deal and triggers a follow-up. Your pipeline reflects what's actually happening.

Follow-ups that actually happen

OpenClaw monitors deal timelines and contact engagement. If a deal has been in the same stage for too long, or a contact opened your last email but didn't reply, OpenClaw drafts and sends the follow-up. Timed correctly, written in your voice, referencing the last conversation.

Meetings get booked without the back-and-forth

When a lead is qualified, OpenClaw checks your HubSpot meeting link availability and sends it at the right moment in the conversation. It also creates the meeting record in HubSpot with notes on who the contact is and what they need, so you walk into the call prepared.

Contact properties stay clean and current

OpenClaw updates contact and company records as it learns new information. A lead mentions their company size in an email? OpenClaw writes it to the property. They say they're evaluating two other vendors? That goes in the notes. Your CRM data gets better with every interaction, without anyone doing manual entry.

Tickets get triaged and routed

When a support ticket comes in through HubSpot, OpenClaw reads the content, categorizes the issue, sets priority, and routes it to the right team member. For common questions, it drafts a response and assigns it for review. Your ticket queue stops being a pile and starts being organized.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace HubSpot?
No. OpenClaw connects to HubSpot through the API and operates it for you. Your HubSpot account, data, workflows, and integrations stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence on top so your CRM can actually make decisions instead of waiting for you.
What HubSpot features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to HubSpot's full API: contacts, companies, deals, tickets, marketing emails, forms, meetings, workflows, and webhook subscriptions. It can read and write to any standard or custom property, move deals through pipeline stages, create and send emails, and respond to real-time events.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most HubSpot integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhook setup happens on day one. The remaining time goes into configuring how OpenClaw handles your specific deal stages, qualification criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which HubSpot plan do I need?
Any HubSpot plan with API access works, including the free CRM tier. The free plan gives you contacts, deals, and basic API access. Paid plans (Starter and up) add marketing emails, workflows, and more webhook triggers. OpenClaw works with whatever your plan supports.
What if I already have HubSpot workflows set up?
OpenClaw works alongside your existing workflows. It doesn't overwrite or disable anything. Your workflows keep firing as they always have. OpenClaw adds reasoning on top, making smarter decisions about what to do with incoming data that your workflows can't handle on their own.
