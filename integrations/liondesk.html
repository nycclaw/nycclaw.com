OpenClaw + LionDesk Integration

OpenClaw connects to LionDesk through its API and acts on your contacts, campaigns, and tasks. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Instant lead response via the API

A new lead submission hits LionDesk. OpenClaw picks it up through the NewSubmission API, reads the lead source, property interest, and contact details, then sends a personalized first message within seconds. Not a template. A real response based on what the lead actually said.

Qualifies leads through real conversation

When a lead responds to your outreach, OpenClaw keeps the conversation going. It asks about budget, timeline, pre-approval status, and neighborhood preferences. All based on criteria you define. Once they're qualified, it books the next step. If they're not ready, it tags them and enrolls them in the right drip campaign.

Tasks get done, not just created

OpenClaw reads your LionDesk tasks through the Tasks API and actually executes them. Follow-up call due? OpenClaw checks the contact's history, drafts a message based on your last interaction, and sends it. Then it marks the task complete and creates the next one. Your task list shrinks instead of growing.

Smart drip campaign enrollment

OpenClaw uses the Campaigns API to enroll contacts in the right drip sequences based on what it learns during conversations. A buyer mentions they're 6 months out? Long-term nurture campaign. A seller says they want to list next month? Immediate follow-up sequence. The right campaign, assigned by reasoning instead of rigid rules.

Contacts stay organized automatically

OpenClaw updates contact records through the Contacts API as it learns about each lead. Tags get added. Notes get written. Custom fields get filled in. When you open a contact in LionDesk, everything is already there: who they are, what they want, where they are in the process. Zero data entry on your end.

Books showings and callbacks

Once a lead is qualified, OpenClaw checks your availability and proposes times for a showing or a call. It handles the back-and-forth scheduling, confirms the appointment, and adds a task in LionDesk with full context. You show up prepared. The lead feels taken care of.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace LionDesk?
No. OpenClaw connects to LionDesk through its API and operates it for you. Your contacts, drip campaigns, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence so your CRM can act on its own instead of waiting for you to log in and do the work manually.
What LionDesk features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to LionDesk's API endpoints for contacts, campaigns (including drip sequences), tasks, lead submissions, tags, and notes. It can also trigger actions through Zapier webhook integrations for real-time events like new leads and status changes. If LionDesk exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most LionDesk integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring lead qualification criteria, response style, and campaign enrollment logic for your specific market and business. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Do I need a specific LionDesk plan?
LionDesk offers API access on its paid plans. If you're on a team or brokerage plan, you'll have everything you need. If you're on an individual plan, check with LionDesk support to confirm API access is included. That's the only requirement on the LionDesk side.
Will OpenClaw interfere with my existing drip campaigns?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing campaigns and automations. It can enroll contacts in drip sequences, update tags, and manage tasks without disrupting anything you've already set up. Your current workflows keep running as-is. OpenClaw just adds intelligence on top.
