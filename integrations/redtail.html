Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Redtail CRM?
No. OpenClaw connects to Redtail through its REST API and operates it on your behalf. Your Redtail account, contact records, workflows, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence so the CRM does more without you babysitting it.
What Redtail features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Redtail's 100+ API endpoints: contacts, activities, notes, calendar events, opportunities, workflows, tags, categories, and custom fields. If Redtail exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Redtail integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. API authentication and initial configuration happen on day one. The remaining time is spent mapping your workflows, activity categories, and client segmentation rules so OpenClaw operates the way your practice already works.
Do I need a specific Redtail plan?
Redtail CRM includes API access on its standard plan ($39/user/month as of 2025, with a database-level flat rate for up to 15 users). There's no premium tier required. If your firm has API access enabled, you're good to go.
Will this work alongside my existing Redtail workflows?
Yes. OpenClaw doesn't overwrite or disable your existing workflows. It works alongside them, triggering workflows when appropriate and adding intelligence to the steps that currently require manual input. Your existing automations keep running. OpenClaw handles the parts they can't.
