OpenClaw + QuickBooks Integration

OpenClaw connects to QuickBooks through the API and webhooks. It watches your accounts, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Invoices created from project milestones

A project milestone gets marked complete in your project tool or CRM. OpenClaw picks that up, pulls the client info and line items from your records, and creates the invoice in QuickBooks. Sends it to the client. You didn't open QuickBooks once.

Overdue payment follow-ups

OpenClaw watches your receivables. When an invoice goes 7 days past due, it sends a polite reminder. At 30 days, the tone firms up. At 60, it flags the account for your attention with a summary of the history. Consistent follow-up without the awkward conversations.

Automatic expense categorization

New bank transactions come in. OpenClaw reads the vendor name, amount, and description, then categorizes them based on your existing patterns and rules. It learns how you've categorized similar transactions before. Those 47 uncategorized items? Handled before you check in.

Customer data synced with your CRM

New customer in your CRM? OpenClaw creates the matching record in QuickBooks. Client updates their billing address? Synced. Payment received? Your CRM knows about it. No more double-entry across systems, no more mismatched records at month-end.

Cash flow alerts before problems hit

OpenClaw monitors your receivables, payables, and account balances. If your cash position is trending toward a shortfall in the next 30 days, it tells you now, not when the bank account is empty. Includes a breakdown of what's outstanding and what's coming due.

Weekly financial summaries

Every Monday morning, OpenClaw pulls your P&L, outstanding invoices, recent expenses, and cash position from QuickBooks and sends you a plain-English summary. What came in, what went out, what's overdue, and what needs attention. No logging into QuickBooks to check the dashboard.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace QuickBooks?
No. OpenClaw connects to QuickBooks through the API and operates it for you. Your QuickBooks account, chart of accounts, and financial data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw automates the manual work you're doing inside it every day. Think of it as giving your accounting software a capable assistant, not replacing it.
What QuickBooks features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to QuickBooks Online's full API: invoices, payments, customers, estimates, expenses, reports, tax categories, and webhooks for entity changes. If QuickBooks exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, create it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most QuickBooks integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The rest is configuring automation rules for your specific invoicing workflow, expense categories, and payment follow-up preferences. Within a week, it's handling things on its own.
Which QuickBooks plan do I need?
Any QuickBooks Online plan works. Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, or Advanced all include API access. QuickBooks Desktop is not supported since it doesn't have the same cloud API capabilities. If you're not sure which version you're on, we can check during the discovery call.
Will OpenClaw mess up my books?
OpenClaw follows rules you define. It won't create invoices, categorize expenses, or send payment reminders without matching criteria you've approved. Every action is logged, and you can review what it did at any time. If it's unsure about a categorization or an unusual transaction, it flags it for your review instead of guessing. Your accountant will still see clean, accurate books.
