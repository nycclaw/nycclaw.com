Lead follow-up falls through the cracks

A new lead comes in from your website or a storm canvassing campaign. It lands in JobNimbus, but nobody calls them for three hours because the sales rep was on a roof. By then the homeowner called two other companies. JobNimbus stores the lead. It doesn't chase it for you.

Material ordering is manual after every estimate

You finish an estimate in JobNimbus with square footage, material type, and quantities. Then you open your supplier's portal, re-enter the same numbers, and place the order by hand. If the estimate changes, you update JobNimbus and then go back to the supplier portal again. Two systems, zero connection between them.

Insurance supplement tracking lives in three places

The adjuster's report is in your email. The supplement request is in a shared Drive folder. The job status is in JobNimbus. Your office manager is toggling between all three, trying to figure out which claims are pending, which got approved, and which need follow-up. There's no single view of where each claim stands.

Subcontractor coordination happens outside JobNimbus

You schedule a crew for Tuesday's install. The notification goes out via text message, maybe a group chat. If the job gets pushed to Wednesday, you update JobNimbus and then separately text the crew. Your project board says one thing. Your group chat says another. Someone shows up on the wrong day.

What OpenClaw adds on top of JobNimbus

JobNimbus stays your project hub. OpenClaw connects it to everything else and handles the logic between systems.

Lead response automation

A new contact hits JobNimbus. Within 60 seconds, OpenClaw sends them a text confirming you got their info, emails a brief intro with your license number and reviews link, and creates a task for your sales rep with the lead's details. The homeowner hears from you before they finish Googling your competitors.

Estimate-to-material-order pipeline

When a job moves to "Sold" on your board, OpenClaw reads the estimate line items, calculates material quantities based on your templates, and sends the order to your supplier's system (or drafts it for your review). Change the estimate? The order updates automatically. No re-entering numbers in a second portal.

Insurance claim tracking sync

OpenClaw pulls supplement status from your email or insurance portal and updates the corresponding job's custom fields in JobNimbus. Approved, pending, denied. All visible on your board without anyone checking a separate system. When a supplement gets approved, your office manager gets a Slack notification with the amount and next steps.

Subcontractor notifications

A job moves to "Scheduled" in JobNimbus. OpenClaw texts your assigned crew with the address, date, time, and scope of work pulled straight from the job record. If the date changes, OpenClaw sends an updated message automatically. Your board and your crew's phones always match.

Job photo routing to clients and adjusters

Your crew uploads photos to the JobNimbus job record from the roof. OpenClaw picks them up and routes them where they need to go: a progress album shared with the homeowner via email, before/after photos attached to the insurance claim file in Google Drive, and a completion photo set sent to the adjuster. One upload, three destinations.

Cross-project financial reporting

OpenClaw pulls estimate totals, invoice amounts, and payment status from JobNimbus, then cross-references them with your QuickBooks data. You get a weekly report showing revenue by job type, outstanding balances, material costs vs. estimates, and profit margins per project. No spreadsheet assembly required.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging, accounting, file storage. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

For JobNimbus users, OpenClaw is the connective tissue between your project boards and everything outside them. It handles the data transfers between your CRM, your suppliers, your accounting system, and your insurance workflows. The stuff that currently requires someone toggling between five browser tabs.

Common questions

Does this replace JobNimbus?
No. JobNimbus stays your project hub. OpenClaw is the layer that connects JobNimbus to your suppliers, QuickBooks, insurance platforms, and communication tools. Your boards, contacts, estimates, and job records stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw just makes them talk to everything else.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are live within 3 to 5 days. Day one covers the JobNimbus API connection, webhook configuration, and external tool authentication. The remaining time goes toward mapping your specific workflows (board stages to supplier orders, photo routing rules, insurance claim tracking) and testing the full chain.
Does OpenClaw work with JobNimbus's QuickBooks integration?
Yes. JobNimbus has a native QuickBooks sync, and OpenClaw doesn't interfere with it. OpenClaw handles the automation around it: triggering invoice creation based on job stage changes, syncing custom field data that the native integration misses, and building financial reports that pull from both systems. They work side by side.
Can OpenClaw connect to EagleView or Hover?
Yes. When measurement data comes back from EagleView or Hover, OpenClaw can automatically pull those numbers into your estimate template, update material quantities, and notify your sales rep that the report is ready. The measurement data flows into your workflow instead of sitting in a separate tab waiting for someone to check it.
What if we use JobNimbus for more than roofing?
JobNimbus works for general contracting, restoration, siding, gutters, and other trades. OpenClaw connects to the same API regardless of your trade. The workflows we build adapt to your specific job types: insurance restoration claims, commercial roofing projects, residential remodeling jobs. The board stages and triggers change, but the integration approach is the same.
