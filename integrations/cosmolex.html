What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, practice management. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace CosmoLex?
No. OpenClaw connects to CosmoLex through its API and operates it for you. Your matters, contacts, trust accounts, and billing data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw handles the repetitive work (time entry, invoice generation, intake response, trust monitoring) so you don't have to.
What CosmoLex features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to CosmoLex's API for contacts, matters, time entries, billing, trust transactions, and general accounting. It can create time entries, generate invoices, monitor trust balances, update matter records, and manage contact data. If CosmoLex exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can work with it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most CosmoLex integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring OpenClaw for your specific practice areas, billing rates, trust accounting rules, and intake criteria. Within a week, it's handling the admin work on its own.
Will OpenClaw mess up my trust accounts?
OpenClaw monitors trust account balances and flags issues. It doesn't move money without your approval. For IOLTA compliance, it watches for low balances, verifies that deposits match expected retainer amounts, and alerts you before problems happen. You stay in control of every transaction.
Do I need CosmoLex's built-in accounting for this to work?
CosmoLex includes accounting by default, so yes, it's already part of your setup. That's actually one of the advantages. Because everything lives in one system, OpenClaw can connect time entries directly to invoices and trust transactions without juggling data between separate platforms.
