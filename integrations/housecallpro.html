OpenClaw + Housecall Pro Integration

OpenClaw connects through the API and listens to webhooks. It reads what's happening in your account, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Instant lead response

New lead hits Housecall Pro? OpenClaw picks it up through the webhook within seconds. It reads the service type, location, and any notes the customer left, then sends a personalized text or email. Not a template. An actual response that addresses what they asked about.

Estimate follow-up on autopilot

OpenClaw watches for the "estimate sent" webhook. If the customer hasn't responded in 48 hours, it follows up with a friendly message referencing the specific work quoted. Three days later, another check-in. You set the cadence. OpenClaw handles the persistence.

Review requests after every job

When a job is marked complete, OpenClaw sends a review request within the hour. It references the specific service performed and the tech who did the work. Timing matters for reviews. Asking while the customer still remembers your name gets 3x the response rate of asking a week later.

Seasonal maintenance outreach

OpenClaw reads your customer history through the API. It knows who got their furnace serviced last October, who has a water heater over 8 years old, who hasn't had their AC checked before summer. It reaches out to each one with a specific, relevant message. That's recurring revenue you're currently leaving on the table.

Customer conversations that qualify

When a lead texts in asking about pricing, OpenClaw responds like a real person. It asks about the scope of work, the property type, their timeline. By the time you pick up the phone, you already know what they need, when they need it, and whether it's worth sending a tech out.

Customer records stay updated

OpenClaw adds notes and updates customer records in Housecall Pro as it learns from each interaction. Equipment details mentioned in a text, preferred scheduling windows, special access instructions. When your tech pulls up the job, the context is already there. No clipboard. No guessing.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Housecall Pro?
No. OpenClaw sits on top of Housecall Pro and operates it through the API. Your HCP account, jobs, customers, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence to the system you already use. Think of it as giving your field service software a brain, not replacing it with something else.
What Housecall Pro features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Housecall Pro's REST API and webhooks: customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, leads, scheduling, and employee data. It listens for 20+ webhook events including job created, job completed, estimate sent, lead created, lead converted, and customer updated. If HCP exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Housecall Pro integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The rest is configuring the AI for your specific service types, follow-up cadence, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads and follow-ups on its own.
Which Housecall Pro plan do I need?
You need the MAX plan. Housecall Pro's API and webhooks are only available on the MAX tier. If you're currently on the Basic or Essentials plan, you'll need to upgrade before we can connect OpenClaw. The MAX plan also includes features like GPS tracking, advanced reporting, and QuickBooks integration, so the upgrade often makes sense on its own.
Will OpenClaw work with my existing Housecall Pro setup?
Yes. OpenClaw works alongside your existing setup. It doesn't overwrite your notification preferences, automated texts, or scheduling rules. Your current workflows still run. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence on top, handling the follow-ups and decisions that Housecall Pro's built-in automation can't make on its own.
