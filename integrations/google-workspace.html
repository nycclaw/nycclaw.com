What OpenClaw does through Google's APIs

Your Workspace stays the same. OpenClaw adds the automation layer that Google doesn't provide out of the box.

Gmail API: smart email handling

OpenClaw reads incoming emails, classifies them by intent, and drafts contextual replies. Appointment confirmations go out automatically. Common questions get answered from your knowledge base. Emails that actually need your attention get flagged. The rest get handled.

Calendar API: cross-platform scheduling

When a new event lands on your Google Calendar, OpenClaw can pull context from your CRM, attach relevant documents from Drive, and send prep notes to your inbox. Reschedules sync automatically. Meeting notes get created in Docs before the call starts.

Drive API: automated file organization

New client signs up? OpenClaw creates the folder structure in Drive, copies your template documents, sets the right sharing permissions, and notifies your team. Files get named consistently. Nothing ends up in the wrong place. No more "where did that contract go?" conversations.

Sheets API: live reporting dashboards

OpenClaw pulls data from your CRM, invoicing tool, and project management system, then writes it directly into Google Sheets. Your dashboard updates itself. Revenue numbers, pipeline stages, client statuses. Always current, always accurate, zero manual entry.

Cross-tool sync through Workspace

A deal closes in your CRM. OpenClaw sends the welcome email from Gmail, creates the project folder in Drive, adds kickoff meetings to Google Calendar, and logs the deal data in your tracking spreadsheet. Four Workspace actions triggered by one external event. No tab switching required.

Privacy-first architecture

OpenClaw runs on your own devices. Your emails, files, and calendar data never leave your machine. Google's OAuth controls exactly what OpenClaw can access, and you can revoke permissions at any time from your Google admin console. No third-party servers, no data middlemen.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging, accounting, file storage. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

For Google Workspace users, OpenClaw is the automation layer that sits on top of Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Sheets. It connects these tools to each other and to the rest of your tech stack, handling the workflows that Google doesn't automate natively.

Common questions

Does this replace Google Workspace?
No. You keep Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Sheets exactly as they are. OpenClaw sits on top and uses Google's APIs to automate the repetitive work you're currently doing by hand. Your Workspace account, your data, your admin controls all stay the same. OpenClaw just makes them do more on their own.
How is this different from Google Apps Script?
Apps Script works inside Google's ecosystem. OpenClaw works across ecosystems. When a new row appears in a Google Sheet, OpenClaw can create an invoice in QuickBooks, update a deal in your CRM, and send a Slack notification. Apps Script can't reach outside Google's walls without significant custom development. OpenClaw also understands context, so it can read an email and decide what to do with it, rather than just following rigid rules.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are running within 2 to 4 days. Day one covers Google API authentication, permissions scoping, and connecting your external tools. The remaining time goes toward configuring your specific workflows, mapping data between systems, and testing everything end to end.
Does OpenClaw have access to all my emails and files?
Only what you allow. OpenClaw uses Google's OAuth scoping, so you control exactly what it can access. You can limit it to specific Gmail labels, certain Drive folders, or individual spreadsheets. OpenClaw runs on your own devices, so your data never passes through our servers. You can revoke access at any time from your Google admin console.
Can this work across multiple users in my organization?
Yes. With domain-wide delegation, OpenClaw can operate across multiple user accounts in your Google Workspace organization. This is common for teams that need centralized reporting, shared calendar management, or automated file organization across departments. Your Workspace admin controls which users OpenClaw can act on behalf of.
