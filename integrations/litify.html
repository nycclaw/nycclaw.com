OpenClaw + Litify Integration

OpenClaw connects to Litify through the Salesforce API. It reads your matters, processes intakes, updates records, and coordinates between teams. Here's what that looks like.

Intake workflows that actually move

New intake arrives in Litify. OpenClaw picks it up, runs conflict checks against existing contacts, routes it to the right practice group, and notifies the assigning partner with a summary. If any step stalls, OpenClaw flags it and follows up. No more intakes sitting in limbo for days.

Matter records that stay current

OpenClaw reads incoming emails and call notes, then updates the relevant matter fields in Litify. Client changed their address? Updated. Opposing counsel filed a motion? Noted on the matter with the date. Attorneys don't have to touch Salesforce for the data to be accurate.

Team handoffs with full context

When a matter transfers between departments or attorneys, OpenClaw compiles a handoff summary: current status, key dates, recent communications, outstanding tasks, and client preferences. The receiving attorney gets a clear picture without digging through Salesforce records for an hour.

A partner asks "How many PI matters did we close in Q4?" OpenClaw queries Litify through the API and returns the answer in plain language. No Salesforce report builder. No waiting for IT. It pulls from your actual data, including custom objects and fields specific to your org.

Deadline and task tracking

OpenClaw monitors statute of limitations dates, filing deadlines, and task due dates in Litify. When something is approaching, it notifies the responsible attorney. If a task is overdue, it escalates. No more relying on someone to manually check the calendar every morning.

Plaintiff case automation

OpenClaw works with Litify's Salesforce-based platform to automate mass tort intake screening, track case milestones, coordinate with co-counsel, and generate status reports across your entire docket. Whether you're running 50 cases or 5,000, the operational work scales without adding headcount.

Cross-department coordination

Litify tracks what happens in each department. OpenClaw connects the dots between them. When the billing team marks time entries as reviewed, the matter lead gets notified. When a paralegal uploads a document, the attorney gets a summary. Information flows without anyone chasing it down.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Litify?
No. OpenClaw connects to Litify through the Salesforce API and operates it on your behalf. Your Litify instance, custom objects, flows, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw handles the day-to-day operational work so your attorneys don't have to navigate Salesforce to keep the system accurate.
What Litify features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects through Salesforce's REST and SOAP APIs, which means it can access matters, contacts, intakes, documents, time entries, billing records, custom objects, and reports. If it exists in your Litify org, OpenClaw can read it, update it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Litify integrations are running within 5 to 7 days. The Salesforce API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent mapping your custom objects, configuring intake workflows, and training the AI on your firm's specific processes and matter types.
Do we need a Salesforce admin for this?
Not on an ongoing basis. We handle the initial API configuration and connected app setup during onboarding. Once OpenClaw is running, it operates through the API without needing admin intervention. If your org has complex permission sets or custom security, we'll work with your admin during setup.
Will OpenClaw work with our custom Litify objects and flows?
Yes. Litify runs on Salesforce, so every custom object, field, flow, and platform event is accessible through the API. During setup, we map your specific schema so OpenClaw knows how to read and write to your custom matter types, intake fields, and workflow triggers.
Does OpenClaw work with Litify for mass tort cases?
Yes. OpenClaw handles high-volume intake screening, qualifies potential claimants against your case criteria, and routes qualified leads into your Litify pipeline automatically. It works across the full mass tort lifecycle, from initial screening through docket-wide status reporting.
