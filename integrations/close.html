OpenClaw + Close CRM Integration

OpenClaw connects to Close through the REST API and webhooks. It watches what's happening in your account, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Instant inbound response

New lead hits Close? OpenClaw picks it up via webhook within seconds. It reads the lead data, checks the source, and sends a personalized email or SMS through Close's API. Not a template. An actual response based on what the lead submitted and what your reps would say.

Post-call notes and actions

After a call ends in Close, OpenClaw reads the call activity data and duration. It creates a structured note on the lead, updates relevant custom fields, and moves the opportunity to the right pipeline stage. Your reps can hang up and dial the next number. The admin is already done.

Pipeline stays current automatically

OpenClaw updates opportunity stages based on actual activity. Lead responded to an email? Moved to "Engaged." Call lasted over 5 minutes and they asked about pricing? "Qualified." No response in 7 days? "Needs Follow-up." Your pipeline reflects reality, not whatever your rep remembered to update.

Smart email replies, not canned sequences

When a lead replies to an email in Close with a question or objection, OpenClaw reads the thread and responds intelligently. It can pause or adjust sequences based on what the lead actually said. A lead asks about pricing? OpenClaw sends the right information instead of the next generic follow-up in the queue.

Lead data stays clean without effort

OpenClaw updates custom fields, adds tags, and writes structured notes on every lead as it learns new information. When a rep opens a lead record before a call, the context is already there: company size, budget range, decision timeline, previous objections. No digging through email threads.

Re-engages stale leads on schedule

OpenClaw monitors lead activity through the API and identifies leads that have gone quiet. It sends a follow-up email or queues a call task for your reps at the right time. Not a blanket "just checking in" message. A specific follow-up based on the last conversation and where things left off.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Close?
No. OpenClaw connects to Close through its REST API and operates your existing account. Your leads, call recordings, email threads, and pipeline data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence on top so the CRM works harder without more effort from your team.
What Close features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Close's full API: leads, contacts, opportunities, activities (calls, emails, SMS, notes), custom fields, Smart Views, sequences, and webhooks for real-time event triggers. If Close exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Close integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. API keys and webhooks connect on day one. The remaining time goes toward configuring OpenClaw for your specific pipeline stages, qualification criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which Close plan do I need?
Any Close plan includes API access. The Startup plan ($49/mo per user) works fine. Higher-tier plans add features like custom activities, call coaching, and predictive dialer, all of which OpenClaw can also integrate with if you have them.
Will OpenClaw interfere with my existing sequences?
No. OpenClaw works alongside your existing Close sequences and automations. It reads events through webhooks and the API, then takes action based on rules you define. Your sequences keep running as they are. OpenClaw handles the parts that need judgment, like responding to replies and updating pipeline stages.
