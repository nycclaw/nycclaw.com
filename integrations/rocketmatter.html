Rocket Matter is powerful. You're still the weak link.

The software does what it's told. The problem is all the things nobody tells it to do.

Billable time disappears every day

You took a 12-minute call with a client about their case strategy. Then you jumped into the next thing. By end of day, that call is a vague memory. Maybe you log it. Probably you don't. Multiply that by every attorney at your firm, every day, and you're looking at thousands of dollars in lost revenue per month. Rocket Matter can track the time. It can't remind you to enter it.

Invoices go out weeks late

You finished the work three weeks ago. The time entries are (mostly) there. But generating the invoice means reviewing entries, adjusting descriptions, checking rates, and hitting send. It takes 15 minutes per matter, and you have 40 active matters. So invoices sit in draft while your cash flow suffers. Clients don't pay faster when you bill slower.

Trust account balances stress you out

You know the rules. Client funds can't be commingled. Retainers need to be tracked per matter. Balances need regular reconciliation. And yet, checking trust account health is something you do when you remember, which is usually right before an audit or when a client asks for an accounting. One slip and you're facing a bar complaint.

New leads wait while you practice law

Someone fills out the contact form on your website at 2 PM. You're in a deposition. By 5 PM, you check your inbox and see the inquiry. You'll call them tomorrow. Tomorrow becomes Thursday. By Friday, they've hired another firm. Client intake happens outside Rocket Matter entirely, and the delay between "interested" and "contacted" is where firms lose the most new business.

OpenClaw + Rocket Matter Integration

OpenClaw connects to Rocket Matter through the API and handles the billing, invoicing, and intake work that keeps falling through the cracks. Here's what that looks like.

Captures billable time automatically

OpenClaw monitors your emails, calls, and calendar events. When you spend 18 minutes on a call about the Henderson matter, it creates a time entry in Rocket Matter with the correct matter, activity code, and description. You review and approve. No more reconstructing your day at 6 PM from memory.

Generates and sends invoices on schedule

Set your billing cycle (monthly, bi-weekly, on matter completion) and OpenClaw generates invoices in Rocket Matter on time. It reviews unbilled entries, flags anything that looks off, and sends the invoice to the client. No more three-week lag between work done and bill sent.

Follows up on overdue payments

Invoice is 7 days past due? OpenClaw sends a polite reminder. 14 days? A firmer follow-up. 30 days? It flags the matter for your attention with a summary of the balance and payment history. You set the rules. OpenClaw handles the awkward conversations so you can focus on the legal work.

Monitors trust account balances

OpenClaw checks trust balances in Rocket Matter against your thresholds. When a client's retainer drops below $2,000 (or whatever you set), it drafts a replenishment request and alerts you. It also reconciles trust records on a schedule, catching discrepancies before they become problems.

Responds to intake leads instantly

New inquiry comes in through your website, email, or phone service? OpenClaw responds within seconds. It asks qualifying questions (practice area, timeline, budget range), checks for conflicts against your Rocket Matter contacts, and books a consultation on your calendar. The lead gets a response at 10 PM on a Tuesday. You wake up to a qualified appointment.

Tracks matter profitability in real time

OpenClaw pulls time, expenses, and payment data from Rocket Matter and calculates profitability per matter, per attorney, per practice area. It flags matters that are trending over budget or where realization rates are dropping. You get a weekly summary instead of discovering the problem at year-end review.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Rocket Matter?
No. OpenClaw connects to Rocket Matter through its API and operates it for you. Your matters, contacts, invoices, and trust accounts stay in Rocket Matter. OpenClaw just handles the work you keep putting off: logging time, sending invoices, following up on payments, and responding to new leads.
What Rocket Matter features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Rocket Matter's full API: matters, contacts, time entries, invoices, payments, expenses, and trust accounting. It can create time entries, generate invoices, update matter status, and pull financial reports. If Rocket Matter exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can work with it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Rocket Matter integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring billing rules, time capture preferences, invoice schedules, and your firm's specific workflows. Within a week, it's handling the routine billing work on its own.
Will OpenClaw mess up my trust accounting?
OpenClaw monitors trust account balances and flags issues, but it doesn't move money without approval. It can alert you when a retainer drops below a threshold, draft replenishment requests, and reconcile records. The actual transfers stay in your hands. Trust accounting is too important for fully autonomous action.
What if my firm already has billing processes in place?
Good. OpenClaw works alongside what you already have. It fills the gaps: the time entries that don't get logged, the invoices that go out two weeks late, the payment follow-ups that never happen. Your existing processes stay intact. OpenClaw just picks up the pieces that fall through the cracks.
