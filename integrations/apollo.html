OpenClaw + Apollo.io Integration

OpenClaw connects to Apollo through the REST API. It searches contacts, manages sequences, handles replies, and moves deals forward. Here's what that looks like.

Automated prospect sourcing

You define the ICP once: industry, company size, title, location, technologies used. OpenClaw runs the searches through Apollo's People Search API on a schedule you set. It filters out duplicates, checks against your existing contacts, and adds fresh prospects to the right sequence. No more Monday morning list-building sessions.

Sequences that adapt to replies

When a prospect replies to your sequence, OpenClaw reads the message and decides what to do. Interested? It responds, qualifies them, and books a meeting. Objection? It handles it based on your playbook. "Not now"? It pauses the sequence and creates a task to re-engage in the timeframe they mentioned. Every reply gets a real response.

Intent signals trigger action

Apollo tracks email opens, link clicks, and website visits. OpenClaw watches those signals and acts on them. A prospect opens your email three times in an hour? OpenClaw bumps them to the top of your call list and drafts a personalized follow-up. No more checking dashboards for hot leads. The hot leads come to you.

Enrichment that feeds your outreach

OpenClaw uses Apollo's enrichment endpoints to pull company revenue, employee count, tech stack, funding data, and recent news before writing outreach. Your first email references something specific about their business. Not "I noticed your company is growing." Actual details from the enrichment data that make the prospect stop and read.

Deal pipeline stays accurate

OpenClaw updates your Apollo deals based on what's actually happening. Prospect booked a call? Moved to "Meeting Scheduled." No response after two weeks? Moved to "Stale" with a re-engagement task created. You open Apollo and the pipeline reflects reality, not last week's optimism.

Contact records that update themselves

Every conversation OpenClaw has with a prospect gets logged back to their Apollo contact record. Tags, notes, custom fields. When you pick up the phone for a qualified call, you know exactly what was discussed, what the prospect cares about, and where they are in the process. No hunting through email threads.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Your weekly pipeline review takes 10 minutes instead of an hour because the data is already clean.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Apollo.io?
No. OpenClaw sits on top of Apollo.io and operates it through the API. Your Apollo account, contacts, sequences, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence to the system you already use. Think of it as giving your sales stack a brain, not replacing it with a different one.
What Apollo.io features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Apollo's full REST API: people search, company search, contact enrichment, email sequences, the dialer, deals and pipeline management, tasks, and engagement tracking. If Apollo exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Apollo integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection goes live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your specific ICP filters, sequence logic, qualification criteria, and outreach style. Within a week, it's sourcing and engaging prospects on its own.
Which Apollo.io plan do I need?
Any Apollo plan with API access works. The Basic plan includes API access with rate limits. Professional and Organization plans give you higher rate limits and more credits per month, which matters if you're running high-volume outbound. We'll help you figure out which plan fits your volume during the discovery call.
Can OpenClaw work with my existing Apollo sequences?
Yes. OpenClaw works alongside your existing sequences and doesn't overwrite anything. It can add contacts to sequences you've already built, or help create new ones based on criteria you define. Your current setup stays intact. OpenClaw just makes it smarter about who gets added, when, and what happens when they reply.
