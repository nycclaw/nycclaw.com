WhatsApp is powerful. You're the bottleneck.

The Business API gives you message templates, interactive buttons, and catalogs. But without orchestration, you're still doing everything by hand.

Templates go out, but nothing comes back

You send appointment reminders and order confirmations through message templates. When a customer replies, someone on your team has to read it, check another system, and manually respond. The template did its job. The follow-up is where things fall apart because there's no connection between WhatsApp and your operational tools.

Interactive buttons that dead-end

You can add reply buttons and list messages to your WhatsApp flows. A customer taps "Confirm Appointment" or "View Invoice." Then what? Without a system connecting that button tap to your calendar, billing tool, or CRM, the interaction stops at the message layer. Someone still has to process it manually.

Media files disappear into chat history

Customers send photos, receipts, signed documents, and voice messages through WhatsApp all day. Those files sit in the conversation thread. They don't get saved to your cloud storage, attached to client records, or linked to open tasks. When you need that document later, you're scrolling through weeks of chat to find it.

No context from your other systems

When a customer messages you on WhatsApp, the person replying doesn't know their order status, account balance, or last support interaction. That information lives in your CRM, your helpdesk, your accounting software. Every conversation starts from scratch because WhatsApp can't pull data from those systems on its own.

What OpenClaw adds on top of WhatsApp

WhatsApp stays your messaging channel. OpenClaw connects it to everything else and adds the logic the Business API can't handle alone.

Message templates that trigger workflows

OpenClaw manages your pre-approved message templates and sends them based on events in your other systems. A new order in Shopify triggers a shipping confirmation template. An overdue invoice in QuickBooks sends a payment reminder. The templates go out automatically, with dynamic variables pulled from live data. No manual sends, no copy-pasting.

Interactive messages that drive actions

OpenClaw builds reply buttons, list messages, and call-to-action flows based on live data. Customer taps "Reschedule Appointment" and OpenClaw checks your calendar for available slots, presents them as a list message, then books the new time when they pick one. One conversation, three systems updated. The button tap flows through OpenClaw's webhook handler and fans out to every connected tool.

Catalog integration with live inventory

WhatsApp's catalog feature shows products inside the chat. OpenClaw keeps it synced with your actual inventory in Shopify, WooCommerce, or your custom database. Prices update automatically. Out-of-stock items get flagged. When a customer adds items and sends an order, OpenClaw creates the order in your e-commerce system and triggers fulfillment. No duplicate data entry.

Webhook automation across your stack

Every incoming message, button tap, and status update hits OpenClaw's webhook endpoint in real time. OpenClaw parses the payload, identifies the customer in your CRM, checks their context, and routes the action to the right system. A delivery confirmation triggers a review request two days later. A support question creates a ticket in Zendesk. The webhook is the entry point; OpenClaw handles the routing.

Media handling with automatic routing

Customer sends a photo, PDF, or voice note through WhatsApp. OpenClaw downloads the media through the Business API, uploads it to Google Drive or Dropbox, attaches it to the client record in your CRM, and confirms receipt in the chat. Signed contracts, damaged-item photos, insurance documents. They all get routed to the right folder automatically.

Automated replies with real context

When someone messages your WhatsApp number, OpenClaw pulls their history from your CRM, checks recent orders, and looks up open support tickets before responding. The reply includes actual data, not generic scripts. A returning customer gets recognized. A new lead gets routed to sales. Every response is informed by live information from your business systems.

For WhatsApp Business users, OpenClaw turns the API from a messaging pipe into a full operations layer. It connects your WhatsApp number to your CRM, accounting software, file storage, and project management tools, so every message template, button tap, and incoming conversation can trigger real business actions across your entire stack.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace WhatsApp?
No. WhatsApp stays your messaging channel. OpenClaw is the orchestration layer that connects your WhatsApp Business API to external tools like your CRM, Google Drive, QuickBooks, and project management systems. Your conversations, templates, and phone number stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw just makes them talk to everything else.
How is this different from the built-in WhatsApp Business app?
The WhatsApp Business app lets you set up quick replies and a basic product catalog. OpenClaw connects through the Business API, which means programmatic access to message templates, interactive list messages, webhook-driven automation, and real integrations with your CRM and accounting tools. The Business app is manual. OpenClaw makes it automatic.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are live within 3 to 5 days. Day one covers the Business API connection, webhook configuration, and external tool authentication. The remaining time goes toward building message templates, configuring interactive message flows, and testing the full chain from incoming WhatsApp message to business action.
Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account already?
You need access to the WhatsApp Business Platform through a BSP (Business Solution Provider) like Twilio, MessageBird, or Meta's Cloud API directly. If you don't have one set up yet, we can help you choose the right provider and get it configured during the setup process.
Do I need to know how to code to use this?
No. We handle the Business API configuration, webhook setup, message template creation, and workflow mapping. You tell us what should happen when a customer sends a message, taps a button, or browses your catalog. We build the connections and test them. You just use WhatsApp the way you already do.
