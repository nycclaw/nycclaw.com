OpenClaw + Guesty Integration

OpenClaw connects to Guesty through the Open API and webhooks. It monitors your account in real time, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Guest messaging that actually helps

Guesty fires a webhook when a guest sends a message. OpenClaw reads it, checks the reservation details and listing info, and responds with the right answer. Lockbox code? Sent. WiFi password? Sent. "Is there a grocery store nearby?" Answered with the address and walking directions. All within 90 seconds, at any hour.

Turnover tasks on autopilot

When a reservation ends, OpenClaw creates cleaning tasks in Guesty with the right property, time window, and assignee. It checks the next reservation's check-in time and flags tight turnovers. If a cleaner marks a task complete, OpenClaw updates the reservation status and sends the incoming guest their check-in details.

Payment monitoring and follow-up

Guesty's payment webhooks tell OpenClaw when a payment fails, when one is overdue, or when a dispute comes in. OpenClaw reads the reservation, sends a polite follow-up to the guest, and flags the issue for you with full context. No more discovering failed payments a week later when you happen to check.

Calendar gaps get filled

OpenClaw monitors your Guesty calendar via the API. When it spots a two-night gap between bookings, it can adjust minimum stay requirements or flag the gap for a price drop. When a cancellation opens up a prime weekend, OpenClaw alerts you immediately instead of letting it sit unnoticed in a calendar view you check once a day.

New bookings handled instantly

A reservation comes in from Airbnb, VRBO, or a direct booking. Guesty fires the reservation.new webhook. OpenClaw picks it up, reads the guest details, sends a personalized welcome message with check-in instructions, and creates the corresponding cleaning and inspection tasks. All before you even see the notification.

Review management and guest follow-up

After checkout, OpenClaw sends a thank-you message through Guesty and asks about the guest's experience. If a guest mentions a maintenance issue during their stay, OpenClaw logs it as a task so it gets fixed before the next arrival. Consistent follow-up across every property, every guest, every time.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Guesty?
No. OpenClaw connects to Guesty through its API and operates your account for you. Your listings, reservations, channel connections, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds a layer of intelligence on top so you spend less time inside the dashboard.
What Guesty features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Guesty's full Open API: listings, reservations, guest messaging, payments, calendar availability, tasks, and owner statements. It also subscribes to 20+ webhook event types covering new bookings, message received/sent, payment status changes, listing updates, calendar changes, and task updates. If Guesty exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Guesty integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your specific properties, guest communication style, check-in procedures, and operational workflows. Within a week, it's handling guest messages and coordinating turnovers on its own.
Which Guesty plan do I need?
You need a Guesty Pro or Enterprise plan with Open API access enabled. Guesty Lite does not include API access. If you're unsure whether your plan qualifies, we can check during the discovery call. The only requirement on the Guesty side is that the Open API is available and you can generate an access token.
Does OpenClaw work with my existing channel connections?
Yes. OpenClaw operates through Guesty, which already syncs with Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and your other channels. When a reservation comes in from any channel, Guesty receives it and fires a webhook. OpenClaw picks it up from there. Your channel connections stay untouched. Nothing changes on the OTA side.
