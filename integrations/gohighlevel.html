OpenClaw + GoHighLevel Integration

OpenClaw connects to GHL through the API and webhooks. It reads what's happening in your account, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Instant lead response

New contact hits GHL? OpenClaw picks it up within seconds through a webhook. It reads the form data, checks the source, and sends a personalized first message by SMS or email. Not a template. An actual response based on what the lead said.

Real conversations, not canned replies

When a lead texts back with questions, OpenClaw responds like a real person. It qualifies them on your criteria (budget, timeline, location, whatever matters to your business) and keeps the conversation going until the lead is ready to book or you need to step in.

Pipeline moves on its own

OpenClaw updates your GHL pipeline stages based on real qualification data. Lead responded positively? Moved to "Engaged." Booked a call? "Qualified." Went silent for a week? "Needs Follow-up." No more dragging cards around every morning.

Books appointments on your calendar

Once a lead is qualified, OpenClaw checks your GHL calendar for availability and books the appointment. The lead gets a confirmation. You get a notification with full context on who they are, what they need, and how the conversation went.

Contacts stay updated without you

OpenClaw adds tags, writes notes, and updates contact fields in GHL as it learns about each lead. When you open a contact record, everything is already there. Who they are, what they want, where they are in the process. No data entry required.

Triggers workflows with context

GHL workflows fire on simple triggers. OpenClaw fires them based on reasoning. A lead mentions they're comparing three vendors? Trigger the competitive offer workflow. A past client asks about referrals? Trigger the referral reward sequence. Decisions, not rules.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace GoHighLevel?
No. OpenClaw sits on top of GoHighLevel and operates it through the API. Your GHL account, workflows, and data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence to the system you already use. Think of it as giving your CRM a brain, not replacing it with a different one.
What GoHighLevel features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to GHL's full API: contacts, pipelines, opportunities, calendars, conversations (SMS and email), workflows, payments, and 50+ webhook event types. If GHL exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most GoHighLevel integrations are fully running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection and webhooks go live on day one. The rest is configuring the AI for your specific pipeline stages, qualifying criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which GoHighLevel plan do I need?
Any GHL plan with API access works. The Starter plan ($97/mo) includes it. If you're on a white-label or resold version of GHL, check with your provider to make sure API access is turned on. That's the only requirement on the GHL side.
What if I already have GHL workflows set up?
OpenClaw works alongside your existing workflows. It doesn't overwrite or interfere with anything you've already built. Your workflows still fire. OpenClaw just adds a layer of intelligence on top, making smarter decisions about when and how to act on what comes through your pipeline.
