Lead scores go up, but nobody acts on them

Freshsales scores your leads based on engagement and fit. A contact hits 85 points. That should mean something. But the score just sits there until you notice it, open the record, and decide what to do. By then it's been two days and the lead has cooled off.

Deals stall in the pipeline

You've got a clean pipeline with five stages. Half your deals have been sitting in "Proposal Sent" for two weeks. Nobody followed up. Nobody moved them. The pipeline looks full, but nothing's actually progressing. You spend your mornings dragging cards and writing follow-up emails you should have sent last Tuesday.

Contact records are half-empty

You had a great call with a prospect. They mentioned their budget, timeline, and that they're also talking to two competitors. That information lives in your head, not in Freshsales. The contact record has a name, an email, and maybe a phone number. Nothing useful for the next touchpoint.

Follow-ups fall through the cracks

Freshsales can remind you to follow up. It can even queue a sales sequence. But when a lead replies with something unexpected, the sequence breaks. Someone asks a pricing question or wants to reschedule. Now it's manual again, and you've got 30 other leads to get to first.

OpenClaw + Freshsales Integration

OpenClaw connects to Freshsales through its REST API. It reads your contacts, deals, and tasks, makes decisions, and takes action. Here's what that looks like.

Acts on lead scores instantly

When a contact's Freddy AI lead score crosses your threshold, OpenClaw picks it up and takes action. It reads the contact record, checks their activity history, and sends a personalized follow-up email within seconds. High-intent leads get attention the moment they're hot, not two days later.

Qualifies leads through real conversation

When a new contact comes in, OpenClaw starts a conversation over email. It asks about budget, timeline, and specific needs based on your qualifying criteria. Not a canned sequence. An actual back-and-forth that adapts to what the lead says and updates the Freshsales record as it learns.

Moves deals through the pipeline

OpenClaw updates deal stages based on what's actually happening. Lead replied positively? Moved to "Engaged." Proposal sent and they asked a follow-up question? "Negotiation." Gone silent for a week? "Needs Follow-up" with a task created for you. No more dragging cards around every morning.

Creates tasks and books appointments

OpenClaw uses Freshsales' tasks and appointments APIs to schedule follow-ups, book meetings, and create reminders. When a lead is ready for a call, it checks availability and books the appointment directly in Freshsales. You get a notification with full context on who they are and what they need.

Keeps contact records complete

OpenClaw writes notes, updates custom fields, and fills in account details as it learns about each lead. Budget mentioned in an email? Added to the deal record. Competitor name dropped? Tagged and noted. When you open a contact in Freshsales, everything is already there. Zero data entry from you.

Manages accounts and bulk operations

Freshsales' API supports bulk upsert for contacts, accounts, and deals. OpenClaw uses this to keep your CRM in sync with other data sources, reassign ownership in bulk, or clean up stale records. It also links contacts to the right accounts automatically based on email domain and conversation context.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

It's not a chatbot you paste into a website. It's an operator that works behind the scenes, handling the tasks you keep putting off because there aren't enough hours in the day.

Common questions

Does OpenClaw replace Freshsales?
No. OpenClaw connects to Freshsales through its REST API and operates it for you. Your Freshsales account, data, and deal pipeline stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw adds intelligence on top of the CRM you already use. Think of it as giving your sales CRM a brain, not swapping it for a different one.
What Freshsales features can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw connects to Freshsales' full API: contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, appointments, notes, emails, marketing lists, and custom fields. It can also read Freddy AI lead scores and activity timelines. If Freshsales exposes it through the API, OpenClaw can read it, write to it, and act on it.
How long does the integration setup take?
Most Freshsales integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. The API connection happens on day one. The remaining time is spent configuring the AI for your specific deal stages, qualifying criteria, and communication style. Within a week, it's handling leads on its own.
Which Freshsales plan do I need?
Any Freshsales plan with API access works. The Growth plan ($9/user/mo) and above include API keys. If you're on the free tier, you'll need to upgrade to get API access. That's the only requirement on the Freshsales side.
Can OpenClaw work with my existing Freshsales workflows and sequences?
Yes. OpenClaw works alongside your existing sales sequences and workflow automations. It doesn't overwrite anything you've already built. Your sequences still run. OpenClaw adds a layer of reasoning on top, making smarter decisions about when and how to act on what comes through your pipeline.
