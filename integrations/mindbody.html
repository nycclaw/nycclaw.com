Mindbody does everything inside Mindbody. That's the problem.

Your studio doesn't run on Mindbody alone. But your data is trapped there.

Sales and accounting live in separate worlds

Every class pack, membership sale, and retail transaction goes through Mindbody's POS. But your accountant uses QuickBooks or Xero. So someone on your team exports CSVs, reformats them, and enters the numbers manually. Every week. It's tedious, error-prone, and completely avoidable.

Marketing tools can't see what happens in class

Mindbody has its own email and marketing features, but they're limited. If you use Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or ActiveCampaign for your real email marketing, those tools have no idea who attended class last Tuesday, whose membership is about to expire, or who hasn't visited in 30 days. You're building campaigns blind.

Staff scheduling doesn't leave the platform

Your instructors' schedules live in Mindbody. Their personal calendars live in Google Calendar or Outlook. There's no native sync between them. Instructors double-book themselves. You find out when they don't show up. The workaround is usually a shared spreadsheet, which is its own kind of problem.

Multi-location reporting is a manual project

If you run two or more locations, you know the drill. Log into each site, pull the reports, paste them into a spreadsheet, and try to make the numbers comparable. Mindbody doesn't give you a consolidated view of revenue, attendance, or membership trends across locations. You build that yourself, every time.

What OpenClaw adds on top of Mindbody

Mindbody stays your studio platform. OpenClaw connects it to everything else and handles the logic Mindbody can't.

Sync Mindbody sales to QuickBooks

Every POS transaction in Mindbody (class packs, memberships, retail items) gets categorized and synced to QuickBooks or Xero automatically. Revenue, tax, discounts, and refunds all map to the right accounts. Your bookkeeper stops chasing CSVs and starts working with clean, current numbers.

Automate post-class follow-ups externally

A client attends their first class. OpenClaw picks up the visit from Mindbody's API and triggers a welcome sequence in Mailchimp, a personalized SMS through Twilio, or a task for your front desk in Asana. Mindbody's built-in emails are generic. Your external tools can be specific.

Sync client data to your external CRM

Mindbody stores client profiles, visit history, and purchase records. OpenClaw syncs that data to HubSpot, Salesforce, or whatever CRM you use for broader relationship management. New clients in Mindbody appear in your CRM. Membership changes update automatically. You get a full picture without maintaining two databases by hand.

Staff schedules in Google Calendar

OpenClaw reads instructor assignments from Mindbody's staff and class schedule endpoints and pushes them to Google Calendar or Outlook. When a class gets rescheduled or an instructor swap happens in Mindbody, the external calendar updates within minutes. No more "I didn't see the change" from your instructors.

Membership churn alerts to Slack

OpenClaw monitors Mindbody's membership and contract data for warning signs: a client hasn't visited in three weeks, a membership is set to expire, a contract cancellation just came through. Instead of discovering churn after it happens, your team gets a Slack message with the client's name, membership details, and visit history so they can reach out before it's too late.

Retail inventory sync

Selling retail through Mindbody's POS? OpenClaw tracks inventory levels and syncs them with your supplier orders, your Shopify store, or a Google Sheet your manager reviews weekly. When stock drops below a threshold, OpenClaw can notify your team or generate a reorder list automatically. No more surprise stockouts on your best-selling items.

What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI assistant that connects to the tools you already use and operates them for you. Email, calendar, CRM, messaging, accounting, file storage. It reads, it responds, it takes action. It runs on your own devices, so your data stays yours.

For Mindbody users, OpenClaw is the connector that the platform doesn't have. It reads from Mindbody's Public API (classes, clients, sales, memberships, staff, locations) and routes that data to your accounting software, your marketing tools, your team chat, and your custom reports. Mindbody stays your booking engine; OpenClaw makes sure the rest of your business knows what's happening inside it.

Common questions

Does this replace Mindbody?
No. Mindbody stays your booking and membership platform. OpenClaw is the layer that connects Mindbody to external tools like QuickBooks, Slack, Mailchimp, and Google Calendar. Your class schedules, client profiles, and POS data stay exactly where they are. OpenClaw just makes them available to the rest of your tech stack.
How is this different from Mindbody's built-in marketing tools?
Mindbody's built-in marketing (automated emails, campaigns) only works with data inside Mindbody and only sends through Mindbody's own channels. OpenClaw lets you route Mindbody data to any external tool. Want class attendance data to trigger a campaign in Mailchimp or Klaviyo? Want membership cancellations to alert your team in Slack? That's what OpenClaw handles. The two work alongside each other.
How long does setup take?
Most integrations are running within 3 to 5 days. Day one covers the Mindbody API connection and webhook configuration. The remaining time goes toward mapping your specific workflows (which sales data goes to QuickBooks, which class events trigger follow-ups, how membership data syncs to your CRM) and testing end to end.
Can OpenClaw work across multiple Mindbody locations?
Yes. Mindbody's API supports multi-site access, and OpenClaw can read data, trigger actions, and generate consolidated reports across all your locations. Cross-location revenue reporting, centralized client profiles, and multi-site class utilization tracking are all standard configurations.
What Mindbody data can OpenClaw access?
OpenClaw reads from Mindbody's Public API, which covers class schedules, client profiles, appointments, staff records, locations, sales transactions, memberships, contracts, and visit history. If the data exists in Mindbody and the API exposes it, OpenClaw can route it to your external tools.
